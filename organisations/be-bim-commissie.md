# BIM-commissie

> **Created 2026-09-06**, closing a gap [[BE-WIV-1998]] flagged
> explicitly: "Belgium appears with an organic act and an oversight act
> but no authorisation regime." Both the founding act's own text and the
> commission's own Rules of Procedure were read directly.

## Description

Confirmed by reading etaamb.openjustice.be's own text of [[BE-WET-BIM-2010]]
directly: the commission oversees the "specifieke en uitzonderlijke
methoden" (specific and exceptional methods) that Article 18/2 of
[[BE-WIV-1998]] permits. Its composition: **three permanent members and
three alternates, all magistrates** — one from the public prosecutor's
office and two judges, one of whom is an investigating magistrate who
chairs the commission. Members must be at least 40 years old, have five
years' relevant experience, and hold a "very secret" security clearance.

## A binding gate, not an opinion

The head of a service may authorise an exceptional method only "na
eensluidend advies van de commissie" — after the commission's
**conforming (unanimous) opinion**. Confirmed by reading the Act's own
text directly, this is a **binding pre-authorisation requirement**, not
an advisory opinion the executive can override — the same shape as
[[NL-TIB]]'s decision, and a sharper counterweight than [[FR-CNCTR]]'s
opinion to the Prime Minister. The commission also monitors methods once
in use and can suspend those found unlawful.

## Belgium's third instrument, and its third body

[[BE-WIV-1998]] (1998) constitutes the services; [[BE-TOEZICHTSWET-1991]]
(1991) constitutes the parliamentary overseer, [[BE-COMITE-I]]; and now
[[BE-WET-BIM-2010]] (2010) constitutes the authorisation gate, this
commission. Three statutes, three distinct functions, spanning twelve
years — a finer-grained separation than the single-instrument countries
in this Atlas (e.g. [[NL-WIV-2017]], which fuses all three).

## Not modelled

- The **Royal Decree of 12 October 2010**, a complementary implementing
  instrument named in the commission's own Rules of Procedure.
- The commission's **relationship to [[BE-COMITE-I]]** — both are
  oversight bodies under different statutes, and no source read this
  pass states how the ex-ante authorisation function and the
  parliamentary review function interact procedurally.

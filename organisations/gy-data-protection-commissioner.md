# Data Protection Commissioner (Guyana)

> **Created 2026-11-17** (scan issue #770: laws with no body). Sourced from Part VII (sections 72
> and 73) of the published Bill of [[GY-DPA]], read directly. `status: unknown`: no appointment
> and no office page were read.

## What the Act gives it

- **Section 72**: a body corporate responsible for administering the Act; the President appoints the
  Commissioner, a person "of eminence in public life", and may remove them only for insolvency,
  conviction, illness or a conflicting interest, after a hearing.
- **Section 73**: monitor and enforce the Act; awareness for the public, controllers, processors and
  children; audits; information to data subjects; monitoring reports to the Minister; examining
  proposed legislation and Government policy that may affect privacy.

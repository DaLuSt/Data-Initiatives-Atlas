## Description

Austria's national statistical institute - the **sixteenth** on
[[EU-ESS]]. Its legal name, **Bundesanstalt Statistik Österreich**, is
confirmed on the institute's own Impressum page (the ESS overview page
describes the partnership generally but does not enumerate members, so the
"sixteenth" count is the Atlas's own tally rather than a sourced claim).

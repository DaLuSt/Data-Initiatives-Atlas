# TIC TIMOR, IP

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #470, Timor-Leste). One news page on the agency's own site and its Timor Digital site were read directly; no English legal name is given, so none is asserted.

## Description

TIC TIMOR, IP (Portuguese: *Agência de Tecnologia da Informação e Comunicação, IP*; Tetum: *Ajénsia TIC TIMOR*) is the government agency that, according to its page of 20 March 2025, oversees and implements the ICT and Electronic Government programmes assigned to the Ministry of Transport and Communications under the IX Constitutional Government. Its stated mission is to "manage the Government's network infrastructure, centralizing Government data". The Tetum text of the page says it is a public institute with administrative, financial, budgetary and patrimonial autonomy under Decree-Law No. 29/2017, and cites Decree-Law No. 46/2023 of 28 July as its organic framework.

The same page names a government digital data centre and application development among its pillars, and says Timor-Leste still has to formalise "three key areas", listing among four items the Data Protection and Privacy Law and the Cybercrime Legislation. The `timordigital.gov.tl` site is "Led & Developed by TIC TIMOR I.P."; it is titled "Timor Digital 2023" and states no period, approval date or goals, so no strategy entity is created.

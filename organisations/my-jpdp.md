# Jabatan Perlindungan Data Peribadi (Malaysia)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #483, Malaysia). Read on the Department's home page in Malay. No founding date is recorded; the page distinguishes the Commissioner's office from the Department.

## What the page shows

Services: registration of data controllers (and a list of the classes of controller that must register), certificate renewal, complaints, data breach notification ("Lapor DBN"). Guidance: the data subject's rights, the seven personal data protection principles, standards, guidance for organisations and data protection officers. Announcements in October 2026: a public consultation paper no. 1/2026 on an AI and personal data protection framework (5 October), the 2025 annual report (5 October) and new ICT equipment for the Department's new office (15 October). The home page also carries a compliance notice for data controller registration.

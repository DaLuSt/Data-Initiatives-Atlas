## Description

ICTU is a government foundation (overheidsstichting) established on
11 April 2001 by [[NL-BZK]] and [[NL-VNG]] to support public bodies in their
use of ICT. It operates as an independent advice and project organisation
within government, working on a not-for-profit basis and converting
government policy into concrete projects, services and products. All tiers of
Dutch government — central government, provinces, municipalities, executive
organisations and water authorities — can commission work from ICTU.

Within the Atlas's scope, ICTU's most significant role is as the manager
(beheerder) of [[NL-NORA]], with BZK as contracting authority. The NORA
gebruikersraad meets at ICTU.

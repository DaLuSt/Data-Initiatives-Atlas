# Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC), Republic of Korea

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #474, Republic of Korea). The English home page was read directly on a runner; it is mostly a menu and news. The Personal Information Protection Act and the Commission's founding date are not on that page and are not asserted.

## Description

Korea's privacy regulator. Its English site lists law and guidelines, a national policy section (requests for access to personal information, dispute mediation, reporting infringements), business policy (pseudonymisation and combination of pseudonymised information, the ISMS-P certification), public-sector policy (a master plan, privacy impact assessments, assessment of public institutions' management level), global policy and cooperation among international privacy authorities, an annual report, resolutions and decisions, a Dispute Mediation Committee, and its chairperson, vice chairperson and commissioners. A section is devoted to the **EU and UK adequacy decisions**, with hotlines for EU Member State and UK supervisory authorities. Headlines on the page include "The PIPC Initiates Equivalence Recognition Review of the United Kingdom" (8 October 2026) and the 2026 Global AI Privacy Forum; neither article was read.

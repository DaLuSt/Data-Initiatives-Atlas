## Description

Estonia's data protection authority, and its member of [[EU-EDPB]].

## The twelfth national DPA in the Atlas

It joins [[NL-AP]], [[DE-BFDI]], [[BE-APD]], [[FR-CNIL]], [[ES-AEPD]],
[[PL-UODO]], [[GB-ICO]], [[IE-DPC]], [[NO-DATATILSYNET]], [[PT-CNPD]],
[[LU-CNPD]] and [[CZ-UOOU]] on the Board.

Every one of them descends from a requirement first written not in the GDPR
but in [[INTL-CONVENTION-108-PROTOCOL]] of 2001, which obliged parties to
have supervisory authorities exercising their functions **in complete
independence**. That descent is recorded in prose on the protocol and
asserted nowhere, because no source read states it.

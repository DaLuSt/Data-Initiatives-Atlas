# AGETIC (Bolivia)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #474 group, Bolivia). Read on AGETIC's own site (agetic.gob.bo). The agency's founding decree is not on the pages read, so no start date is recorded.

## What the site says

Its mission: "Liderar y consolidar el desarrollo e implementación de políticas, programas, proyectos y servicios de Gobierno Electrónico y Tecnologías de la Información y Comunicación, para alcanzar la soberanía tecnológica y la transformación digital del Estado". Its objectives page says it works on free software, promotes open data and releases designs, content and code. The home page, in September 2026, shows its public services (Ciudadanía Digital, digital signature, a state interoperability platform launched 1 December 2025, the e-government implementation plan PIGE updated for 2025-2028) and a note that services run by ADSIB pass to AGETIC.

## Duties from the two decrees read

- [[BO-DS-5340]]: hosts and administers the gob.bo platform and issues its technical guidelines.
- [[BO-AGENDA-DIGITAL-2030]]: monitors and evaluates the Agenda (the single additional provision of DS 5367).

## Not recorded (leads only)

Search summaries say a Supreme Decree of 2015 (reported as No. 2514) created AGETIC and that DS 3251 of 2017 approved the e-government plan. Neither decree was read (`discovery/unresolved.md`).

# Union for the Mediterranean (UfM)

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #542, Southern Europe's regional bodies). Sourced from the Secretariat's own pages and documents, read directly. **The list of Member States was not found on a page that could be read**, so no edge to a member country is recorded; only the EU, as co-president.

## Description

"An intergovernmental organisation that brings together 43 countries to strengthen regional cooperation and dialogue through specific projects and initiatives" (the Secretariat's "who we are" page). It was launched on 13 July 2008 at the Paris Summit "as a continuation of the Euro-Mediterranean Partnership (Euro-Med), also known as the Barcelona Process, established in 1995" (Rules of Procedure of the DEWoG, read directly). The EU and Jordan are co-presidents (press release of 30 September 2014; the 2020 "How the UfM works" sheet also says "Jordan and the EU are Co-Presidents"). The Secretariat is based in Barcelona. Syria suspended its membership on 1 December 2011 and Libya has observer status (the same sheet).

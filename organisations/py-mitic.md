# Ministerio de Tecnologías de la Información y Comunicación (MITIC), Paraguay

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #473, Paraguay). Its home page was read directly on a runner; it is mostly a menu.

## Description

Paraguay's ministry for ICT, with a deputy ministry for information and communication technologies and one for communication. Its menu lists **Gobierno Electrónico**, innovation and the digital economy, infrastructure and connectivity (free Wi-Fi, a government cloud, "Nube PY"), digital inclusion, **Ciberseguridad y Protección de la información**, ICT guidelines, a Digital Agenda under Transformación Digital, and a legal-framework page.

## Data protection, not recorded

Paraguay has no general personal-data law in force on any source seen: Ley 6534/2020 covers credit data only (secondary sources), and a general bill passed the Chamber of Deputies and was returned to the Senate in October 2025 (press reports, not read). The ministry is reported as the likely home of a future supervisory agency; none of that is on its home page.

# Interparliamentary Assembly of the CIS Member Nations (IPA CIS)

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #543, Eastern Europe's regional bodies). Sourced from the Assembly's own site, which the agent cannot reach and a GitHub runner can; two pages read there. The Commonwealth of Independent States itself is not modelled: its site was not read.

## Description

"An interstate body of the Commonwealth of Independent States", composed of "the parliamentary delegations of the Member Nations". Its member parliaments, as the site lists them: Azerbaijan, Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, the Kyrgyz Republic, the Russian Federation, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan. Kazakhstan and Tajikistan are also members but have no country anchor in the Atlas yet, so they have no edge. Ten permanent commissions draft model laws, which are "submitted to the national parliaments for their use in the law-making process"; they "are recommendatory and serve as guidelines in the development and approximation of the Commonwealth legislation". The Mandate page says the Assembly has adopted "about 600 model laws and other documents" and that "more than 70% of all provisions are implemented in the legislation of the CIS countries".

## In the Atlas

Its model law-making in information and communication technology: [[INTL-IPA-CIS-INFO-CODE-2012]]. The 2017 article also says a revised Model Law on Personal Data and one on promoting a digital society were being drafted; their texts and current status were not found (`discovery/unresolved.md`).

# Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (COMESA)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #550, Eastern Africa's regional bodies). From COMESA's own overview and EGEE-ICT pages, read directly. ## What is sourced

"The history of COMESA began in December 1994 when it was formed to replace the former Preferential Trade Area which had existed from the earlier days of 1981." Established by its Treaty "as an organisation of free independent sovereign states"; "21 Member States, population of over 640 million". Its strategy is "economic prosperity through regional integration".

# ANTIC (Cameroon)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #483, Cameroon). Read on the Agency's own site (in French). **No `start_date` is recorded**: the founding decree is dated 8 April 2002 (below), but when the Agency began to operate is not on the pages read (its first board was appointed in 2006).

## Key dates, from the Agency's presentation page

- **8 April 2002:** decree No. 2002/092 creates the Agency, with the global mission of promoting and following the public authorities' action in ICT, under the technical supervision of the Presidency.
- **21 December 2010:** law No. 2010/012 on cybersecurity and cybercrime (electronic-security regulation: certification, security audit, security watch) and law No. 2010/013 on electronic communications (promotion and follow-up of ICT policy and regulation of the Internet through domain names, `.cm`, and IP addresses at national level).
- **10 April 2012:** decree No. 2012/180 organises the Agency (statutory missions, per the missions page); later reorganised by decree No. 2019/150 of 20 March 2019 (listed on its texts page, not read).
- Governance: a board with a chairman and 11 members; a Director General and a Deputy.

## Missions (missions page)

Promotion and follow-up of public action in ICT (national ICT strategy, common needs of public services, technical standards and interoperability, State websites, training, international technical cooperation, `.cm` registration, dispute mechanisms) and regulation, control and follow-up of information-systems and network security and electronic certification, with the telecommunications regulator.

## What the page says

"Sciensano is the Belgian scientific institute for human, animal and environmental health. We conduct scientific research, carry out surveillance and support the government and several agencies and organisations by providing them with expertise, advice and services in the field of health." Its laboratories assess risks "related to food, the environment and infectious agents", control "the quality of health products and services" and "assess Belgium's health status and healthcare". It is a member of the Pasteur Network and of IANPHI, and recognised as a Federal Scientific Institution under the Royal Decree of 30 October 1996.

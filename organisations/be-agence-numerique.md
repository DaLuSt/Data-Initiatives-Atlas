## Description

The Agence du Numérique (AdN) is Wallonia's digital-transformation
agency, a **société anonyme de droit public** contributing to the
definition, implementation and monitoring of Walloon digital policy.

## Succeeded the Agence wallonne des Télécommunications

Reading `wallex.wallonie.be`'s own consolidated text of the **décret du
28 novembre 2013** directly: its **Article 25** provides that, with
effect from **1 January 2015**, all rights and obligations of the
**Agence wallonne des Télécommunications (AWT)** — subsidies, public
contributions, intellectual property, information assets and staff —
transfer by operation of law to the Agence du Numérique. The decree
itself creates the broader **Agence pour l'Entreprise et l'Innovation**
regime under which the AdN operates. Since **2016** it has driven the
region's **Digital Wallonia** strategy.

## Reformed in 2026

The AdN's own "Missions" page, read directly, describes six statutory
missions: assisting the Walloon government's digital-strategy
programmes, coordinating public/private/associative digital actors,
monitoring technological and regulatory developments, issuing
non-binding policy recommendations, **structuring, standardising and
disseminating data**, and executing delegated missions. Secondary
reporting (not independently fetched this pass) describes a decree
adopted by the Walloon Parliament on **25 February 2026** substantially
reforming the Agency's governance, with reinforced multi-year funding
(2025-2029) of €9.794 million annually.

## Not modelled

- The **Agence wallonne des Télécommunications (AWT)**, the AdN's
  predecessor, fully absorbed in 2015. Named here in prose only.
- The **Agence pour l'Entreprise et l'Innovation**, the broader legal
  regime the 2013 decree establishes, of which the AdN is one part.

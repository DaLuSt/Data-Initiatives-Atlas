# Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #549). **Known only through two documents**: the 2010 Supplementary Act on personal data protection and ECOWAS's release of 22 November 2024, read directly. The ECOWAS Treaty (1975, revised 1993), the founding date and the member list were not read, so there are no member edges.

## What is sourced

The 2010 Act names the Authority of Heads of State and Government, the Council of Ministers, the ECOWAS Parliament and the Commission, and cites Articles 7 to 9, 27, 32, 33 and 57 of the revised Treaty. The 2024 release names the ECOWAS Commissioner for Infrastructure, Energy and Digitalisation and the Directorate of Digital Economy and Post, and the ministers responsible for telecommunications, ICT and digitalisation.

# National Privacy Commission

> **Verified 2026-10-09.** Created and described by the Act's own text, read directly. The Commission's own site (privacy.gov.ph) answers 403 to the agent and to a GitHub-hosted runner and is not cited.

## Description

Section 9 of [[PH-DPA]] attaches the Commission to the Department of Information and Communications Technology and provides for a Privacy Commissioner and two Deputy Privacy Commissioners appointed by the President for a term; section 42 attaches it to the Office of the President if that Department does not yet exist.

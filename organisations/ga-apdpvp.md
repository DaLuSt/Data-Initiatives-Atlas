# Autorité pour la Protection des Données Personnelles et de la Vie Privée (APDPVP), Gabon

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #472, Gabon). Known from the law that creates it; whether members have been appointed and the Authority is operating was not established, and no site of its own was found.

## Description

Created by Article 7 of [[GA-LOI-025-2023]] to see to the protection of personal data and privacy in Gabon; the law's definitions give it the prior-formalities, formal-notice and indirect-access roles of a data-protection authority. Secondary sources describe a bill replacing the earlier Commission nationale pour la protection des données à caractère personnel (CNPDCP) with this body; that history was not read.

# Provedor de Direitos Humanos e Justiça (PDHJ)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #470, Timor-Leste). Two pages on the institution's own site were read directly. Its founding instrument and date are not stated on those pages and are not asserted.

## Description

The PDHJ is Timor-Leste's ombudsman for human rights and good governance; its site writes the name in English as both "Ombudsman for Human Rights and Justice" and "Provedor for Human Rights and Justice". Its stated objective is that "fundamental rights and freedoms are protected and good governance is promoted", and people can file a complaint with it over a decision or action they believe violated their human rights.

## Data protection in Timor-Leste

On 15 January 2026 the PDHJ called for "a comprehensive Data Protection Law and a balanced Cybercrime Law" and for an independent Data Protection Commission with power to audit and sanction public and private entities. It says that a 2025 Petition for Omission it brought on the missing data-protection law was followed by a binding decision of the Court of Appeal, which it describes as turning the task into "an immediate constitutional obligation". That account is the PDHJ's own and is recorded as such; the Court's decision was not read. [[TL-TIC]]'s page of 20 March 2025 lists the same law among those still to be formalised, which is why the Atlas records **no data-protection act** for Timor-Leste: none is in force, on every source read.

# Eurasian Economic Commission (EEC)

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #543). Sourced from the Union's home page and the Commission's own news items, read directly.

## Description

"A permanent supranational regulatory body of the Union", whose "core tasks ... are fostering the conditions to support the operation and development of the Union, and drafting proposals in the field of economic integration within the Union." The Commission's Board approved the rules on mutual recognition of electronic digital signature in B2G interactions on 22 August 2023 ([[INTL-EEC-EDS-B2G-2023]]); the Commission's Minister in charge of Internal Markets, Informatization, Information and Communication Technologies is named in that item (the Atlas does not record people). Kazakhstan, also a Member State, has no country anchor in the Atlas yet, so it has no edge.

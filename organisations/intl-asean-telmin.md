# ASEAN Telecommunications and IT Ministers Meeting (TELMIN)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #557). Known **only** from the two frameworks it adopted, read directly. Its terms of reference, the member list of its working groups and its meeting records were not read.

## What is sourced

The 2016 Framework was "ADOPTED AT Bandar Seri Begawan, Brunei Darussalam, ... twenty fifth day of November ... two thousand and sixteen", at what the Framework calls the ASEAN Telecommunications and IT Ministers Meeting; the ASEAN ICT Masterplan 2020 was adopted at the 15th TELMIN on 27 November 2015 in Da Nang. The Digital Data Governance Framework carries the same heading.

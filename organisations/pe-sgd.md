# Secretaría de Gobierno Digital (Peru)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #478, Peru). Known from Legislative Decree 1412 as signed in 2018. The unit may since have been renamed or reorganised (the PCM's current organisation was not read), so the name is the decree's and no founding date is recorded.

## What the decree gives it (arts. 8 and 9)

As governing body for digital government: programme, direct, coordinate, supervise and evaluate its application; draft and propose rules; issue guidelines, procedures, methodologies, models, directives and other standards; supervise compliance where relevant. Public bodies adopt its standards for exchanging data and information.

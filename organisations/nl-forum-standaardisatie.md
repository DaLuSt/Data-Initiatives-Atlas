## Description

Forum Standaardisatie advises the Dutch public sector on the use of open
standards. It was established in 2006 by cabinet decision, together with the
College Standaardisatie, through an installation decree. It functions as an
independent advisory body composed of experts drawn from government, the
private sector and academia.

Its central instrument is the *lijst open standaarden* (list of open
standards), which is split between standards carrying a 'pas toe of leg uit'
(apply-or-explain) obligation and a list of recommended standards. Standards
are added or removed through a formal assessment procedure against published
criteria, and any organisation may nominate a standard.

The `start_date` of 2006 records the establishment of the Forum; the exact
date and instrument reference still need confirmation from the
Staatscourant, and the relationship to the College Standaardisatie (which
may since have been reorganised) is unresolved.

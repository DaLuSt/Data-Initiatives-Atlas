  Lebanon's telecommunications regulator, established under Law 431 of 2002 as an independent public institution to liberalise,
  regulate and develop telecommunications. A Chairman and four Members, appointed by the Council of Ministers for a single
  five-year term, run it; its decisions must be reasoned, it must make its data, documents and records available to the public
  on written request except where commercial confidentiality or competition is at risk, and it publishes an annual report in
  the Official Gazette. The current Board was appointed by Decree No. 1328 of September 2025.

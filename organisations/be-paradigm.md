## Description

Paradigm is the Brussels-Capital Region's public digital-transformation
body — the region's ICT partner for computer, telematics and
cartographic development serving regional, community and local
institutions.

## A four-decade legal lineage, one continuous body

Reading `be.brussels`'s own **"Wettelijke basis van Paradigm"** page
directly finds a single institution renamed and re-mandated repeatedly
rather than replaced:

- **Law of 21 August 1987** — founds the body as the **Centre
  d'Informatique pour la Région Bruxelloise (CIRB)** / **Centrum voor
  Informatica voor het Brusselse Gewest (CIBG)**.
- **Ordinance of 20 May 1999** and **ordinance of 29 March 2001** —
  reorganise the CIRB/CIBG.
- **Ordinance of 8 May 2014** — designates the CIRB/CIBG as the
  region's **"gewestelijke dienstenintegrator"** (regional service
  integrator), the page's own account of how the body came to operate
  under the name **Paradigm**, though the page does not give an exact
  date the name itself changed.

A further ordinance of **February 2024** (per secondary reporting, not
independently fetched this pass) is described as formally establishing
the current Paradigm mandate.

## Not modelled

- The individual 1999, 2001, 2014 and February 2024 ordinances as
  separate Atlas entities — named here in prose as one continuous
  legal history rather than four instruments, consistent with how the
  Atlas treats other renamed-in-place bodies (e.g. [[DE-BMV]]).

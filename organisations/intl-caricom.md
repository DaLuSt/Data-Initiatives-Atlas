# Caribbean Community (CARICOM)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #547, the Caribbean's regional bodies). From CARICOM's own site and the Caribbean Telecommunications Union's page, read directly. The Revised Treaty's text and the founding date were not read.

## Description

"All members subscribe to the Community's principles outlined in the Revised Treaty of Chaguaramas (2002). Leaders of member states shape the Community's policies and priorities. They meet twice yearly ... at the Conferences of Heads of Government." Fifteen Member States, plus Associate Members (Anguilla, Bermuda, the British Virgin Islands, the Cayman Islands, Curaçao and others). Five Member States have an anchor in the Atlas (Barbados, Guyana, Haiti, Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago); the other ten (Antigua and Barbuda, Bahamas, Belize, Dominica, Grenada, Montserrat, Saint Lucia, St Kitts and Nevis, St Vincent and the Grenadines, Suriname) have none, so they have no edge. Its regional statistics programme is run by the Secretariat and is not modelled.

# Gas and Electricity Markets Authority (GEMA)

> **Verified 2026-10-08.** Schedule 1 to the NIS Regulations 2018 and section 1
> of the Utilities Act 2000 were read directly at legislation.gov.uk. Ofgem's
> About page was read directly.

## Description

Section 1(1) of the **Utilities Act 2000** provides: "There shall be a body
corporate to be known as the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority (in this Act
referred to as 'the Authority')". The legislation.gov.uk commencement note says
section 1(1), (2) and (4) came into force on 1 November 2000 (S.I. 2000/2917).

Under [[GB-NIS-REGULATIONS]] Schedule 1 it is a **joint competent authority**
with the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero, for electricity
and (apart from some essential services) gas. In Northern Ireland the Schedule
names the Department of Finance (Northern Ireland) instead.

## Not asserted

- **Its relationship to Ofgem.** Ofgem's own About page, read directly, says
  "Ofgem stands for the Office of Gas and Electricity Markets", "We regulate the
  energy markets" and "We are a non-ministerial government department". It does
  not mention the Authority, and no source read states how the two relate, so
  Ofgem is not recorded as an alias or a separate entity. A search summary
  describes the Authority as Ofgem's governing board; that was not read on a
  primary page.
- The Secretary of State's own role, which is a ministerial office, not a body.

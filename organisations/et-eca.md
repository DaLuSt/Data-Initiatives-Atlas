# Ethiopian Communications Authority (ECA)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #473, Ethiopia). Its home page was read directly on a runner. The Proclamation itself was not.

## Description

The regulator of the communications sector, established by the Communications Service Proclamation No. 1148/2019. Its site covers licensing of telecommunications, postal and information-technology services, radio spectrum, numbering, type approval, a Government Certification Authority, consumer affairs and public consultations, and publishes policy, laws, regulations, directives and frameworks. Recent items include determinations of Significant Market Power and interconnection rates.

## Data protection, not recorded

Secondary sources say the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024 (adopted 4 April 2024, in force 24 July 2024) names the ECA as the enforcing body and that the Proclamation supports the Digital Identification Proclamation No. 1284/2023. **Neither is on the ECA home page read**, and neither Proclamation's text was read, so no Act entity or data-protection edge is recorded.

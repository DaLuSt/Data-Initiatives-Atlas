# Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU)

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #543, Eastern Europe's regional bodies). Sourced from the Union's own home page and the Eurasian Economic Commission's own news items, read directly. The Treaty's text was not read.

## Description

"An international organization for regional economic integration. It has international legal personality and is established by the Treaty on the Eurasian Economic Union." It provides "for free movement of goods, services, capital and labor" and "pursues coordinated, harmonized and single policy in the sectors determined by the Treaty and international agreements within the Union". Its supreme body is the Supreme Eurasian Economic Council of the Heads of State; the permanent supranational regulatory body is [[INTL-EEC]]. Members: Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, the Kyrgyz Republic and the Russian Federation. Kazakhstan, also a Member State, has no country anchor in the Atlas yet, so it has no edge.

## What it carries in the Atlas

Digital trade and e-signature rules, read through the Commission: [[INTL-EEC-EDS-B2G-2023]] and the Third Major Protocol ([[INTL-EAEU-PROTOCOL-3-2023]]) that clarifies the "transboundary space of trust" in Annex 3 to the Treaty. The Union's "Digital Agenda until 2025" (Supreme Council Decision No. 12 of 11 October 2017, named in secondary sources) is not modelled because its official text was not found; see `discovery/unresolved.md`.

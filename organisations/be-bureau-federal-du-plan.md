# Bureau fédéral du Plan (Federal Planning Bureau)

> **Created 2026-09-19**, to source [[BE-INDICATEURS-DEVELOPPEMENT-DURABLE]],
> the body's own sustainable-development indicator publication, partially
> closing `discovery/unresolved.md` row #175.

## Description

Confirmed by reading plan.be's own "Who we are" page directly: the FPB
"supports political decision-making with objective figures and facts"
through forecasts and policy analysis in the economic, social and
environmental domains. It is "a de facto independent 'institution of
public utility' that reports to the Prime Minister and the Minister of
Economy," and also holds designation as an **Independent Fiscal
Institution (IFI)**.

## Three names, one continuous body

Confirmed by reading the same page directly: the institution was **set up
in 1959** under the name **Bureau of Economic Programming**, renamed the
**Planning Bureau in 1970**, then the **Federal Planning Bureau in 1994**
following Belgian state reform. `start_date` is recorded as the 1994
renaming, since that is the name and legal form under which the
institution currently operates and the sourced anchor date for the current
entity; the 1959/1970 predecessor names are recorded in prose rather than
as separate entities or a `previous_version` chain, since no source read
this pass describes each transition as a formal succession rather than a
continuous body being renamed.

## Not modelled

- The **Bureau of Economic Programming** (1959) and **Planning Bureau**
  (1970) predecessor names, as distinct entities — see above.
- Its status as an **Independent Fiscal Institution (IFI)**, a separate
  EU-mandated designation not independently researched this pass.

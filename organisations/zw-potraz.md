# POTRAZ (Zimbabwe)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #478, Zimbabwe). Read on POTRAZ's own site and in the Act. `start_date` is not recorded: the page gives only "February 2001".

## What the pages say

Sector regulator: it regulates communications, promotes universal services, allocates radio spectrum and ensures service quality. As Data Protection Authority ([[ZW-CDPA-2021]], ss. 5-6) it licenses data controllers (the home page shows a licence handover ceremony), publishes implementation guidelines (appointment of data protection officers, 2024; licensing of data controllers, 2025; consent, data breach notification and handling, cross-border transfers and children's personal information, 2026), runs data protection officer training (a 2026 calendar), holds data privacy symposia (the second in January 2025) and Africa Data Protection Day events, and takes complaints.

## Description

The Agencia Digital de Andalucía was created by the **twenty-second
additional provision of Law 3/2020, of 28 December** — Andalusia's own
2021 Budget Law — as **"una agencia administrativa"** under **Article
54.2.a) of Law 9/2007, of 22 October**, on the Administration of the
Junta de Andalucía. Its Statutes were approved by **Decreto 128/2021, of
30 March 2021**, published in the BOJA on **8 April 2021**. It carries
**"personalidad jurídica pública diferenciada, plena capacidad jurídica y
de obrar y patrimonio y tesorería propios"** — differentiated public
legal personality, full legal capacity, and its own assets and treasury.

## What it does

The agency integrates all information-technology, telecommunications and
open-government services of the Junta de Andalucía's regional
administration — the same consolidating function [[ES-MADRID-DIGITAL]]
performs for Madrid, though ADA was created once, by a single decree,
rather than through Madrid's decades of renaming.

## What is sourced

"Les six pays qui composent la Communauté Économique et Monétaire de l'Afrique Centrale": Cameroon, Central African Republic, Congo, Gabon, Equatorial Guinea, Chad. The home page presents the Commission of CEMAC as the institution that informs on "l'intégration économique et monétaire de notre sous-région" (news of July 2026 on the Ebola situation and inflation). The directive is adopted by the Council of Ministers of the Central African Economic Union on a proposal of the Commission and after the opinion of the Inter-State Committee.

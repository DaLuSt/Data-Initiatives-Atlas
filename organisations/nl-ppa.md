# Platform Provincie-Architecten (PPA)

> **Created 2026-09-06**, closing a gap [[NL-PETRA]]'s own entity had
> flagged twice as "the weakest entity in the Atlas": no maintainer was
> ever sourced for it, and the entity carried only an unsourced Atlas
> assumption that [[NL-IPO]] might be the owner. Reading petra.wikixl.nl's
> own "Over de PETRA" page directly names the real body — and it is not
> IPO.

## Description

Confirmed by reading petra.wikixl.nl's own page directly: "Het beheer van
de PETRA-wiki ligt nu in handen van vakgroep PPA (Platform Provincie
Architecten)" — management of the PETRA wiki now lies in the hands of the
PPA working group. "Elke provincie neemt hieraan deel met minimaal één
architect" — every province participates with at least one architect. A
WebSearch-only synthesis (not independently confirmed by a directly-read
page) adds that BIJ12 also attends, and that the platform more broadly
develops standards, exchanges practice, and supports joint digitalisation
efforts across the twelve provinces — not confirmed directly, so treated
as corroborating context rather than a sourced claim.

The PPA took over management of the PETRA wiki at its November 2021
republication; no source read establishes when the PPA itself was formed.

## Not IPO

[[NL-PETRA]]'s own entity previously carried `organisations: [NL-IPO]` as
an explicitly-labelled Atlas assumption, never sourced. Nothing read this
pass names [[NL-IPO]] in connection with PETRA or the PPA at all — the
real maintainer is this cross-provincial working group of architects, a
structurally different kind of body than IPO (the provinces' formal
inter-governmental association). The assumption is removed accordingly.

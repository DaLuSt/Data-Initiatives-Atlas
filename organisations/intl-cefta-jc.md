# CEFTA Joint Committee

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #542). Sourced from the Committee's own decisions and its Ministerial Conclusions of 2 December 2025, read directly.

## Description

The Joint Committee is the body of the CEFTA Parties that adopts decisions under CEFTA 2006. Its 18th meeting was held in Pristina on 2 December 2025 and was attended by the authorised representatives of the Parties; the Conclusions record the adoption of Decisions 8/2025, 9/2025, 10/2025 and Recommendation 1/2025.

## Digital decisions in the Atlas

[[INTL-CEFTA-JC-2-2024]], [[INTL-CEFTA-JC-10-2025]] and [[INTL-CEFTA-JC-11-2025]]. Its other decisions (customs, rules of origin, SPS, intellectual property, parcel delivery, geo-blocking) are not modelled.

# Information and Data Protection Commission (Botswana)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #471, Botswana). Known from the Act's long title on the e-Laws listing only; the Commission's own site could not be found (a dedicated site was not located, and the government portal `gov.bw` has an expired certificate).

## Description

The body whose continuation the Data Protection Act, 2024 ([[BW-DPA-2024]]) provides for. Its powers, divisions and independence are not recorded here because the Act's sections were not read; secondary sources mention a Data Protection Division and an Access to Information Division.

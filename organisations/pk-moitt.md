# Ministry of Information Technology and Telecommunication (Pakistan)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #476, Pakistan). Read on the Ministry's own site, moitt.gov.pk. No founding date is recorded.

## Mandate, from its own page

Eleven items under Schedule II of the Rules of Business 1973, among them: an integrated plan and policy for IT and telecommunications; coordination with provincial governments, autonomous bodies, the private sector and international organisations; promoting IT applications; guidelines for standardising software used in government; "planning, policy making and legislation covering all aspects of telecommunications excluding radio and television"; matters of the National Information Technology Board (not modelled here: its own site gives 2014 as its establishment) and the Pakistan Software Export Board; the administration of the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Ordinance 2007 and its rules; and federal functions towards the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority.

## Policies it lists

Its Policies page lists, with status as shown there, the Data Governance Policy 2026 (Draft, 26 June 2026; see [[PK-DATA-GOVERNANCE-POLICY-2026]]), the National Artificial Intelligence Policy (Approved, 31 July 2025), the National Cyber Security Policy 2021 (Approved, 27 July 2021), the Digital Pakistan Policy (Approved, 2 July 2018) and the National Semi Conductor Policy (Draft, 25 November 2024). Only the first was read.

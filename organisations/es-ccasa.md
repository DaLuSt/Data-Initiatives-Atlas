# Centro de Cálculo de Álava (CCASA)

## Description

CCASA is Álava's own provincial IT company, founded on **4 November
1969** — decades before its Gipuzkoan counterpart [[ES-IZFE]] (1993) —
by four founding parties: the **Diputación de Álava**, the
**Ayuntamiento de Vitoria-Gasteiz**, the **Caja de Ahorros Municipal de
Vitoria**, and the **Cámara de Comercio**. It began with six employees
and one computer, originally serving the banking sector, before becoming
primarily focused on the Diputación de Álava's own needs by the late
1980s.

## Now a medio propio of the Diputación Foral

Confirmed directly on the Diputación Foral de Álava's own transparency
portal: CCASA today "ostenta el carácter de medio propio en el ámbito de
las Tecnologías de la Información y la Comunicación" (holds the status
of in-house resource in ICT) for the **Diputación Foral de Álava**, its
**foral autonomous bodies**, and the **Juntas Generales de Álava**
(Álava's provincial assembly) — a narrower, more exclusive relationship
than a mere service contractor.

## The third of four Basque NISAE companies

Confirmed directly on euskadi.eus's own NISAE page (already cited on
[[ES-EJIE]] and [[ES-IZFE]]): CCASA is one of the four public IT
companies — with **EJIE, IZFE and LANTIK** — that jointly constitute the
**Nodo de Interoperabilidad y Seguridad de las Administraciones de
Euskadi (NISAE)**, serving the Basque Government and the three Provincial
Councils (Álava, Bizkaia, Gipuzkoa). CCASA is Álava's own contribution
to that four-way structure, the oldest of the three provincial companies
by over two decades. All four NISAE companies are now modelled: LANTIK,
the last, is [[ES-LANTIK]] (Bizkaia, added 2026-09-25).

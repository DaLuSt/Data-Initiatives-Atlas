## Description

Established by article 3 of [[NL-WET-SUWI]]; its tasks are in chapter 6 (article 34: the Algemene Ouderdomswet, the Algemene nabestaandenwet and the Algemene Kinderbijslagwet; article 35: its insured-persons administration).

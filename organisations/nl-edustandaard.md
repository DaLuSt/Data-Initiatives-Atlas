## Description

Edustandaard is a network organisation strengthening the digital
infrastructure of Dutch education by registering, monitoring and
developing data-exchange agreements and standards across all education
sectors. Reading `edustandaard.nl`'s own page directly: it brings
together public and private chain partners — [[NL-SURF]], MBO Digitaal,
Kennisnet, the Ministry of Education, Culture and Science, the Dienst
Uitvoering Onderwijs, education institutions, publishers and software
suppliers.

## Founded as a vereniging, now a network organisation

Secondary reporting from 2005 (`ictoblog.nl`, not independently
corroborated by a primary source this pass) dates its founding to
**December 2004**, as the **vereniging EduStandaard** — arising from a
need, identified in the education field's Educatieve Contentketen
programme, for user support, maintenance and promotion of data-exchange
standards. Edustandaard's own current site describes itself instead as
a **"netwerkorganisatie"** (network organisation) rather than a formal
association, without stating when or why that framing changed.

## Maintains ROSA

Edustandaard's own page, read directly, states its **Architectuurraad**
(Architecture Council) is supported by [[NL-ROSA]]'s own Quality
Assurance team when advising on coherence between education-sector
agreements and standards — corroborating, from Edustandaard's own side,
what [[NL-ROSA]]'s file had already established from `edustandaard.nl`'s
"Relevante architecturen" page: the Architecture Council maintains ROSA
itself. That `maintained-by` edge is recorded on [[NL-ROSA]]'s own
entity.

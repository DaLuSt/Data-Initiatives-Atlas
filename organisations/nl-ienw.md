## Description

IenW is the Dutch ministry responsible for transport, aviation, public
works, dikes/flood management, and water resource management. Reading
`rijksoverheid.nl`'s own page directly: it works toward "an accessible
country with strong dikes... where you travel safely from A to B,"
covering road safety and vehicle regulation, dike and flood management,
climate adaptation, and aviation/drones.

## History

Confirmed by reading `en.wikipedia.org` directly, corroborating the
rijksoverheid.nl page: the ministry was **established 14 October 2010**
as the **Ministry of Infrastructure and the Environment**, formed from a
merger of the Ministry of Transport and Water Management and the Ministry
of Housing, Spatial Planning and the Environment. In **2017** it was
**renamed** to its current form, with environmental responsibilities
transferred elsewhere.

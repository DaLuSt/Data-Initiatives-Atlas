# Global Cross-Border Privacy Rules (CBPR) Forum

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #556, Eastern Asia's regional bodies; APEC and the Forum span Asia, Oceania and the Americas and are added once). From the Forum's own site, read directly. `start_date` is the date of the Declaration (21 April 2022); the site says only "established in 2022". The Global CBPR Framework, the system's programme requirements and the list of accountability agents were not read.

## What is sourced

Members (at the time of reading): Australia, Canada, the Dubai International Financial Centre, Japan, the Republic of Korea, Mexico, the Philippines, Singapore, Chinese Taipei and the United States. Associates: Bermuda, Nigeria, Mauritius and the United Kingdom. The home page (news of 10 June 2026) says the Forum published its third annual report and its 2026/2027 work programme, and (23 March 2026) is updating the Global CBPR System's programme requirements from 50 to more. Certification is voluntary and verified by third-party Accountability Agents. Anchored members and associates are linked below; the Dubai International Financial Centre, Chinese Taipei and Bermuda have no country anchor.

The Declaration of 21 April 2022 names seven economies as signatories (Canada, Japan, the Republic of Korea, the Philippines, Singapore, Chinese Taipei and the United States); the About page names nine as founders (adding Australia and Mexico). The difference was not explained on the pages read.

# MERCOSUR

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #545, South America's regional bodies). **Known only from two documents on MERCOSUR's own and Uruguay's official sites**: the minutes of Working Subgroup 13 of 22 May 2026 and the text of the 2021 e-commerce agreement. `mercosur.int` answers 403 to the agent, so the bloc's own pages, its founding treaty and its member list were not read.

## What is sourced

Four States Parties sign the e-commerce agreement and send delegations to the subgroup; Bolivia's delegation takes part under Decision CMC 20/19. The subgroup meets under a Pro Tempore Presidency (Paraguay's in May 2026). The agreement's preamble places it "en complemento a la iniciativa MERCOSUR Digital, al Acuerdo de Reconocimiento Mutuo de Certificados de Firma Digital del MERCOSUR y a otras normas": neither of those two is modelled, as their texts were not read.

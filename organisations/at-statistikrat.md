# Statistikrat (Austria)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #459, oversight bodies above statistical offices). Statistics Austria's page on the Statistikrat was read directly. The text of §§ 44-47 of the Bundesstatistikgesetz 2000 was not read, so the Act is not an entity here and the member institutions are not listed.

## Description

The Statistikrat is the council of the Bundesanstalt Statistik Österreich ([[AT-STATISTIK]]), governed by §§ 44 to 47 of the Bundesstatistikgesetz 2000. Per Statistics Austria's page it has sixteen members serving five years: four appointed by the Federal Chancellor and twelve delegated by institutions. Its tasks include opinions and recommendations, an annual activity report, and an annual report on the compliance with the statistical principles of § 24 of the Act. That last task is what places it beside the UK's [[GB-OSR]], France's [[FR-ASP]] and the EU's [[EU-ESGAB]], although a search summary (not read as a primary text) also describes it as giving opinions on draft regulations and EU statistical acts and commenting on work programmes and budgets, which the page read did not list.

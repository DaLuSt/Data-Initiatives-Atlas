# GCC Ministerial Committee for Posts and Telecommunications

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #553). Known **only** from the item on its 27th meeting. Its mandate, earlier meetings and the later ones (the item's date is February 2023) were not read.

## What the item says

Held on 5 February 2023 in Riyadh, chaired by Oman's Minister of Transport, Communications and Information Technology, with the heads of the relevant ministries or regulators of the United Arab Emirates, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait and the GCC Secretary General. Topics: the adoption of the guiding framework for the protection of personal information of GCC citizens, the adoption of an action plan for the telecommunications sector, and Saudi Arabia's initiative for a Research Award for Technology and Digital Economy Foresight.

# European Statistical Governance Advisory Board (ESGAB)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #459). The establishing Decision was read in full and Eurostat's ESGAB page directly; the Board's own reports were not read.

## Description

The ESGAB is the independent body of seven experts that oversees how the European Statistics Code of Practice is applied across the European Statistical System, and by the Commission (Eurostat) in particular. It is the EU-level counterpart of the national oversight bodies above a statistical office ([[GB-OSR]] in the UK, [[FR-ASP]] in France). Unlike those, it reports to the Parliament and the Council rather than to a national legislature, and it advises rather than regulates: the Decision gives it no power to sanction.

Its annual report goes to the European Parliament and the Council. Members serve three years, renewable once; the chairperson is chosen by the Council and approved by the Parliament. Details are in [[EU-DECISION-235-2008]].

## Description

SIDN manages the **.nl** country-code top-level domain. Reading
`sidn.nl`'s own milestones page directly, in the organisation's own
words: **"We took over responsibility for registering .nl domain names
on 31 January 1996."** Its founders were **Piet Beertema, Ted Lindgreen
and Boudewijn Nederkoorn**.

## The world's first active ccTLD

The same page states **.nl was the first active country-code top-level
domain in the world**, due to Piet Beertema, who requested it from
internet pioneer Jon Postel while working at the **Centrum Wiskunde &
Informatica (CWI)**. CWI registered the first `.nl` domain,
**cwi.nl, on 1 May 1986** — nearly a decade before SIDN itself existed
to take over the registration function at scale.

## Scale today

SIDN is **ICANN-accredited** and not a commercial enterprise. It
registered around **6.2 million** `.nl` domain names in 2024, making
`.nl` the fourth-largest country-code top-level domain, after China,
Germany and the United Kingdom (per secondary reporting, not
independently fetched this pass).

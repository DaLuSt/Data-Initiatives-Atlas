# Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)

> **Created 2026-11-01** (roadmap #470). Known **only** from one PIB document. Its mandate, its other laws (the Information Technology Act 2000, among them), its programmes and its founding were not read: `meity.gov.in` serves a JavaScript page, so the Ministry's own pages were not readable here.

## What is sourced

"The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology invited public comments on the draft Rules before finalising them." The consultation "received 6,915 inputs" and held sessions in Delhi, Mumbai, Guwahati, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Bengaluru and Chennai.

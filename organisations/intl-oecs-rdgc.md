# OECS Regional Data Governance Council (RDGC)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #547). From three OECS media releases (2023, 2025, 2026), read directly. The OECS Regional Data Governance Charter, which a search result links to the council's work, could not be read (`emishub.org` returns a challenge page), so it is not modelled.

## What is sourced

Launched on 11 October 2023 in Saint Lucia, attended by directors of statistics of member states and officials of the World Bank, CARICOM, the Caribbean Development Bank and the Eastern Caribbean Central Bank; a component of the World Bank-funded **OECS Data for Decision Making** project (approved May 2022) for the participating countries Grenada, Saint Lucia and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. It aims to "improve the capacity ... to produce and publicly disseminate statistical data" and, in the 2025 release, to harmonise "data policies and practices, including the processes to capture, produce, manage, and share data for regional-level analytics". Chairs: Kenita Paul of Grenada (elected October 2023), then Corneil Williams of Saint Kitts (handed over at the 4th meeting, which opened on 28 April 2025). At that meeting it committed to a regional data governance framework, a five-year implementation plan for training and capacity building, a work programme and a budget. The 6th meeting (21 to 24 April 2026, Grenada) dealt with the 2030 census round and an OECS microdata catalogue (metadata standards, anonymisation protocols, access policies).

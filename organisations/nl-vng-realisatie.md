## Description

VNG Realisatie is VNG's implementation arm, working at the intersection
of municipalities' societal tasks, service delivery, execution and ICT
— a business unit of [[NL-VNG]] itself, not a separate legal person.

## From KING to VNG Realisatie

Reading `binnenlandsbestuur.nl` directly: the organisation began as
**KING** (Kwaliteitsinstituut Nederlandse Gemeenten), a foundation whose
separate legal status was discontinued in **2013** as it progressively
merged into VNG. It was renamed **VNG Realisatie effective 1 January
2018** — described as **"het sluitstuk van het integratieproces"** (the
final piece of the integration process), which also broadened its
remit beyond information and service delivery to other municipal
executive domains. Since **1 January 2023**, VNG Compliance has merged
into it.

# USMCA Free Trade Commission

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #544). Chapter 30 and Chapter 34 of the agreement as published by USTR, and Canada's governance page, read directly. Its decisions, joint statements and meeting dates were not read one by one.

## Description

"Composed of government representatives of each Party at the level of Ministers or their designees" (Article 30.1). Its functions (30.2): consider implementation and operation, consider proposals to amend, supervise subsidiary bodies, consider ways to enhance trade and investment, adopt the dispute-settlement Rules of Procedure and Code of Conduct, and review the panelist roster every three years; it may also adopt certain modifications (tariff schedules, rules of origin and similar lists). Article 34.7 gives it the joint review of the agreement on the sixth anniversary of entry into force. Canada's page lists its decisions (No. 1 of 2 July 2020 to No. 5 of 22 February 2023) and joint statements from 2021 to 2024; it does not say whether there are later ones, and none was read.

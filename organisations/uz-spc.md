# State Personalisation Centre under the Cabinet of Ministers

> **Verified 2026-10-09 against the law's text only.** The Centre's own site was not read. The English name is the Atlas's translation of the Cyrillic name in Article 8 and has not been checked against an English-language source; confidence is medium for that reason.

## Description

Article 8 of [[UZ-ZRU-547]] names it as the authorised state body; the same Article lists that it implements state policy in the field of personal data, takes part in developing state programmes, and approves the model procedure for processing personal data.

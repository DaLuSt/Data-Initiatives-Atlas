# Personal Information Protection Commission (Japan)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #482, Japan). Read on the Commission's English site. No founding date is recorded: the pages read give the Act's number and year but not the Commission's start, and the Commission has been reorganised since 2003 (not read).

## What the pages say

- **Status.** An independent commission with a high degree of independence and political neutrality, of the type of the Fair Trade Commission and the Nuclear Regulation Authority.
- **Duties.** Formulating and promoting the basic policy; supervision, monitoring; complaints mediation; specific personal information protection assessment (for My Number data); public relations; international cooperation; accredited personal information protection organisations; reporting to the Diet.
- **Both Acts.** It supervises the Act on the Protection of Personal Information ([[JP-APPI]]) and, for specific personal information, the My Number Act ([[JP-MYNUMBER-ACT]]), for which the page says the Digital Agency ([[JP-DIGITAL-AGENCY]]) is responsible.
- **Recent work (home page, 2026).** Memoranda of cooperation with Singapore's PDPC (July 2026), the Philippines' National Privacy Commission (June 2026) and the Canadian Privacy Commissioner (December 2025); research on the Global Cross-Border Privacy Rules system; the triennial-review reform policy ([[JP-APPI-TRIENNIAL-REVIEW-2026]]).

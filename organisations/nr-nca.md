  The communications regulator of Nauru. The Communications and Broadcasting Act 2018, read directly on WIPO Lex, establishes it in section 5(1): a body corporate with perpetual succession (5(3)), 'responsible to the Minister for Telecommunications' (5(2)) and 'independent and impartial in the exercise of its powers and functions' (5(5)); it has three members appointed by the Minister, among them the Chief Regulator (section 6), who is 'the head of the Authority' (section 14), and its functions include to 'grant, renew, suspend and revoke licences' (section 11(b)).
  The Act repeals the Telecommunications and Regulatory Affairs Act 2017 (long title and section 115); what that Act had set up was not read. No page of the Authority itself was read.

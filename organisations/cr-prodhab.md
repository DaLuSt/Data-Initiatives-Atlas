# PRODHAB (Costa Rica)

> **Created 2026-10-17** (roadmap #477, Costa Rica). Read on the Agency's own site. **No `start_date`**: the page gives the law that created it (7 July 2011, published 5 September 2011), not the day the Agency began.

## What the page says

Main aim: to guarantee anyone, whatever their nationality, residence or domicile, respect for their right to informational self-determination regarding their private life and other personality rights, and their freedom and equality with respect to automated or manual processing of their data. It guides citizens in completing the forms for exercising their rights and entities in meeting the obligations of Law 8968 and its regulation. The regulation was reformed twice, by executive decrees 40008-JP (December 2016) and 41582-JP (February 2019), "updating Costa Rican personal data protection rules to international trends". The site offers an automated virtual assistant, multimedia materials and a registration service.

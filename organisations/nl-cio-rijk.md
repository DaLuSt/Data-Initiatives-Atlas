## Description

CIO Rijk leads the Dutch central government's **CIO-stelsel**, which
coordinates ICT and information provision across all ministries. Each
ministry has its own Chief Information Officer; CIO Rijk chairs the
**CIO-beraad**, where departmental CIOs and major public service
providers decide on government-wide digitalisation matters — cloud
policy and responsible generative-AI use are the two named examples.

## Reformed 1 January 2026

Reading `rijksoverheid.nl`'s own article directly: the **Besluit
CIO-stelsel Rijksdienst 2025**, in force from that date, strengthens
CIO Rijk's own authority to establish **binding frameworks** across
ministries and lets it decide independently when the CIO-beraad cannot
reach consensus. The reform also adds three new advisory roles — Chief
Privacy Officer, Chief Data Officer and Chief Technology Officer — and
formally brings the CIO for the Caribbean Netherlands into the system.
The responsible ministry is **BZK** ([[NL-BZK]]), on the proposal of
State Secretary Van Marum.

# Southern African Development Community (SADC)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #552, Southern Africa's regional bodies). From SADC's own site (members, history and treaty, ICT pages) and the ITU's publication of the data protection model law, read directly. The SADC Treaty's text and the date SADC was founded in its present form were not read (the history page says Heads of Government agreed in 1992 to transform SADCC into SADC).

## What is sourced

Sixteen Member States, of which seven have a country anchor (Botswana, Malawi, Mauritius, Seychelles, South Africa, Tanzania, Zimbabwe); the other nine (Angola, Comoros, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini, Lesotho, Madagascar, Mozambique, Namibia, Zambia) have none, so no edge. The ICT page records the 2001 Declaration on Information and Communication Technologies and the 2012 Regional Infrastructure Development Master Plan (priorities through 2027); neither was read.

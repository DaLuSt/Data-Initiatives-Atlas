# Information Regulator (South Africa)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #474, South Africa). Its home page was read directly on a runner.

## Description

An independent body established under section 39 of [[ZA-POPIA]], subject only to the law and the Constitution and accountable to the National Assembly. It has a **dual mandate**: it monitors and enforces compliance with POPIA and with the Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2000 (PAIA, Act 2 of 2000). Its site covers registration of Information Officers, complaints, enforcement notices, PAIA resources and fees, a section on Acts, rules of procedure and Government Gazette notices, and invitations for public comment (for example on regulations on processing health or sex-life data under section 112(2)(c), 30 September 2025).

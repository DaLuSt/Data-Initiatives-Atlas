# Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales (Chile)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #473, Chile). Known from the law's title and the Diario Oficial's recitals; the law's articles on the Agency were not read. **Status `planned`:** the law creates the Agency but its main provisions enter into force on 1 December 2026, and secondary sources say it is not yet constituted.

## Description

The new supervisory body created by [[CL-LEY-21719]] to oversee processing of personal data and apply the sanctions the law provides. The Academia Judicial's page and the Diario Oficial give no description of its legal nature; secondary sources call it an autonomous, technical, decentralised public-law body, which was not read here and is not asserted.

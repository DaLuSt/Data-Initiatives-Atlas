## Description

Geonovum develops and manages the Dutch base set of geo-standards, whose
purpose is to make geographic information findable, accessible,
exchangeable and reusable via the national geo-information infrastructure.

Its standards management is documented rather than ad hoc: Geonovum
maintains a generic management document describing what it does at each
component of the BOMOS model, plus a specific management document per
managed standard recording supplementary agreements. Decision-making within
the escalation procedure runs in principle between the Programmaraad — the
commissioning body for geo-standards management — and Geonovum.

Its base programme is funded by subsidy from the ministries of the Interior
and Kingdom Relations ([[NL-BZK]]) and of Agriculture, Nature and Food
Quality, by [[NL-KADASTER]] and by the Geological Survey of the Netherlands,
part of [[NL-TNO]]. Geonovum reports on this spending to its subsidy
providers and through its annual report.

The Programmaraad and BOMOS are not yet Atlas entities. BOMOS in particular
is a Dutch standards-management model likely to warrant its own entity in
Batch 4; it is queued in `discovery/research-queue.md`.

## Description

NOiV — Nederland Open in Verbinding — was a Dutch government action plan
and programme office for open standards and open source software. Reading
`noraonline.nl`'s own wiki page directly: the Second Chamber requested the
plan in 2007 and it was adopted by the cabinet at the **end of 2007**; the
programme ran through to its conclusion at the **end of 2011**; and it
**succeeded the OSOSS programme (2003–2008)**. It was executed on behalf
of the **Ministry of Economic Affairs** and the **Ministry of the
Interior and Kingdom Relations**.

## Commissioned BOMOS

Reading `forumstandaardisatie.nl`'s own BOMOS founding document directly
(the 2010 "BOMOS versie 2 — deel 1: de basis" PDF): the concrete questions
organisations faced about managing and developing standards were, in the
document's own words, **"voor het programmabureau Nederland Open in
Verbinding aanleiding om met de standaardisatiecommunity een hulpmiddel te
maken"** — the reason for the NOiV programmabureau to make a tool,
together with the standardisation community. That tool became
[[NL-BOMOS]].

This sits alongside, rather than contradicts, what [[NL-BOMOS]]'s own file
already records: a 2006 Bureau Forum Standaardisatie working group
produced a memo that formed BOMOS's technical starting point, while NOiV's
programmabureau was the body that then convened the standardisation
community around that starting point to actually produce BOMOS version 1
in 2009. Two different, compatible roles — originating memo, and
commissioning/convening programme office — both real and both sourced.

## Status

Purely historical: the programme concluded at the end of 2011, and no
source read this pass describes any successor body inheriting its mandate
directly. Its open-standards role passed, in practice, to the
already-established [[NL-FORUM-STANDAARDISATIE]] (est. 2006) and the
[[NL-OBDO]]/`pas toe of leg uit` regime it maintains, but no source states
a formal supersession, so no `successor` is asserted.

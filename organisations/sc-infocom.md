# Information Commission (Seychelles)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #474, Seychelles). Read on the Commission's own site, infocom.sc. The page gives no founding date, so none is recorded.

## What the page says it can do

Investigate complaints and data breaches; conduct audits and compliance reviews; issue enforcement notices requiring corrective action; impose administrative fines where appropriate; provide education and outreach. Its menu also lists the Access to Information Act, 2018 (and its 2021 amendment), which was not read.

# Pakistan Digital Authority

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #476, Pakistan). Known only from the Ministry's draft Data Governance Policy; the Authority's own site was not found, and the Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025 (Act No. I of 2025), its source of powers, was not read. No founding date is recorded. The Policy's machine-readable block names it as the publishing authority, and points to a standards portal at standards.dnp.gov.pk, which was not opened.

## What the draft policy gives it

Clauses 16.1 to 16.9, read in full: issue the supporting instruments listed in the Policy's Annex II; steward the National Data Catalogue; oversee WASL (the governed national interoperability pathway for inter-agency data exchange) and the National Open Data Portal; run compliance, conformance and audit; recognise and supervise data trusts and intermediaries; issue binding written directions to public bodies; designate a National Chief Data Officer; establish by notification and chair a National Data Governance Council (federal and provincial governments, sector regulators, custodians of primary data registers and others). Clause 16.9 says supervision of personal-data processing as a matter of data-subject rights belongs to "the authority designated under the personal data protection law", with which PDA would coordinate by memorandum of understanding.

All of this is what the **draft** proposes; none of it has been checked against the Act or a Gazette notification.

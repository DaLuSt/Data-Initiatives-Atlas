## Description

LDI is Rhineland-Palatinate's own central IT service provider,
confirmed directly on its own page: **"Der LDI wurde am 1. Januar 2003
über ein Landesgesetz gegründet"** — founded 1 January 2003 by a state
law, operating as a **Landesbetrieb** (state enterprise), the same legal
form already recorded on [[DE-IT-NRW]], [[DE-HZD]] and [[DE-BITBW]].

## A longer prior history, not independently confirmed

A Rhein-Zeitung account (found by search, **not independently fetched**
this pass) states LDI's institutional prior history traces to the
**Daten- und Informationszentrum (DIZ)**, the state's central computing
centre founded in **1996**, and that a July 2006 IT-structure
reorganisation significantly strengthened LDI's role as the Land's IT
service provider. Per the Atlas's no-fabrication discipline, this detail
is recorded here in prose only — not asserted with the same confidence
as the 2003 founding, which rests on LDI's own page.

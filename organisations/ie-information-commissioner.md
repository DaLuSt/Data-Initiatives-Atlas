# Information Commissioner (Ireland)

> **Created 2026-11-23** (roadmap #770). Two statutory instruments were read directly and both name this office as the appeal body. The Freedom of Information Act 2014 itself, which constitutes the office, was not read, and the Commissioner's own site (oic.ie) answered 403, so `confidence` is medium and no start date or holder is recorded.

## What is sourced

- **Environmental information:** regulation 12 of S.I. No. 133 of 2007 establishes the office of Commissioner for Environmental Information, independent in its functions, held by whoever holds the office of Information Commissioner, and gives the appeal against a public authority's decision.
- **Re-use of public sector information:** S.I. No. 376 of 2021 defines the Information Commissioner as the "Appeal Commissioner"; regulation 15 sets the appeal against a refusal, a charge or conditions, and regulation 16 allows delegation to staff.

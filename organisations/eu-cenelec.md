## Description

CENELEC is the European Standardisation Organisation responsible for
standardisation in electrotechnical engineering, and one of the three ESOs
recognised by [[EU-REG-1025-2012]] alongside [[EU-CEN]] and
[[EU-ETSI]]. Confirmed by reading cencenelec.eu directly (2026-08-28):
CEN and CENELEC are "two distinct private international non-profit
organizations" that work in close partnership, sharing a joint web
presence and management structure, and together operate through 34
national member countries.

Confirmed by reading Wikipedia's CENELEC article directly (2026-08-28):
CENELEC was established in **1973** and, as described there, maintains 34
full members — all EU member states, three EFTA states, plus Türkiye,
Serbia, North Macedonia and the United Kingdom. Though not an EU
institution, its standards ("EN" standards) gain official standing through
[[EU-REG-1025-2012]], applicable across the EU and the EEA. This
membership breakdown is Wikipedia's own account and was not
cross-checked against a CENELEC primary membership list; it is recorded
at `confidence: medium` accordingly.

Its relevance to the Atlas is narrower than CEN's or ETSI's — it enters
mainly through the ISO/IEC standards underpinning [[NL-BIO]], where the IEC
side of NEN-EN-ISO/IEC 27001 and 27002 runs through the electrotechnical
chain. That chain cannot be completed until IEC exists (Batch 13).

`coverage: low`. Note the Dutch counterpart structure: [[NL-NEN]] has been
the name of a cooperation between the NNI foundation and the Dutch
electrotechnical committee NEC since 2000 — the NEC being CENELEC's national
counterpart. That was flagged as an open modelling question in Batch 2 and
remains open.

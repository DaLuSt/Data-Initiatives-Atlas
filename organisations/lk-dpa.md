# Data Protection Authority, Sri Lanka

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #472, Sri Lanka). Its home page was read directly on a runner.

## Description

The Authority "was established under the PDPA, in August 2023, to regulate personal data processing and protect individuals' privacy", and is "committed to ensuring that all entities, both public and private, adhere to the legal obligations set out in the Act". Its site lists the Board of Directors, the Mandate of the DPA and the Background of the Data Protection Law, and gives an address in the BMICH, Colombo 07. The Authority's background page, read directly the same day, adds that the Act was passed by Parliament on 19 March 2022 and certified by the Speaker, that **Part V, which establishes the Authority, came into force on 17 July 2023**, that Parts VI, VIII, IX and X came into operation on 1 December 2023 and that Parts I, II, III and VII were to follow on 18 March 2025. The start date is left empty because the home page says August 2023 and the background page 17 July 2023 for Part V; the two are not reconciled.

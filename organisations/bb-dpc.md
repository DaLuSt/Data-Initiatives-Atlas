# Data Protection Commission (Barbados)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #474, Barbados). Read on the government's own page for the Commission. The page gives no founding date and names no Commissioner, so neither is recorded.

## What the page lists

Its main activities: administer the regulatory framework for processing personal data; register data controllers and data processors; promote public awareness of the risks, rules, safeguards and rights; investigate complaints of data breaches.

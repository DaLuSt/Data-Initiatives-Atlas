# Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority (MACRA)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #472, Malawi). Its home page was read directly on a runner; the page is mostly a menu and a spectrum-pricing note.

## Description

Malawi's communications regulator. The site lists licensing and quality-of-service work in telecommunications, broadcasting and postal and courier services, consumer protection, the **Malawi CERT**, a Universal Service Fund and an innovation fund, and publishes Acts, rules and regulations and policies.

## Data protection, not recorded

Secondary sources say the Data Protection Act 2024 designates MACRA as the data-protection authority (Government Notice No. 40 of 2024 fixing 3 June 2024 as commencement). **None of that was found on `macra.mw` in the page read**, and the Act's text (a law-firm PDF) was not read, so no Act entity or data-protection edge is recorded.

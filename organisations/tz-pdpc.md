# Personal Data Protection Commission (Tanzania)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #483, Tanzania). Known from the Act that establishes it; the Commission's own site was not reached, so its current leadership, premises and decisions are not recorded. No founding date is recorded: the Act commenced on 1 May 2023, but when the Commission was in fact set up was not read.

## What the Act says

Body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal, able to hold property, contract and sue (s. 6); functions listed in s. 7 (monitor compliance, register controllers and processors, among others); staff (s. 13); investigation powers (s. 42); offences for obstruction (s. 43); sources of funds (s. 51).

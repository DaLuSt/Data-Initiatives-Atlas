# African Union (AU)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #548 and the other African sub-regions: a body that spans them is added once). **Known only through its data instruments**: the Malabo Convention's treaty page and status list and the Data Policy Framework, read directly. The AU's constitutive act, membership and organs were not read; no country edges are drawn (the 55 countries on the status list are those of the treaty, not a membership list).

## What is sourced

An Assembly (which adopted the Malabo Convention in 2014), an Executive Council (which endorsed the Digital Transformation Strategy in February 2020 and the Data Policy Framework in February 2022), a Commission with an Infrastructure and Energy Department that publishes the framework, and a Specialized Technical Committee on Communication and ICT.

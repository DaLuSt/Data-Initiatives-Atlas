# CARICOM Secretariat

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #547). From the Secretariat's own page and its Data Protection and Privacy Rules, read directly.

## Description

Functions listed on its page include servicing meetings of the Organs and Bodies of the Community, conducting studies, providing services and technical assistance to Member States on request, and "collects, stores and disseminates to the Member States of the Community, information relevant for the achievement of its objectives". The Office of the Deputy Secretary-General oversees, among others, an Integrated Information System and the Regional Statistics Programme. Its rules for the personal information it holds are [[INTL-CARICOM-PDPR-2020]].

## Description

CEN is one of the three European Standardisation Organisations recognised by
Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 as providers of European standards, alongside
[[EU-CENELEC]] (electrotechnical) and [[EU-ETSI]] (ICT and
telecommunications). It brings together the national standardisation bodies
of 33 European countries.

Through Regulation 1025/2012 — which entered into force on 1 January 2013
and is the legal framework for the public-private partnership between the
ESOs and the EU/EFTA — the three organisations may receive a request to
produce harmonised European standards supporting EU legislation and
policies.

## The NEN connection

[[NL-NEN]] is the Netherlands' national standardisation body, which makes it
one of the 33 CEN members by the composition rule above. The

that the membership is inferred from a sourced composition rule rather than

## What the item says

"The participants noted the growing importance of international information security as a key element in the international security system" and agreed on joint efforts "to prevent the use of digital space for purposes incompatible with the tasks of ensuring international peace, security and stability". The SCO's Deputy Secretary-General spoke of cooperation "in preventing the use of information and communications technology for criminal purposes". Wuhan is described as hosting "the largest regional Cybersecurity Centre" being established.

# Ministry of Communication and Aviation (Solomon Islands)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #471, Solomon Islands). Two pages on the ministry's own site were read directly.

## Description

Its Communication Division "is responsible for the oversight and coordination of Information and Communication Technologies service and infrastructure delivery in Solomon Islands", and, since communication services are a core mandate, administers "certain functions of the Telecommunication, Postal Corporation and Television Acts". A separate page lists "Provide Oversight for and Coordinate the Implementation of the National ICT Policy and the National Broadcasting Policy" among its ICT services functions. The division works with regulators such as the Telecommunication Commission Solomon Islands and the Television Board (stated on a units page seen only through a search summary).

## What was not found

No data-protection or privacy law is recorded: secondary sources say there is none and that the only related rule is in the Telecommunications Act 2009, which was not read (PacLII returns a 403 challenge). A draft National ICT Policy of 2017 was seen by search summary only.

# Office of the Privacy Commissioner (New Zealand)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #478, New Zealand). Read on the Commissioner's own site. No founding date is recorded: the pages read do not give one.

## What the pages say

Its job: regulate the Privacy Act 2020 and make sure agencies know the rules; help individuals whose privacy has been breached; educate New Zealanders about their privacy rights. It is an Independent Crown Entity, funded by the state and independent of ministerial control, with seven teams. The Privacy Act page says the Act gives the Commissioner power to issue codes of practice, and that a number of codes were amended in 2026 to include the new rule 3A.

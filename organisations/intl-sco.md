# Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #554, Central Asia's regional bodies; the SCO spans Central, Eastern and Southern Asia and is added once). From the SCO's own site, read directly. The Charter's text was not read, and the current list of ten member states was not read by name (the news item of 2018 speaks of "all the eight SCO member states"), so only the six founders, of which four have an anchor, are linked.

## What is sourced

"The SCO's history began on 15 June 2001, when the heads of the above six countries adopted a historical decision to establish the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation" in Shanghai. The Charter, "the fundamental document of the organisation", was signed on 7 June 2002 at St Petersburg, entered into force on 19 September 2003 and was registered with the UN Secretariat on 1 February 2013. Its legal framework comprises "over 25 agreements and regulations", among them the Agreement on Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure. Kazakhstan and Tajikistan, founders, have no country anchor.

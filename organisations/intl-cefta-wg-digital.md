# CEFTA Working Group on Digital Cooperation

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #542). Sourced from the Terms of Reference in the Annex to Decision 10/2025, read directly.

## Description

Established by [[INTL-CEFTA-JC-10-2025]] "to support and facilitate the CEFTA Parties' cooperation and exchange of information on digital services, including but not limited to trust services and electronic identification". Members are "representatives/officers from the administration responsible for policy development and/or implementation of digital services", one member and one substitute per Party. It decides by consensus, is chaired by the Party that chairs the Sub-Committee on Trade in Services, meets at least once a year and reports to that Sub-Committee, in an annual report that is part of the Sub-Committee's report to the Joint Committee.

Its functions include supporting "discussions and actions in aligning the rules regarding digital services, including but not limited to trust services, electronic identification, and CEFTA Recognition Frameworks". Under [[INTL-CEFTA-JC-11-2025]] it is to determine the requirements and develop the methodology for the recognition of advanced electronic signatures and seals, with a first draft within one year of that decision entering into force.

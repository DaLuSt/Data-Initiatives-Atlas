## Description

The Publications Office is the EU's official publisher. Throughout this
Atlas it is named as the publisher of every EUR-Lex citation — the Official
Journal texts underpinning the entire `legislation/` folder — which makes it
one of the most frequently relied-upon organisations in the graph while also
being one of the least documented in it.

Its one sourced substantive role here is as a co-initiator of
[[EU-DCAT-AP]], alongside DG CONNECT and the Interoperable Europe
Programme.

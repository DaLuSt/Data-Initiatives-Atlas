## Description

The SKW is Poland's **military counter-intelligence** service, the
inward-facing half of the military pair. Its outward-facing counterpart is
[[PL-SWW]]; both are constituted by [[PL-USKWSWW-2006]].

Functionally it is the Polish equivalent of Germany's [[DE-BAMAD]] and
France's [[FR-DRSD]].

## No longer resting only on secondary description — 2026-09-18

Until this pass, no page published by SKW itself, or by its constituting
Act, had been read directly — this entity and [[PL-SWW]] rested on the
ABW's page about the College for Special Services and an encyclopaedia
entry about the Sejm committee, sources *about* the service rather than
*from* it or its own statute.

`dziennikustaw.gov.pl` — the government's official Journal of Laws
portal, distinct from the CAPTCHA-blocked ISAP — serves the constituting
Act's own scanned text. Article 1 creates SKW in these words: a special
service and state-protection service for threats *"wewnętrznymi dla
obronności Państwa, bezpieczeństwa i zdolności bojowej Sił Zbrojnych
Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej"* (internal to the defence, security and combat
capability of the Polish Armed Forces) and other units subordinate to or
supervised by the Minister of National Defence. Article 3.2 subordinates
the Head of SKW to that Minister; Article 3.3 places the Head's activity
*"pod kontrolą Sejmu"* — under Sejm control, the same parliamentary
oversight layer [[PL-UABWAW-2002]] gives the civilian services.

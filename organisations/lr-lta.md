  Liberia's telecommunications regulator. Section 8 of the Telecommunications Act 2007 establishes 'a public authority called the Liberia Telecommunications Authority', an independent legal entity that regulates telecommunications services and the sector, with the powers to own property, enter contracts and sue. Part III (sections 8 to 11) sets out its Commission, disqualification and removal, and its functions.
  It issues regulations under the Act, among them the Cybersecurity Regulations 2026 (LTA REG 0013 - 2026), whose personal data provisions are described on that regulation's entity. Its own site was not reached, so its current state is taken from those texts.

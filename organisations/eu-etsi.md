## Description

ETSI is the European Standardisation Organisation for information and
communications technology, and produces globally applicable ICT standards.
It is one of the three ESOs recognised by Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012,
alongside [[EU-CEN]] and [[EU-CENELEC]].

Of the three ESOs, ETSI is the most likely to matter to the Atlas's
subject area — ICT standardisation underpins digital identity,
cybersecurity certification and data-exchange standards. **No specific ETSI
standard has been researched or modelled**, so that relevance is asserted in
prose rather than shown in the graph. `coverage: low`, and this is the
clearest place where Batch 9's standards remit was left incomplete.

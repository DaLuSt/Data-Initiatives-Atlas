# MR-DIGITAL (Nordic Council of Ministers for Digitalisation)

> **Created 2026-10-17** (roadmap #541, Northern Europe). Known from the programme it approved; the Council's own pages (norden.org, 403) were not read, so its membership, its predecessor council (MR-DIGITAL was set up in 2017 per search summaries only) and its other declarations are not described here. The links to countries are low-confidence, from the programme's reference to "the Nordic and the Baltic countries".

## What the programme says

It outlines political priorities and objectives for 2025-2030; approved 20 September 2024; the cooperation spans the Nordic and the Baltic countries, working with the other ministerial councils and with civil society and industry; the Nordic Council was involved in developing it. Annual ministerial meetings (a November 2025 declaration at Tampere on the European Digital Identity Wallet is known only from search summaries).

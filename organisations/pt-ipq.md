## Description

IPQ is Portugal's national standardization body, established in **1986**,
managing and developing the **Portuguese Quality System (SPQ)**.

## The first of the CEN group with a sourced ISO edge

The structural-fixes batch created four standards bodies — [[BE-NBN]],
[[FR-AFNOR]], [[ES-UNE]], [[PL-PKN]] — and gave none of them an
[[INTL-ISO]] edge, because CEN-CENELEC's membership rule covers the European
organisations and says nothing about ISO.

IPQ gets one, because **ISO's own member list names it**. That is the
difference between a rule that implies membership and a source that states
it, and it is why this entity carries three edges where those four carry two.

# Organization of the Black Sea Economic Cooperation (BSEC)

> **Created 2026-10-19** (roadmap #543, Eastern Europe's regional bodies). Sourced from the Organization's own site, read directly. The Charter's text, the founding date and the budget and structure pages were not read.

## Description

Thirteen member states: Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bulgaria, Georgia, Greece, Moldova, North Macedonia, Romania, Russia, Serbia, Türkiye and Ukraine. The Organization works through working groups, each supported by a manager in its permanent international secretariat (PERMIS) and coordinated by a member state; the working group on ICT has Türkiye as country coordinator from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2028. Its statutory documents page lists the Charter, an additional protocol on privileges and immunities, a headquarters agreement and rules of procedure, plus agreements and memoranda on combating crime, emergency assistance and transport, none of which was opened.

## What it carries in the Atlas

The ICT working group ([[INTL-BSEC-WG-ICT]]), the economic agenda whose Goal 12 sets its ICT priorities ([[INTL-BSEC-ECONOMIC-AGENDA-2023]]) and the 2018 Baku declaration on ICT cooperation ([[INTL-BSEC-BAKU-DECLARATION-2018]]). Its statistics working group is described on the same site (country coordinators Georgia, then Moldova from 1 July 2027; a coordination centre for statistical data and economic information that was reactivated after years of inactivity) but is not modelled as a separate entity.

# Procurador de los Derechos Humanos (Guatemala)

> **Created 2026-11-17** (scan issue #770: laws with no body). Sourced from articles 46 to 49 and 69 of
> [[GT-LEY-ACCESO-INFO-57-2008]], read directly. `status: unknown`: the office's own site
> (pdh.org.gt) returned HTTP 403, and Decreto 54-86 itself was not read, so neither the office's
> founding date nor its present operation is stated.

## What the Law gives it

| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| 46 | Regulatory authority: protects the right of access under Decreto 54-86 |
| 47 | Exercises its Decreto 54-86 powers (articles 13, 14 and others) in this field |
| 48 | Receives each obligated body's written report on the previous year, by the last working day of January; may ask the reasons for pending, extended, refused and confidential-information cases |
| 49 | May report on requests, response times and appeals in its annual report to Congress |
| 69 | A specific budget line for these tasks |

The Law's Chapter VI on *hábeas data* (articles 30 to 35) binds the obligated bodies; the text
read does not make the Procurator a data protection authority for private controllers.

# Information Technology Industry Development Agency (ITIDA), Egypt

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #471, Egypt). Its English home page was read directly; the 2004 law itself was not.

## Description

ITIDA's own page says the E-Signature Law, issued in 2004, established the agency, and presents its work on the IT industry, export, licensing of information-technology services and e-signature. The law's number is not given on the page read. Per the Ministry's menu it is one of the bodies affiliated to [[EG-MCIT]].

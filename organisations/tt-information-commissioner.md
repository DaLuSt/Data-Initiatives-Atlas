# Office of the Information Commissioner (Trinidad and Tobago)

> **Created 2026-11-22** (scan issue #770: laws with no body). Sourced from Part II (sections 7 to 9)
> of [[TT-DPA-2011]], read directly. `status: unknown`: the Act commences by Presidential
> Proclamation, no proclamation was read, and no page of the office itself was found.
> Search summaries recorded on [[TT-DPA-2011]] (not read from a Legal Notice) say Part II was proclaimed
> and that the office had not been set up; neither is relied on here.

## What the Act gives it

- **Section 7**: a body corporate, the Office of the Information Commissioner.
- **Section 8**: the Commissioner heads it, is appointed by the President for five years (renewable), and must be
  an attorney-at-law of at least ten years' standing with training or experience in economics, finance,
  information security, technology, audit or human resource management.
- **Section 9**: monitoring the administration of the Act; audits and investigations; advice on the privacy
  implications of proposed legislation; orders to stop non-compliant collection or destroy such collections;
  authorising data matching; orders on fees, complaints and appeals; compliance guidelines.

# Instance de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (IPDCP), Togo

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #473, Togo). Known from the law that creates it. Whether the Instance is operating was not established from a primary source; secondary sources report a launch in Lomé in March 2025 and mandatory declarations for video surveillance from July 2025. No site of its own was found.

## Description

An independent administrative authority created by [[TG-LOI-2019-014]], charged with seeing that processing of personal data complies with the law. Its direction committee has fixed-term members and a president named by presidential decree from among them; membership is incompatible with being a member of the Government (Articles 59-61).

# Information Commission (The Gambia)

> **Created 2026-11-22** (scan issue #770: laws with no body). Sourced from Part VIII (sections 38 to 41)
> of the Bill behind [[GM-PDPP-BILL-2024]], read directly. `status: unknown`: neither the Access to
> Information Act 2021 that establishes the Commission nor any page of the Commission was read.

## What the Bill gives it

The Bill does not create a new authority. It adds data protection to an existing one: the Information
Commission of the Access to Information Act 2021 "shall also be responsible for data protection matters"
(38(1)), must act with complete independence (38(2)), must include a commissioner specialised in personal
data protection (39), and gets functions (40) and investigation and enforcement powers (41) on top of its
access-to-information ones.

That pairing, one body for access to information and for data protection, is also how Antigua and Barbuda
is set up: its Data Protection Act 2013 gives the Information Commissioner of the Freedom of Information Act
2004 the data protection role ([[AG-INFORMATION-COMMISSIONER]]).

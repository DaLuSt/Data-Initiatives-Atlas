## Description

AcICT independently advises ministers and parliament on the management
of ICT projects and information systems at ministries, independent
administrative bodies, the police and the judiciary. Ministries must
notify the college of any planned project with an ICT component
exceeding **five million euros**.

## From a temporary bureau to a permanent college

Reading `adviescollegeicttoetsing.nl`'s own article directly: the body
began in **2014** as **Bureau ICT-toetsing (BIT)**, created on the
advice of the parliamentary **Elias Commission**. It was rebranded
**Adviescollege ICT-toetsing in 2021**, and on **1 July 2024** its own
**Instellingswet** (institutional law) took effect, giving it permanent
statutory legal status — converting it from a temporary bureau into a
standing advisory college. The college's own site describes this as
internationally unique: no other country has a comparable independent
body advising government leadership and parliament directly on ICT
management, and it has issued over 120 recommendations across its
operating history.

# Office of the Data Protection Commissioner

> **Verified 2026-10-09.** Section 5 of the Act and the Office's home page were read directly.

## Description

Established by section 5 of [[KE-DPA]]; part of [[KE]] as a State Office. The Act places registration of data controllers and processors with the Data Commissioner (section 18). Its site names its mandate as derived from the Data Protection Act 2019.

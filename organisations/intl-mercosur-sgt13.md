# MERCOSUR Working Subgroup 13 (Electronic Commerce)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #545). From the record of its 37th ordinary meeting (22 May 2026), read directly. Earlier records and its mandate were not read.

## What the record says

Held by videoconference under Paraguay's Pro Tempore Presidency with Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay and Uruguay and Bolivia (under Decision CMC 20/19). Items: (1) incorporation of Resolution GMC 37/06 (Argentina: no incorporation needed under Decision CMC 20/02; Brazil: incorporated by internal acts; Uruguay: not needed; Paraguay: its Law 4017/2010 and Law 6822/2021 already carry its principles) and of the 2021 agreement ([[INTL-MERCOSUR-ECOMMERCE-2021]]); (2) joint work with other forums: a draft Mutual Recognition Agreement on Electronic Identification and Authentication, which Paraguay hopes to sign in the semester, and a rule on internet domain registration that is with the Trade Commission; (3) a reserved Argentine paper on guidelines for negotiating e-commerce with third parties; (4) a seminar on the data economy proposed by Brazil, terms of reference to be resent.

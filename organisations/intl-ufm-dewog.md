# UfM Digital Economy and Internet Access Working Group (DEWoG)

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #542). Sourced from the group's Rules of Procedure (version 1.1 of 10 December 2015) and two Secretariat pages, read directly. Its work plan and observers list were not read; whether it still meets was not found.

## Description

The Declaration of 30 September 2014 ([[INTL-UFM-DIGITAL-2014]]) called for the group "to assist the digital economy Senior Officials in creating synergies among current initiatives, provide input to future actions, exchange information and good practices, including funding possibilities". Its objective, per the Secretariat, is the implementation of the Declaration, "to remove the regional barriers in the digital sector such as: e-trust services including e-signatures, e-health, connectivity for research & education institutions, harmonization of digital economy-related legal frameworks, in particular telecommunications or open data".

The UfM co-Presidencies co-chair it (the EU's represented by DG CONNECT of the European Commission, Jordan's by its Ministry of Information and Communications Technology), the Secretariat acts as its secretariat, and each UfM country nominates one Member and one Substitute. The inaugural meeting was held on 9 and 10 December 2015 in Barcelona, where it agreed by consensus its Rules of Procedure, a Workplan for 2016 and a list of permanent observers.

## A platform of the same name and purpose

The Secretariat now has a page for the "UfM Regional Platform on Digital Economy and Internet Access" whose objective is worded identically ("foreseen by the Ministerial Declaration on Digital Economy (2014)", "gathers representatives of the relevant ministries and agencies"). No page read says the Platform is this group renamed, so the Atlas does not assert it; it is likely.

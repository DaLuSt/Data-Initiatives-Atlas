## Description

Het Waterschapshuis is the coordination and execution body for digital
information provision and communication technology serving all **21**
Dutch water authorities. Reading its own twentieth-anniversary article
directly: **"Rond 2004 groeide het inzicht dat de waterschappen hun
snel veranderende ICT-opgaven niet langer individueel konden
oplossen"** (around 2004 the insight grew that the water authorities
could no longer solve their fast-changing ICT challenges
individually) — by pooling knowledge, systems and capacity, they could
move faster and control costs better. It was founded in **2005**.

## From foundation to joint arrangement

It began as a "stichting" (foundation), later restructured as a
**"gemeenschappelijke regeling"** — a Dutch public-law joint
arrangement — specifically so the 21 water authorities themselves
became its owners and commissioning parties, rather than external
beneficiaries of a foundation they did not control.

## A connector, not a task-taker

Reading its own "About" page directly: Het Waterschapshuis explicitly
**does not** take tasks away from the individual water authorities.
It is described as "vooral een spin in het web" (mainly a spider in
the web) — coordinating joint procurement and contract management of
shared information systems, supporting digital transformation and
organisational change, sharing knowledge and innovation, and
overseeing around eight joint programmes. Its permanent staff is small;
most projects are staffed by seconded water-authority personnel forming
national teams.

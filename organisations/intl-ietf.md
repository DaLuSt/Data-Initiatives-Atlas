## Description

The IETF is the standards organisation for internet protocols. Like
[[INTL-W3C]] and unlike [[INTL-ISO]], [[INTL-IEC]] and [[UN-ITU]], it is a
**direct-membership** organisation rather than one based on national
delegation.

`INTL` scope, not `UN`.

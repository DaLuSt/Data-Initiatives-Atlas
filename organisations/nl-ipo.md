## Description

The IPO is the umbrella organisation of the twelve Dutch provinces. It
brings the provinces together administratively and stimulates cooperation on
topics including data sharing, information security and digital service
delivery, working through an Interprovinciale Digitale Agenda.

Its relevance to the Atlas is that it makes the provincial tier a
participant in government-wide data governance: the IPO ensures provinces
cooperate on digital questions that exceed individual provinces, including
work with central government, municipalities and water authorities on
[[NL-IBDS]], [[NL-FDS]], shared frameworks for information security and
data exchange, and [[NL-NDS]].

Together with [[NL-VNG]] and [[NL-UVW]], the IPO forms the set of
koepelorganisaties representing the decentralised tiers of Dutch government.

# Digital Agency (Japan)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #482, Japan). Read on the Agency's English site (page updated 18 September 2026). The pages read give no founding date or founding law, so neither is recorded.

## What the page says

Mission: human-friendly digitalisation, "no one left behind". Leadership: a Minister for Digital Transformation, a State Minister and a Parliamentary Vice-Minister (new Minister took over on 24 September 2026, from the home page). Organisation (as of 10 December 2025): the Strategy and Organization Group, the Digital Interoperability Framework Group, the Service for Citizens Group and the Information Systems for Government Uses Group, and an Experts Unit. **Competent legal entities:** the Japan Agency for Local Authority Information Systems (J-LIS, which issues the My Number Card and runs the My Number systems), the National Printing Bureau (operations for the public basic information database and related systems) and the Information-technology Promotion Agency (IPA: digital infrastructure, human resources, cybersecurity). The Commission's page names it as responsible for the My Number Act ([[JP-MYNUMBER-ACT]]).

# Direction nationale du renseignement et des enquêtes douanières (DNRED)

## Description

DNRED is France's customs intelligence and investigation service.
Confirmed directly on the DGDDI's own page: its lineage traces to a
Paris-Enquêtes customs division created **25 March 1935**, but its
current legal form dates to the **Arrêté du 29 octobre 2007**, read
directly on Légifrance, which establishes it as a **service à
compétence nationale** reporting to the Director General of Customs
and Indirect Duties (DGDDI). A further arrêté of 8 March 2024 amended
its organisation.

## What it does

Confirmed directly on both sources: DNRED implements the DGDDI's
intelligence, control and anti-fraud policy, gathering and processing
intelligence on **serious tax fraud and money laundering** on behalf of
the tax authority (DGFiP), and combats trafficking in weapons,
narcotics, tobacco and counterfeit goods, economic and financial crime,
and terrorism financing.

## One of six

Confirmed directly on the DGDDI's own DNRED page: DNRED has been one of
the **six "premier cercle" services** of the French intelligence
community (alongside DGSE, DGSI, DRM, DRSD and TRACFIN) since 2008 —
already recorded on [[FR-CNCTR]]'s own file, whose page also states DRM
and [[FR-TRACFIN]], not DNRED, are the two services without access to
the full range of intelligence-gathering techniques the 2015 law
provides; no source read this pass states or implies otherwise for
DNRED.

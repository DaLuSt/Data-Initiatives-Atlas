# BSEC Working Group on Information and Communication Technologies

> **Created 2026-10-19** (roadmap #543). Sourced from the working group's own pages on the Organization's site, read directly. The meeting reports, the statements' texts and the plan's dates were not read in full.

## Description

Türkiye is country coordinator from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2028; a manager of the secretariat is in charge. Terms of reference: increase confidence in ICT among members (information exchange, recommendations, joint R&D proposals); cooperate on harmonising relevant national legislation, broadband networks including satellite, joint projects and e-government, e-business, e-learning and e-healthcare; common action on the "digital divide"; seminars and exchange of know-how; and attracting investment and financial support. Its plan of action for 2024-2025 (the responsible institution named is Armenia's Ministry of High-Tech Industry) lists exchange on digital transformation, digital public infrastructure, blockchain, big data, open data and e-government, government clouds, a BSEC innovation cooperation network, "joint projects on mutual recognition of electronic signature certificates between member states, where feasible", cooperation between computer emergency response teams, and collaboration on artificial intelligence "with a focus on ethical, transparent, and inclusive innovation". Whether any of these produced a decision was not found; a ministerial meeting is marked "TBD".

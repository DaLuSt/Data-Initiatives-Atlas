## Description

ČSÚ is Czechia's national statistical institute and the **eleventh** member
of [[EU-ESS]] in the Atlas.

Every EU member state the Atlas holds is now represented in the European
Statistical System. The three non-member states are not, each for a reason
recorded on its own entity: [[GB-ONS]] reaches [[UN-CES]] instead, and
[[NO-SSB]] and [[CH-BFS]] have no ESS edge because the system is defined as a
partnership with the institutes **of the member states**.

# Overheidsbreed Beleidsoverleg Digitale Overheid (OBDO)

> **Verified 2026-08-20.** Every cited source was read and confirmed to
> support what this entity says. `verification: primary-source`.

## Description

The OBDO is the government-wide policy consultation body for the Dutch
digital government. Its remit is advisory: it advises the responsible state
secretary on digital-government policy that is accessible, reliable, secure,
comprehensible and inclusive; on treating the digital transition as an
integral part of policy development; on the agreements and frameworks to be
applied; on the development of the generic digital infrastructure; and on
aligning sectoral digitalisation ambitions with generic policy.

Its membership spans administrative levels: representatives of ministries,
CIO Rijk, the [[NL-VNG]], the Interprovinciaal Overleg (IPO) and the Unie
van Waterschappen (UvW). The chair is the BZK Deputy Director-General for
Digitalisation and Government Organisations (DGDOO).

Although its formal role is advisory, the OBDO also takes decisions in
practice — it is described as the body that decides on standards proposed by
[[NL-FORUM-STANDAARDISATIE]]. The precise boundary between its advisory and
decision-making functions is unresolved and needs primary sources; two
Staatscourant publications (2018 and 2022) are queued in
`discovery/research-queue.md` as likely instellingsbesluiten.

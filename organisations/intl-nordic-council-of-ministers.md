# Nordic Council of Ministers

> **Created 2026-10-17** (roadmap #541, Northern Europe). Known from one of its own publications, the digitalisation cooperation programme; the Council's own site (norden.org) answers 403 to the agent and to a runner, so its structure, member list and other ministerial councils are not described. No founding date is recorded.

## What the programme says about it

Vision of the Nordic Council of Ministers: "for the Nordic region to become the most sustainable and integrated in the world by 2030". Sector-specific cooperation programmes guide the work of all ministerial councils in 2025-2030, on the three strategic priorities of Vision 2030: a green Nordic region, a competitive Nordic region and a socially sustainable Nordic region. Its Secretary General is Karen Ellemann (foreword). Digitalisation is cross-sectoral and works with the other councils (Environment and Climate, Growth, Energy, Health, Education and Research, Natural Resources, Culture, and the Freedom of Movement Council).

## Description

BZK is the Dutch ministry within which responsibility for the digital
government is coordinated. In the entities covered by Batch 1 it recurs in
three roles: coordinating policy owner for the digital-government agenda,
policy commissioner of [[NL-LOGIUS]], and the ministry under which the MIDO
bureau sits organisationally.

## Relationships

- Policy commissioner of [[NL-LOGIUS]], which operates GDI services.
- The [[NL-OBDO]] is chaired by BZK's Deputy Director-General for
  Digitalisation and Government Organisations (DGDOO).
- Co-founder (with [[NL-VNG]]) of [[NL-ICTU]].
- Coordinating ministry for [[NL-DIGIBETER]] and, through it,
  [[NL-DATA-AGENDA-OVERHEID]].

## Atlas interpretation

BZK is included here as the institutional centre of gravity for Dutch
digital-government policy, rather than as a general profile of the ministry.
Its non-data responsibilities are out of scope for the Atlas.

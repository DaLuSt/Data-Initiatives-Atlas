# TRACFIN (Traitement du renseignement et action contre les circuits financiers clandestins)

## Description

TRACFIN is France's financial intelligence unit. Confirmed directly on
Légifrance's own text: created as a coordination cell by the **Décret du
9 mai 1990**, within the **Ministry of Economy, Finance and Budget**, to
**"recueillir, de traiter et de diffuser le renseignement sur les
circuits financiers clandestins et le blanchiment de l'argent"** — gather,
process and disseminate intelligence on clandestine financial circuits
and money laundering — and to coordinate investigative resources
nationally and internationally.

## From coordination cell to national service

Confirmed directly on Légifrance's own text of **Décret n° 2006-1541 du
6 décembre 2006**: TRACFIN was elevated to a **service à compétence
nationale**, reporting to both the **Minister of Economy** and the
**Minister of Budget**, tasked with receiving and processing
suspicious-transaction declarations, and restricted to security-cleared
civil servants only.

## One of six, sharing DRM's limitation

Confirmed directly on [[FR-CNCTR]]'s own services page: TRACFIN is one
of the **six "premier cercle" services** of the French intelligence
community (alongside DGSE, DGSI, DRM, DRSD and DNRED) since 2008. The
same page states TRACFIN and [[FR-DRM]] are the **two services that do
not have access to the full range** of intelligence-gathering techniques
the law of 24 July 2015 provides — already recorded on [[FR-DRM]]'s own
file, confirmed here from TRACFIN's side.

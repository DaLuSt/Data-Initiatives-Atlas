## Description

The European Skills Agenda is a European Commission **five-year action
plan**, **published 1 July 2020**, "to help individuals and businesses
develop more and better skills and to put them to use, by strengthening
sustainable competitiveness, ensuring social fairness" — confirmed
directly on `pact-for-skills.ec.europa.eu`'s own resource page. A second
Commission page, `employment-social-affairs.ec.europa.eu`, independently
confirms the same date and describes it as "a five year action plan with
**12 actions**."

It set quantitative upskilling/reskilling targets for the following five
years, including raising adult (25-64) participation in learning to
**50%**. It was adopted alongside a proposed Council Recommendation on
vocational education and training and a Communication and proposed
Council Recommendation supporting youth employment.

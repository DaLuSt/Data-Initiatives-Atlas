## Description

The Transition Pathway for Tourism is a European Commission strategic
framework, **published 4 February 2022**, **co-created with industry,
public authorities, social partners and other stakeholders** — confirmed
directly on transport.ec.europa.eu's own page. It identifies **27 areas
of measures**, confirmed independently on the Commission's own
announcement page, across four domains: the **green transition** of
tourism, the **digital transition** of tourism, the **resilience** of EU
tourism, and **skills** for the tourism workforce.

Implementation is tracked through an open **call for commitments**, in
which stakeholders pledge support for specific measures throughout the
Pathway's rollout.

## What is sourced

"HIPCAR was designed to support the Caribbean countries in improving their competitiveness by harmonizing approaches to ICT development." The launching meeting took place on 15-16 December 2008 in Grenada, with 44 persons from seven countries and 16 regional organisations. Stage I produced assessments of existing legislation and model policy guidelines and legislative texts; Stage II offered technical assistance to countries transposing them. The page lists assessment reports on e-transactions, e-evidence, access to public information, privacy and data protection, cybercrimes and interception of communications, and in-country assistance pages for eight countries. Only the privacy model is modelled: [[INTL-HIPCAR-PRIVACY-MODEL-2013]]. The other model texts were not read.

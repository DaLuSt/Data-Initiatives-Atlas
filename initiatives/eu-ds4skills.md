# DS4Skills

> **Created 2026-09-05**, closing part of the gap [[EU-SKILLS-DATA-SPACE]]
> itself had flagged: named deployment projects were entirely absent, and
> ESCO/Europass/the European Skills Agenda were presumed connected without
> a source. This entity supplies the first; the second is now checked and
> found negative rather than merely unresearched.

## Description

Confirmed by reading digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu's own "Common European
data spaces" page directly: under its rollout listing for the Skills data
space, exactly two projects are named — **DS4Skills** and **EDGE-Skills**
— with no further text. DS4Skills' own site describes itself as "a common
European Data Space for Skills that connects key stakeholders across
Europe to enhance digital skills, improve employment opportunities."

HaDEA's own announcement, read directly, calls it **"Europe's first Data
Space for Skills,"** confirms it was **"officially launched in January
2025,"** is **"co-funded by the European Commission"** and **"coordinated
by DIGITALEUROPE"** with **"22 partners."**

## ESCO, Europass and the European Skills Agenda — checked, not confirmed

[[EU-SKILLS-DATA-SPACE]]'s own text presumed this data space "builds on"
ESCO, Europass and the European Skills Agenda. Neither the HaDEA
announcement nor the DS4Skills site mentions any of the three. This is
recorded as a checked-and-negative finding, not left as an unresearched
presumption: on the sources read, DS4Skills is not stated to build on any
of them.

## Not modelled

- **EDGE-Skills**, the other project named on the same Commission page
  alongside DS4Skills.
- DS4Skills' **22 partner organisations**, not individually named on the
  pages read.
- The project's **predecessor**, referenced on ds4skills.eu ("picks up the
  work of its predecessor and proudly carries its name forward") but not
  identified by name or date.

## Description

SYNERGIES is a **Horizon Europe Innovation Action**, confirmed via its
official CORDIS record (Grant Agreement **101069839**): coordinated by
**TXT e-tech S.r.l.** (Italy), with **25 partners** across **Italy,
Greece, Spain, Cyprus, Ireland, the Netherlands, Finland, Denmark, Belgium
and Romania**. Total cost **€10,180,687.51**, EU contribution
**€7,972,950.00**, running **1 September 2022 – 28 February 2026**.

CORDIS states its objective as a "reference Energy data Space
implementation" integrating information from diverse stakeholders —
particularly consumers as data owners — alongside the building and
mobility sectors, enabling data-driven energy services built on consumer
flexibility and establishing "a sustainable economy around shared energy
data" in "a secure, trustful, fair and acceptable manner."

## One of six named CEEDS-supporting projects

See [[EU-OMEGA-X]] for the full finding: Int:net's own coordination-site
page names SYNERGIES as one of five Horizon Europe Innovation Actions
(alongside DATA CELLAR, EDDIE, Enershare and Omega-X) supporting CEEDS
deployment, plus Int:net itself as the sixth, a Coordination and Support
Action. **All five Innovation Actions are now modelled; only Int:net, the
Coordination and Support Action, remains.**

## Not modelled

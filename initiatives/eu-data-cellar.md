## Description

DATA CELLAR is a **Horizon Europe Innovation Action**, confirmed via its
official CORDIS record (Grant Agreement **101069694**): coordinated by
**RINA Consulting S.p.A.** (Italy), with **around 30 partners** across
**Italy, Greece, Spain, Belgium, Norway, France, Portugal, Bulgaria,
Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands, Cyprus, Austria, Switzerland and the
United Kingdom**. Total cost **€8,530,793.93**, EU contribution
**€6,954,062.50**, running **1 June 2022 – 30 November 2025**.

CORDIS states its objective as creating "a public energy dataspace that
will support the creation, development and management of LECs [Local
Energy Communities] in EU" — an "interoperable, modular, and secure
energy data space" delivering datasets, a Decision Support Tool and AI
models, while facilitating integration with other EU energy data
initiatives.

## One of six named CEEDS-supporting projects

See [[EU-OMEGA-X]] for the full finding: Int:net's own coordination-site
page names DATA CELLAR as one of five Horizon Europe Innovation Actions
(alongside EDDIE, Enershare, Omega-X and SYNERGIES) supporting CEEDS
deployment, plus Int:net itself as the sixth, a Coordination and Support
Action.

## Not modelled

- **EDDIE, SYNERGIES** and **Int:net** — the remaining named projects.
- DATA CELLAR's own **Local Energy Community pilot sites**, named only
  categorically on the CORDIS record, not itemised.

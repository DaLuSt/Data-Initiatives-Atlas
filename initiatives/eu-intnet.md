## Description

Int:net (IntNET) is a **Horizon Europe Coordination and Support Action**,
confirmed via its official CORDIS record (Grant Agreement **101070086**):
coordinated by the **Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft** (Germany), with **11
participants** across **Germany, Austria, Ireland, Spain, Belgium, Italy
and France**. Total cost and EU contribution both **€4,995,541.50** (a CSA
is funded at 100% of costs, unlike the five Innovation Actions above),
running **1 May 2022 – 31 October 2025**.

CORDIS states its objective as bringing together European energy-sector
stakeholders to develop, test and implement interoperable energy
services: establishing a cross-domain community that persists beyond the
project, an assessment methodology and maturity model for
interoperability, standardised testing procedures, and a quality
certification for compliant smart grid products.

## The source for the "six named projects" finding, and one of the six itself

Int:net's own `community.intnet.eu` coordination-site page is the direct
source [[EU-OMEGA-X]], [[EU-ENERSHARE]], [[EU-DATA-CELLAR]], [[EU-EDDIE]]
and [[EU-SYNERGIES]] all cite for being named as CEEDS-supporting Horizon

as the sixth: "one Coordination and Support Action (Int:net)." This
closes `discovery/unresolved.md` row #132 completely — all six named

## Description

UN 2.0 is the Secretary-General's vision for a rejuvenated United Nations
system, issued as a policy brief in **September 2023**, intended to equip
the organisation to support member states in the twenty-first century and
accelerate progress on the Sustainable Development Goals.

Its core is the **quintet of change** — five areas of modern expertise the
UN aims to strengthen:

| Capability | Framing |
|---|---|
| **Data** | Building impactful data ecosystems |
| Innovation | Scaling new solutions |
| **Digital** | Digital impact |
| Foresight | Navigating uncertainty |
| Behavioural science | Enabling better choices |

Two of the five — data and digital — are squarely within this Atlas's
scope; the other three are noted for completeness but not pursued.

`start_date: 2023-09-01` is a **placeholder for "September 2023"**, the same
convention flagged as a schema problem in `discovery/unresolved.md`: a
first-of-month date is indistinguishable from a real one.

Note the quintet PDF sits under a `2021/09` path while the policy brief is
dated September 2023, which suggests the quintet framing predates the brief
or the file was re-filed. Unresolved.

## Description

UN 2.0 is the Secretary-General's vision for a rejuvenated United Nations
system, issued as a policy brief in **September 2023**, intended to equip
the organisation to support member states in the twenty-first century and
accelerate progress on the Sustainable Development Goals.

Its core is the **quintet of change** — five areas of modern expertise the
UN aims to strengthen:

| Capability | Framing |
|---|---|
| **Data** | Building impactful data ecosystems |
| Innovation | Scaling new solutions |
| **Digital** | Digital impact |
| Foresight | Navigating uncertainty |
| Behavioural science | Enabling better choices |

Two of the five — data and digital — are squarely within this Atlas's
scope; the other three are noted for completeness but not pursued.

`start_date: 2023-09-01` is a **placeholder for "September 2023"**, the same
convention flagged as a schema problem in `discovery/unresolved.md`: a
first-of-month date is indistinguishable from a real one. It stays a
placeholder deliberately: the policy brief is confirmed to a month (September
2023) but not to a specific day, and the date-fabrication discipline this
batch applies (see `international/un/un.md`) says leave the precision at
what the source actually gives rather than pad it.

**The `2021/09` file-path oddity, resolved.** The previously-flagged
question — why the quintet PDF sits under a `2021/09` path while the policy
brief is dated September 2023 — is closed. Un.org's *current* URL for the
policy brief is `un.org/two-zero/sites/default/files/2023-09/un-2.0_policy-
brief_en.pdf` (found via WebSearch, confirmed to exist), correctly dated to
September 2023 and titled "Our Common Agenda Policy Brief 11: UN 2.0." The
originally-cited `2021/09` quintet PDF is a different, older file on a
different path — not evidence that the quintet framing predates the brief,
just a stale or secondary URL. No relationship or date changes as a result;
this closes a documentation question rather than a modelling one.

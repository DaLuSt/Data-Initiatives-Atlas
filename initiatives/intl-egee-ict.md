## What is sourced

"A four (4) year programme that aims at supporting the effective review and/or development of various regional policy and regulatory framework in a harmonized manner that will contribute to enhancing competition, improved access to cost effective and secure ICT services." Implemented in five RECs: COMESA (lead), the East African Community, the Intergovernmental Authority on Development, the Indian Ocean Commission and SADC. Planned work: capacity building; model policy and regulatory frameworks for cross-border interconnection, competition, trade facilitation, e-commerce and gender perspectives; licensing and universal access policies. The EAC, IGAD and IOC have no entity of their own in the Atlas yet.

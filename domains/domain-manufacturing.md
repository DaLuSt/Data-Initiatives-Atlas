# Manufacturing

## Description

Classification node for manufacturing and industry. Created 2026-09-25 at
the user's request to add domains reflecting the sector list in the Dutch
Cyberbeveiligingswet (Cbw) — "Vervaardiging" is one of the seven
important-entity sectors in the Cbw's own Bijlage 2, confirmed by reading
`ncsc.nl`'s "Valt mijn organisatie onder de Cyberbeveiligingswet (NIS2)?"
page directly (2026-09-25), which lists the same seven sectors as
Directive (EU) 2022/2555's (NIS2) Annex II.

Like [[DOMAIN-GOVERNMENT]], this is a taxonomy node rather than a
researched entity: it carries no factual claims and therefore no sources.
The sourced claim that Manufacturing is an important sector lives on
[[NL-CBW]] and [[EU-NIS2]], not here.

## Relationships

Used by [[NL-CBW]] and [[EU-NIS2]] (important-sector scope), and by the
Atlas's industrial data-ecosystem cluster: [[EU-MANUFACTURING-DATA-SPACE]]
(one of the fourteen common European data spaces), [[DE-PLATTFORM-INDUSTRIE-4-0]]
(the German government-industry platform underlying the Manufacturing-X
family), [[DE-MANUFACTURING-X]] (the cross-sector initiative it feeds),
[[DE-CATENA-X]] (automotive), [[DE-FACTORY-X]] (mechanical and industrial
engineering), [[DE-CONSTRUCT-X]] (construction) and [[DE-AEROSPACE-X]]
(aerospace supply chains) — the last filed here rather than under
[[DOMAIN-SPACE]], since it covers manufacturing supply chains rather than
space operations.

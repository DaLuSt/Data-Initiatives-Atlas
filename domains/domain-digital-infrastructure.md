# Digital Infrastructure

## Description

Classification node for digital and ICT infrastructure. Created
2026-09-25 at the user's request to add domains reflecting the sector
list in the Dutch Cyberbeveiligingswet (Cbw) — "Digitale infrastructuur"
and "Beheer van ICT-diensten" are two of the eleven essential-entity
sectors in the Cbw's own Bijlage 1, and "Digitale aanbieders" is one of
the seven important-entity sectors in Bijlage 2, confirmed by reading
`ncsc.nl`'s "Valt mijn organisatie onder de Cyberbeveiligingswet (NIS2)?"
page directly (2026-09-25), which lists the same sectors as Directive (EU)
2022/2555's (NIS2) Annexes I and II. The Atlas groups all three under one
domain, since no Atlas entity yet distinguishes network/cloud
infrastructure from managed ICT services or digital-platform providers.

This is distinct from [[DOMAIN-CYBERSECURITY]], which classifies the legal
obligations, authorities and baselines governing network and information
system security — not the infrastructure and providers those obligations
apply to.

Like [[DOMAIN-GOVERNMENT]], this is a taxonomy node rather than a
researched entity: it carries no factual claims and therefore no sources.
The sourced claim that these are essential/important sectors lives on
[[NL-CBW]] and [[EU-NIS2]], not here.

## Relationships

Used by [[NL-CBW]] and [[EU-NIS2]] (essential/important-sector scope), and
by [[LU-ILR]], Luxembourg's multi-sector regulator, which regulates
electronic communications alongside its other sectors.

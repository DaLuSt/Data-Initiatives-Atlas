# Postal and Courier Services

## Description

Classification node for postal and courier services. Created 2026-09-25
at the user's request to add domains reflecting the sector list in the
Dutch Cyberbeveiligingswet (Cbw) — "Post- en koeriersdiensten" is one of
the seven important-entity sectors in the Cbw's own Bijlage 2, confirmed
by reading `ncsc.nl`'s "Valt mijn organisatie onder de
Cyberbeveiligingswet (NIS2)?" page directly (2026-09-25), which lists the
same seven sectors as Directive (EU) 2022/2555's (NIS2) Annex II.

Like [[DOMAIN-GOVERNMENT]], this is a taxonomy node rather than a
researched entity: it carries no factual claims and therefore no sources.
The sourced claim that Postal and courier services is an important sector
lives on [[NL-CBW]] and [[EU-NIS2]], not here.

## Relationships

Used by [[NL-CBW]] and [[EU-NIS2]] (important-sector scope), and by
[[LU-ILR]], Luxembourg's multi-sector regulator, which regulates postal
services alongside its other sectors.

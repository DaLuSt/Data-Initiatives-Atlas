# Energy

## Description

Classification node for the energy sector. Created 2026-09-25 at the
user's request to add domains reflecting the sector list in the Dutch
Cyberbeveiligingswet (Cbw) — "Energie" is the first of the eleven
essential-entity sectors in the Cbw's own Bijlage 1, confirmed by reading
`ncsc.nl`'s "Valt mijn organisatie onder de Cyberbeveiligingswet (NIS2)?"
page directly (2026-09-25), which lists the same eleven sectors as
Directive (EU) 2022/2555's (NIS2) Annex I.

Like [[DOMAIN-GOVERNMENT]], this is a taxonomy node rather than a
researched entity: it carries no factual claims and therefore no sources.
The sourced claim that Energy is an essential sector lives on [[NL-CBW]]
and [[EU-NIS2]], not here.

## Relationships

Used by [[NL-CBW]] and [[EU-NIS2]] (essential-sector scope), and by three
energy data-space initiatives: [[EU-CEEDS]] (the Common European Energy
Data Space), [[EU-ENERSHARE]] (a Horizon Europe project supporting its
deployment), and [[DE-ENDA]] (Germany's Gaia-X/IDSA reference architecture
for an energy data space). Also used by [[LU-ILR]], Luxembourg's
multi-sector regulator, which regulates electricity and gas alongside its
other sectors.

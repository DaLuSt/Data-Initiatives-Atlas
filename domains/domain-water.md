# Water

## Description

Classification node for drinking-water supply and waste-water management.
Created 2026-09-25 at the user's request to add domains reflecting the
sector list in the Dutch Cyberbeveiligingswet (Cbw) — "Drinkwater" and
"Afvalwater" are two of the eleven essential-entity sectors in the Cbw's
own Bijlage 1, confirmed by reading `ncsc.nl`'s "Valt mijn organisatie
onder de Cyberbeveiligingswet (NIS2)?" page directly (2026-09-25), which
lists the same sectors as Directive (EU) 2022/2555's (NIS2) Annex I. The
Atlas uses one domain for both, since no Atlas entity yet needs the
drinking-water/waste-water distinction drawn separately.

Like [[DOMAIN-GOVERNMENT]], this is a taxonomy node rather than a
researched entity: it carries no factual claims and therefore no sources.
The sourced claim that Drinking water and Waste water are essential
sectors lives on [[NL-CBW]] and [[EU-NIS2]], not here.

## Relationships

Used by [[NL-CBW]] and [[EU-NIS2]] (essential-sector scope). No other
Atlas entity is tagged to this domain yet — the Atlas holds no dedicated
water-utility or water-data entity.

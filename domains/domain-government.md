# Government

## Description

The Government domain classifies entities concerned with public
administration and digital government — as distinct from sectoral domains
such as Health, Mobility or Energy, which the Atlas treats separately.

This is a classification node rather than a researched entity: it exists to
give the Batch 1 Netherlands entities a shared domain to hang from, and to
be the first anchor of the domain taxonomy described in
`metadata/taxonomy.md` §1. It carries no factual claims and therefore no
sources; `verification: primary-source` reflects that there is nothing here
requiring external verification.

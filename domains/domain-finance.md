# Finance

## Description

Classification node for banking and financial-market infrastructure.
Created 2026-09-25 at the user's request to add domains reflecting the
sector list in the Dutch Cyberbeveiligingswet (Cbw) — "Bankwezen" and
"Infrastructuur financiële markt" are two of the eleven essential-entity
sectors in the Cbw's own Bijlage 1, confirmed by reading `ncsc.nl`'s "Valt
mijn organisatie onder de Cyberbeveiligingswet (NIS2)?" page directly
(2026-09-25), which lists the same sectors as Directive (EU) 2022/2555's
(NIS2) Annex I. The Atlas uses one domain for both, since no Atlas entity
yet needs the banking/market-infrastructure distinction drawn separately.

Like [[DOMAIN-GOVERNMENT]], this is a taxonomy node rather than a
researched entity: it carries no factual claims and therefore no sources.
The sourced claim that Banking and Financial market infrastructure are
essential sectors lives on [[NL-CBW]] and [[EU-NIS2]], not here.

## Relationships

Used by [[NL-CBW]] and [[EU-NIS2]] (essential-sector scope), and by
[[EU-FINANCIAL-DATA-SPACE]], one of the fourteen common European data
spaces, covering finance.

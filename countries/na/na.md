# Namibia

## Description

Namibia (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`NA`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Communications Regulatory Authority ([[NA-CRAN]]), the Communications Act (No. 8 of 2009) that created it ([[NA-COMMUNICATIONS-ACT-2009]]), the Communications Amendment Act (No. 6 of 2020) ([[NA-COMMUNICATIONS-AMENDMENT-ACT-2020]]) and its ICT data portal ([[NA-CRAN-DATA-PORTAL]]), all from the Authority's own site; no Act's text was opened. No Namibian data protection law is in the Atlas: none was found on a readable page.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: the data protection bill, the cyber security bill and the NAM-CSIRT, among others, are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

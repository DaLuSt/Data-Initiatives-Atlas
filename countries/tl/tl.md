# Timor-Leste

## Description

Timor-Leste (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`TL`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the government's ICT and e-government agency ([[TL-TIC]]) and the ombudsman ([[TL-PDHJ]]). **No data-protection act is recorded because none is in force on any source read**: the agency's page of 20 March 2025 lists a Data Protection and Privacy Law and a Cybercrime Legislation among the areas still to be formalised, and the ombudsman's page of 15 January 2026 calls for both.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

# United States of America

## Description

United States of America (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`US`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Foundations for Evidence-Based Policymaking Act of 2018 ([[US-EVIDENCE-ACT-2018]], which includes the OPEN Government Data Act), the E-Government Act of 2002 ([[US-E-GOV-ACT-2002]]) and the federal open data portal ([[US-DATA-GOV]]), read as enrolled public laws on govinfo.gov and on Data.gov's own page.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). There is no federal data protection authority or general privacy statute among the entities: the Privacy Act of 1974 and its Office of Management and Budget guidance, the sectoral privacy laws, the state laws, the Federal Trade Commission, NIST and the chief data officers' council were not read. The page for the Privacy Act on govinfo.gov was not found at the address tried. It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

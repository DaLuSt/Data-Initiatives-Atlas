# Cameroon

## Description

Cameroon (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`CM`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the National Agency for Information and Communication Technologies ([[CM-ANTIC]]) and two laws named on the Agency's own pages: the cybersecurity and cybercrime law of 2010 ([[CM-LOI-2010-012]]) and the 2023 charter on the protection of children online ([[CM-LOI-2023-009]]).

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). The laws' own texts, the electronic communications law (2010/013), the telecommunications regulator, and any personal data protection law were not read: the Agency's list of sector texts, as read, names no general data protection law. It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

# Eswatini

## Description

Eswatini (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`SZ`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the telecommunications regulator ([[SZ-ESCCOM]]) and the law the Data Protection Act, 2022 ([[SZ-DPA-2022]]), from the regulator's own site; the law's text was not opened. The Commission also runs the national computer incident response team ([[SZ-SZCIRT]]).

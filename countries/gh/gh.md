# Ghana

## Description

Ghana (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`GH`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the National Information Technology Agency ([[GH-NITA]]), the Act that established it ([[GH-ACT-771]]), its Digital Public Infrastructure initiative ([[GH-DPI]]) and its Government CERT ([[GH-GOVCERT]]), all from the Agency's own site.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). Ghana's data protection law (reported as Act 843 of 2012) and the Data Protection Commission were not read: the Commission's sites did not answer. The Act 771 text, the Ghana Card law and the cybersecurity law were not read either. It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

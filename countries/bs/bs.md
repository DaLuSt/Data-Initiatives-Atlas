# Bahamas

## Description

The Bahamas (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`BS`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Utilities Regulation and Competition Authority ([[BS-URCA]]) and the two 2009 Acts that created the electronic communications regime: the URCA Act ([[BS-URCA-ACT-2009]]) and the Communications Act ([[BS-COMMUNICATIONS-ACT-2009]]), from the Authority's own governance page. The Data Protection (Privacy of Personal Information) Act and its commissioner are not here: no readable page was found (`bahamas.gov.bs` answers 403).

## What Czechia added to the Atlas

**A general act on how the state manages its own data.**

The Atlas holds a great deal of law *about* data — data protection acts, open
data transpositions, cyber security acts. It has almost none about **how a
state manages its own data and lets others reach it**.

[[CZ-ZAKON-60-2026]] does exactly that, and names [[CZ-DIA]] as the **single
information point** and the node connecting Czech data sources to the
European data portal.

The nearest comparators are Dutch and are not statutes: [[NL-IBDS]] is a
strategy and [[NL-FDS]] a system. **The Netherlands built the arrangement;
Czechia legislated it.**

## A standards split no other country here has

[[CZ-UNMZ]] holds the **formal membership** at ISO, IEC, CEN and CENELEC and
the legal responsibility on behalf of the state. **ČAS** — Česká agentura pro
standardizaci — performs the **operational work**: technical committees,
drafting, publication.

Everywhere else the member and the drafter are one body. The
`participates-in` edges are asserted on ÚNMZ, because that is what
membership means — and **ČAS is not modelled**, which understates Czech
standardisation, since the body that actually produces ČSN standards is
absent from the graph.

## Not modelled

- **ČAS**, as above.
- Czechia's **cyber security act** (No 181/2014) and its NIS2 successor, so
  [[CZ-NUKIB]] has no act beside it.
- **Act No 12/2020** on the right to digital services, [[CZ-DIA]]'s
  constituting statute.
- The **NCKB** as a separate body — the sources present it as a section of
  NÚKIB, not an entity.
- **Portál občana** and Czech electronic identification.
- The **regions and municipalities**.

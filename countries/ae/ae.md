# United Arab Emirates

## Description

United Arab Emirates (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`AE`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the federal personal-data law ([[AE-PDPL-45-2021]]), the decree-law that set up the regulator ([[AE-DECREE-LAW-44-2021]]) and the UAE Data Office ([[AE-DATA-OFFICE]]), all from the UAE Government's portal. Free-zone regimes (DIFC, ADGM) and Emirate-level laws such as the Dubai Data Law are not recorded.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

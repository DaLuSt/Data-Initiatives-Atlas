# Peru

## Description

Peru (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`PE`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the personal data protection law ([[PE-LEY-29733]]), its 2024 regulation ([[PE-DS-016-2024-JUS]]), the National Authority for Personal Data Protection ([[PE-ANPD]]), the Digital Government Law ([[PE-DL-1412]]) and the digital government governing body ([[PE-SGD]]), read on gob.pe and in the texts of the two laws.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). The 2024 regulation's own text, the regulation of the Digital Government Law (Decreto Supremo 029-2021-PCM), the National Digital Security Centre and the Peruvian open data portal were not read. It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

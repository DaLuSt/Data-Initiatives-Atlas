# Thailand

## Description

Thailand (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`TH`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the telecommunications regulator ([[TH-ETDA]]) and the law the Electronic Transactions Act B.E. 2544 (A.D. 2001) (Revised Edition) ([[TH-ETA-2001]]), from the regulator's own site; the law's text was not opened. The Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 and its committee are not here: `pdpc.or.th` answers 403 to the agent and the fetch tool.

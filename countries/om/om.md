# Oman

## Description

Oman (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`OM`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Telecommunications Regulatory Authority ([[OM-TRA]]) and the telecommunications regulation law that established it, Royal Decree 30/2002 ([[OM-TELECOM-LAW-2002]]), from the Authority's own Arabic pages (its English pages need JavaScript and show nothing to the agent); neither text was opened. The personal data protection law and the national cybersecurity bodies are not here.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: the personal data protection law, the electronic transactions law and the ministry responsible for digital affairs, among others, are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

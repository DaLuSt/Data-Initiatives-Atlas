# Kazakhstan

## Description

Kazakhstan (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`KZ`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Ministry of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Development ([[KZ-MAIDD]]), the state body that, per its own page, leads and coordinates state policy on informatisation, e-government, personal data and its protection, information security, communications, spatial data and data management; the page is in Kazakh and its documents were not opened. No data protection law is in the Atlas: the legal databases (`adilet.zan.kz`) are JavaScript pages.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: the Law on Personal Data and its Protection, the digital code and the national statistics bureau, among others, are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

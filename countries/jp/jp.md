# Japan

## Description

Japan (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`JP`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Personal Information Protection Commission ([[JP-PPC]]), the Act on the Protection of Personal Information ([[JP-APPI]]) and the reform policy decided in January 2026 ([[JP-APPI-TRIENNIAL-REVIEW-2026]]), the Digital Agency ([[JP-DIGITAL-AGENCY]]) and the My Number Act ([[JP-MYNUMBER-ACT]]), all read on the Commission's and the Agency's own English sites; the Acts' texts were not read.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). The Acts' own texts (the translations on e-Gov and the Commission's site), the Basic Act on the Formation of a Digital Society, the Digital Agency's founding law and strategies, and the sectoral data laws were not read. It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

# Mongolia

## Description

Mongolia (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`MN`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Communications Regulatory Commission ([[MN-CRC]]) and three laws from the Commission's own list of the laws of Mongolia, with the English titles it gives: on the protection of personal information ([[MN-LAW-PERSONAL-INFORMATION-2021]]), on electronic signatures ([[MN-LAW-ELECTRONIC-SIGNATURES-2011]]) and on communications ([[MN-LAW-COMMUNICATIONS-2001]]). No law's text was opened (the links go to `legalinfo.mn`, which does not answer the agent).

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: the cyber security law, the ministry responsible for digital development and the national statistics office, among others, are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

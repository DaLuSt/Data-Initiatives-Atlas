## Description

Bolivia (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`BO`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the e-government agency AGETIC ([[BO-AGETIC]]), the decree that created the state's digital platform gob.bo ([[BO-DS-5340]]) and the Agenda Digital 2030 ([[BO-AGENDA-DIGITAL-2030]]), all read on AGETIC's own site.

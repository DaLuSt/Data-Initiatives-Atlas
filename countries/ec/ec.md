# Ecuador

## Description

Ecuador (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`EC`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Organic Law on Personal Data Protection ([[EC-LOPDP]]), read in full as published in the Registro Oficial, and the Superintendency of Personal Data Protection ([[EC-SPDP]]), read on its own site.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). The law's general regulation, the Superintendency's own resolutions, the Ministry of Telecommunications and the national digital government policy were not read. It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

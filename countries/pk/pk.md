# Pakistan

## Description

Pakistan (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`PK`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Ministry of Information Technology and Telecommunication ([[PK-MOITT]]), the Pakistan Digital Authority ([[PK-PDA]]) and the draft Data Governance Policy of June 2026 ([[PK-DATA-GOVERNANCE-POLICY-2026]]), all from the Ministry's own site.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). The Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025, which the draft policy names as PDA's source of powers, was not read, and no personal data protection law has been enacted according to the draft policy itself. It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

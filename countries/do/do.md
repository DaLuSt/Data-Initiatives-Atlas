# Dominican Republic

## Description

The Dominican Republic (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`DO`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the telecommunications regulator ([[DO-INDOTEL]]), the General Telecommunications Law ([[DO-LEY-153-98]]), and two laws that the regulator's own Marco Legal page lists as affecting its functions: the personal data protection law ([[DO-LEY-172-13]]) and the law on high-technology crimes ([[DO-LEY-53-07]]). None of the laws' texts was read.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: the e-commerce and digital signature law, the national data protection supervisor (if any) and the government's digital agencies are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

# Papua New Guinea

## Description

Papua New Guinea (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`PG`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Department of Information and Communications Technology ([[PG-DICT]]), the Digital Government Act 2022 ([[PG-DIGITAL-GOVERNMENT-ACT-2022]]), the Digital Transformation Policy ([[PG-DIGITAL-TRANSFORMATION-POLICY]]), the Digital Government Plan 2023-2027 ([[PG-DIGITAL-GOVERNMENT-PLAN-2023-2027]]) and the National Data Governance and Protection Policy ([[PG-DATA-GOVERNANCE-POLICY]]), all from the Department's own site; no document's text was opened.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: the Government Cloud framework, the cyber security and cybercrime laws and the national statistics office, among others, are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model). The Department's site carries unrelated gambling links in its footer, which suggests it has been tampered with; the Atlas relies only on the Department's own text.

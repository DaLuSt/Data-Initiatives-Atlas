# Guatemala

## Description

Guatemala (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`GT`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the law Decreto 57-2008, Ley de Acceso a la Información Pública ([[GT-LEY-ACCESO-INFO-57-2008]]), whose Chapter VI ('Hábeas data') is the data protection regime this pass found, and the General Telecommunications Law ([[GT-LEY-GENERAL-TELECOM-94-96]]). Its text was read directly (a text-layer PDF). No general data protection law was found: search results describe bills as pending for years.

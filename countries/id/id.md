# Indonesia

## Description

Indonesia (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`ID`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Ministry of Communication and Digital ([[ID-KOMDIGI]]) and its 2016 Regulation on Personal Data Protection in Electronic Systems ([[ID-PERMEN-KOMINFO-20-2016]]), both from the ministry's legal documentation site (JDIH), which publishes the regulation's record and text. Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection and any authority it provides for are not here: the ministry's list does not carry it and no readable official page for it was found.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: Law No. 27 of 2022, the Electronic Information and Transactions Law and the national cyber and crypto agency, among others, are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

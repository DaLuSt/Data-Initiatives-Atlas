# Bangladesh

## Description

Bangladesh (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`BD`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the ICT Division ([[BD-ICT-DIVISION]]) and four 2026 Acts from the Division's own list of laws and rules (in Bengali): the Personal Data Protection Act ([[BD-PDPA-2026]]), the National Data Management Act ([[BD-NDMA-2026]]), the Cyber Protection Act ([[BD-CPA-2026]]) and its Amendment Act ([[BD-CPA-AMENDMENT-2026]]). The English titles are this session's renderings of the Bengali; the Acts' texts could not be read (the gazette PDFs are in a legacy font that does not extract to readable text).

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: the Agency to Innovate Act 2023, the Digital Security Act's history, the Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission and the statistics bureau, among others, are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

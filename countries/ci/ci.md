# Côte d'Ivoire

## Description

Côte d'Ivoire (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`CI`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the telecommunications and ICT regulator, which is also the personal data protection authority ([[CI-ARTCI]]), and four laws named on its own list of laws: the personal data protection law ([[CI-LOI-2013-450]]), the cybercrime law ([[CI-LOI-2013-451]]), the information society orientation law ([[CI-LOI-2017-803]]) and the electronic communications law of 2024 ([[CI-LOI-2024-352]]). None of the texts was read.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

The laws' texts, the decrees and orders on the regulator's lists, the separate data protection authority's site (autoritedeprotection.ci, linked from the regulator's page, was not read), the electronic transactions law and national digital strategies were not read.

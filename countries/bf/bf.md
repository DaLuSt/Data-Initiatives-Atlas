# Burkina Faso

## Description

Burkina Faso (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`BF`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the data protection authority ([[BF-CIL]]), the 2021 data protection law ([[BF-LOI-001-2021]], read as text) and two laws of August 2026 known only by their titles on the authority's own list ([[BF-LOI-037-2026]], [[BF-LOI-ORG-038-2026]]).

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). The two 2026 laws' texts (the promulgation decrees on the authority's site are scanned images), whether they replace the 2021 law and the authority, the digital government and electronic communications laws, and the regulator of communications were not read. It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

## Organisations

- [[EE-RIA]] — Riigi Infosüsteemi Amet, the Information System Authority
  _(operates the data exchange layer, RIHA and the data portal)_
- [[EE-AKI]] — Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, the data protection authority
  _(Estonia's seat on [[EU-EDPB]])_
- [[EE-STATISTIKAAMET]] — Statistics Estonia _(`part-of` [[EU-ESS]] on its
  own description, the same strength of evidence as [[PL-GUS]])_

## Legislation

- [[EE-IKS]] — Isikuandmete kaitse seadus _(implements [[EU-GDPR]]; in force
  **15 January 2019**, nearly eight months after the Regulation applied)_
- [[EE-ATS]] — Avaliku teabe seadus, the Public Information Act _(the legal
  basis given for [[EE-RIHA]])_

## Platforms and infrastructure

- [[EE-X-TEE]] — the data exchange layer _(Estonia's deployment of
  [[INTL-X-ROAD]]; data flows source-to-recipient, never through a central
  store)_
- [[EE-RIHA]] — administration system for the state information system
- [[EE-ANDMEPORTAAL]] — the Estonian data portal _(established **2025**;
  merges the open data catalogue with the database descriptions)_

## Programmes

- [[EE-E-RESIDENCY]] — e-Residency _(launched 1 December 2014; the only thing
  in the Atlas that issues a state digital identity to people with no
  residence in the issuing country)_

## What Estonia added to the Atlas's understanding

- **A national system that became an international product.** X-Road passed
  into joint Estonian–Finnish ownership under [[INTL-NIIS]] in 2017 and is now
  run well beyond its members. No other national artefact in the Atlas has
  done that — [[NL-DIGIKOPPELING]] and [[BE-BELGIF]] stayed national. It
  forced the split between [[INTL-X-ROAD]] (the software) and [[EE-X-TEE]]
  (Estonia's deployment).
- **The first jointly-governed digital infrastructure body.** [[INTL-NIIS]]
  is owned by [[EE]], [[FI]] and [[IS]] together, and gave two of those three
  their first substantive entity.
- **A catalogue of systems merged with a catalogue of datasets.**
  [[EE-ANDMEPORTAAL]] consolidates what every other country in the Atlas keeps
  apart.
- **Digital identity without territory.** [[EE-E-RESIDENCY]] breaks the
  assumption every other identity platform here shares.

## Not modelled

- **CERT-EE** and the Küberturvalisuse seadus — Estonia has no cybersecurity
  entity at all, where [[NL]], [[BE]], [[DE]], [[PL]], [[CZ]] and [[PT]] do.
- ~~**Statistikaamet**, Statistics Estonia~~ — now [[EE-STATISTIKAAMET]].
- **Eesti Pank**, the central bank — Estonia's *other* named producer of
  official statistics, alongside [[EE-STATISTIKAAMET]].
- Estonia's **Open Data Directive** transposition. Estonia was not among the
  nineteen member states served with letters of formal notice in September
  2021, so it appears to have notified on time; the instrument is
  unidentified.
- The **eID card and Mobile-ID**, the identity infrastructure e-Residency is
  built on.

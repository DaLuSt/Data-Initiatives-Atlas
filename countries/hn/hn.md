# Honduras

## Description

Honduras (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`HN`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the telecommunications regulator ([[HN-CONATEL]]) and the law the Framework Law of the Telecommunications Sector (Ley Marco del Sector de Telecomunicaciones) ([[HN-LEY-MARCO-TELECOM]]), from the regulator's own site; the law's text was not opened. The Law on Transparency and the Special Law on Private Communications are also listed by the regulator; they were not modelled.

# Kiribati

## Description

Kiribati (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`KI`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Digital Transformation Office ([[KI-DTO]]), the Data Protection Act 2025 ([[KI-DPA-2025]]), the Cybersecurity Act 2026 ([[KI-CYBERSECURITY-ACT-2026]]), the Digital Government Act 2023 that the first two cite ([[KI-DIGITAL-GOVERNMENT-ACT-2023]]) and the National ICT Policy 2019 ([[KI-NATIONAL-ICT-POLICY-2019]]). The two Acts and the Policy were read as text from the PDFs the Ministry publishes; the Digital Government Act is known only by citation.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

# Kuwait

## Description

Kuwait (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`KW`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Communication and Information Technology Regulatory Authority ([[KW-CITRA]]) and the Authority's Resolution No. 42 of 2021 on the Data Privacy Protection Regulation ([[KW-CITRA-RES-42-2021]]), both from the Authority's own site. Kuwait has no general data protection authority that was found; the Authority's regulation is the instrument the Atlas holds.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: the Authority's establishing Law No. 37 of 2014, the Electronic Transactions Law and the Cybercrime Law, among others, are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

# Rwanda

## Description

Rwanda (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`RW`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Law on the protection of personal data and privacy ([[RW-LAW-058-2021]], whose trilingual text was read in the Official Gazette), the National Cyber Security Authority that enforces it ([[RW-NCSA]]), the Rwanda Utilities Regulatory Authority ([[RW-RURA]]) and the Ministry of ICT and Innovation ([[RW-MINICT]]), from those bodies' own sites.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: the National Cyber Security Authority's standards and directives, RICTA, the national data revolution policy and the statistics institute, among others, are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

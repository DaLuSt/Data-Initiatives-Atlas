# Saint Lucia

## Description

Saint Lucia (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`LC`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Data Protection Act ([[LC-DPA-2011]], Act 11 of 2011, partly in force since 31 January 2023), its 2015 amendment ([[LC-DPA-AMENDMENT-2015]]) and the Data Protection Commissioner the Act provides for ([[LC-DATA-PROTECTION-COMMISSIONER]]), from the Revised Laws of Saint Lucia (2023) on the Attorney General Chambers' own site.

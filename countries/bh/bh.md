# Bahrain

## Description

Bahrain (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`BH`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Personal Data Protection Law ([[BH-PDPL-2018]]) and the Ministry of Justice, Islamic Affairs and Waqf ([[BH-MOJ]]) that performs the tasks of the Personal Data Protection Authority, the National Cyber Security Centre ([[BH-NCSC]]) and its strategy for 2025-2028 ([[BH-NCSS-2025-2028]]), and the National Digital Economy Strategy ([[BH-NDES]]), from the National Portal's own pages and the Centre's site; the Law's Arabic text and the strategies' documents were not opened.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: the Information & eGovernment Authority, the Telecommunications Regulatory Authority, the national open data portal and the electronic transactions law, among others, are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

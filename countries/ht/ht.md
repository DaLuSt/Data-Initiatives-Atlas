# Haiti

## Description

Haiti (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`HT`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the national identification office ([[HT-ONI]]) and the decree of 1 June 2005 that set up the national identification card, number and register ([[HT-DECRET-2005-ID]]). **No data-protection act is recorded**: the identification decree has no such provision, and the instruments mentioned by secondary sources (a 2017 bill, an arrêté published on 15 May 2018) were not read.

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

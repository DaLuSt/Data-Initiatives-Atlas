# Brunei Darussalam

## Description

Brunei Darussalam (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`BN`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the Authority for Info-communications Technology Industry ([[BN-AITI]]), the Personal Data Protection Order, 2025 that AITI administers ([[BN-PDPO-2025]]), the Guide on AI Governance and Ethics ([[BN-AI-GOVERNANCE-GUIDE]]) and AITI's strategic plan 2026-2030 ([[BN-AITI-2030]]), all from AITI's own sites; no document's text was opened.

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance: the Telecommunications Order 2001, Digital Brunei 2030 and the national cybersecurity bodies, among others, are not in the Atlas yet (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

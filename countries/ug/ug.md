# Uganda

## Description

Uganda (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`UG`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the national IT authority ([[UG-NITA-U]]) and the personal data protection office listed on its site ([[UG-PDPO]]). **The Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 is not recorded as an entity**: it is known from secondary sources only (the Uganda Legal Information Institute returns a challenge page).

## What this anchor does not yet carry

Only the entities listed in [the country index](index.md). It is a first sourced slice, not a survey of the country's data governance (see `countries/README.md` on the participation model).

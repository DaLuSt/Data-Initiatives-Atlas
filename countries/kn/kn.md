# Saint Kitts and Nevis

## Description

Saint Kitts and Nevis (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2: **`KN`**) is a **base country anchor**. It carries the telecommunications regulator ([[KN-INFORMATION-COMMISSIONER]]) and the law Data Protection Act, 2018 ([[KN-DPA-2018]]), from the regulator's own site; the law's text was not opened. The Act was read as text on a law firm's copy; the Government's own news item of 4 May 2018 confirms the Bill passed. No official page for the Act or the Commissioner was reached.

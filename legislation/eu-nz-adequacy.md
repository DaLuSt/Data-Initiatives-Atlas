# European Commission adequacy decision for New Zealand

> **Created 2026-11-07** (data-sharing links beyond Europe). The decision’s own text and the Commission’s adequacy page were read through a fetch tool that returns a model’s summary unless stated in the evidence; the Brazil decision was read in full from the Commission’s PDF.

## What it says

Commission Implementing Decision 2013/65/EU of 19 December 2012 (C(2012) 9557), adopted under Article 25(6) of Directive 95/46/EC, finding that New Zealand ensures an adequate level of protection for personal data transferred from the Union. Its recital 7 relies on the Privacy Act 1993 as amended in 2010; the Privacy Commissioner is the authority named in the Annex. It stays in force under Article 45(9) GDPR.

It predates the GDPR and survives under Article 45(9) GDPR, which keeps decisions taken under Article 25(6) of Directive 95/46/EC in force until they are amended, replaced or repealed.

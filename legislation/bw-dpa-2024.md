# Data Protection Act, 2024 (Botswana, Act 18 of 2024)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #471, Botswana). The e-Laws listing page was read directly; the Act's PDF was not, so no section is quoted.

## What is sourced

Botswana e-Laws, run under the Attorney General's Chambers, lists the **Data Protection Act, Act 18 of 2024**, commencing **14 January 2025** (the page notes "through SI 4/2025", read here as the statutory instrument). Its long title begins by providing for "the continuation of the Information and Data Protection Commission" ([[BW-IDPC]]) and covers the protection of personal data and individuals' privacy in relation to it.

## What was not read

The text of the Act, its date of assent (not on the page), and the repealed 2018 Act: secondary sources say the 2024 Act replaced a 2018 Act that never took effect because its commencement was repeatedly postponed; that is not recorded here as fact.

Applies in [[BW]] (anchor edge). [[BW-IDPC]] is `governed-by` this Act.

# ECA Digital (Brazil)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #481, Brazil). Known from the Agency's pages; the law's text was not read. `start_date` is the date of the law; when its obligations take effect was not read.

## What is sourced

New rules for children and adolescents online; "obrigações para plataformas digitais com vistas à prevenção e à mitigação de riscos ao público infantil, incluindo disposições sobre supervisão parental" (Institucional page, truncated in the read). The Agency is the autonomous administrative authority for applying it.

# Decreto Supremo N° 5340 (Bolivia)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #474 group, Bolivia). The decree's text was read in full from AGETIC's PDF. `start_date` is the date it was issued ("a los veintiséis días del mes de febrero"); the Gazette publication date was not seen. The decree is a **supreme decree** (executive decree); Bolivia has no row in `metadata/rank-basis.md`, so no `rank` is set.

## What the text says

- **Art. 2.** Creates the platform under the domain www.gob.bo, administered by [[BO-AGETIC]].
- **Art. 3.** Applies to the Executive Organ, its entities, public companies and companies where the central State holds a majority. The Legislative, Judicial and Electoral organs, other central entities and the autonomous territorial entities may ask to be included.
- **Art. 4.** Minimum components: a portal to institutional websites; the State Procedures Portal; State open data; information observatories.
- **Arts. 5-7.** AGETIC sets the technical guidelines and formats (the Ministry of Justice and Institutional Transparency sets the minimum content of institutional sites); AGETIC hosts and administers; entities must register, publish and update, with the usual exceptions for defence, State security and protected secrets.
- **Transitional provisions.** Timed steps for guidelines and a staged roll-out. After them, arts. 9, 10 and 11 of DS 3525 of 4 April 2018 are repealed.

AGETIC's normative page lists the follow-up guidelines as Ministerial Resolution 060/25 of 16 April 2025 (read as a listing only).

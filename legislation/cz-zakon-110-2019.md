## Description

Act No **110/2019 Sb.** on the processing of personal data, Czechia's GDPR
adaptation — enacted **12 March 2019**, in effect from **24 April
2019**.

## The eighth national GDPR instrument

Joining [[NL-UAVG]], [[DE-BDSG]], [[ES-LOPDGDD]], [[PL-ODO]],
[[IE-DPA-2018]], [[PT-LEI-58-2019]] and, across the EEA boundary,
[[NO-PERSONOPPLYSNINGSLOVEN]] — all carrying
`implements-requirement-from` [[EU-GDPR]], with [[GB-UK-GDPR]]
(`derived-from`) and [[CH-REVDSG]] (`aligned-with`) outside that set.

**Luxembourg's is still missing** — the one member state in the Atlas whose
GDPR instrument was not identified.

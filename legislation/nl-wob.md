# Wet openbaarheid van bestuur (Wob)

> **Verified 2026-08-27.** Both cited pages were read directly.
> `verification` moves from `search-only` to `primary-source`.

## Description

The Wob was the Dutch government transparency act until 1 May 2022, when it
was replaced by [[NL-WOO]]. Confirmed by reading kbvg.nl directly: "the Woo
takes effect on May 1st, replacing the Wob immediately without transition
provisions." The Wob recognised primarily a passive disclosure obligation:
government information was disclosed in response to a citizen's request,
the Wob-verzoek — a term that entered general Dutch usage and still appears
in older material. kbvg.nl, read directly, describes the shift precisely:
under the Wob, "bestuursorganen een grote mate van vrijheid hadden om te
bepalen welke documenten zij uit eigen beweging openbaar maakten"
(administrative bodies had substantial discretion over voluntary
disclosure) — discretion the Woo replaces with binding active-disclosure
duties.

## Description

The Wob was the Dutch government transparency act until 1 May 2022, when it
was replaced by [[NL-WOO]]. It recognised primarily a passive disclosure
obligation: government information was disclosed in response to a citizen's
request, the Wob-verzoek — a term that entered general Dutch usage and still
appears in older material.

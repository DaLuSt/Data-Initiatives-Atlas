# Decision No. 10/2025 of the CEFTA Joint Committee on Digital Cooperation

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #542). The Decision's own PDF, read directly, with its Annex.

## Description

"Adopted in Pristina on 2 December 2025", listed among the documents adopted at the 18th Joint Committee meeting. Article 1 establishes [[INTL-CEFTA-WG-DIGITAL]], "to support and facilitate the CEFTA Parties' cooperation and exchange of information on digital services, including but not limited to trust services and electronic identification". Article 2 amends Decision No. 1/2015 on the CEFTA bodies to add the group under the Sub-Committee on Trade in Services and the Terms of Reference as its Annex 15. It enters into force on adoption.

Its preamble rests on Article 18 of Additional Protocol 6 (the Parties' dialogue on regulatory issues of electronic commerce, including recognition of electronic signature certificates) and on the e-commerce roadmap's foreseen "general framework for the recognition of electronic signatures and other trust services".

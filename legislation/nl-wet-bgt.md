# Wet basisregistratie grootschalige topografie

> **Verified 2026-08-21.** Every cited source was read and confirmed to
> support what this entity says. `verification: primary-source`.

## Description

The statutory basis of [[NL-BGT]]. BWBR0034026 is confirmed as this act's
own identifier on `wetten.overheid.nl`.

## Staged commencement, in three steps

| Date | What came into force |
|---|---|
| **1 January 2016** | The register's content, and the obligations of the *bronhouders* and the register holder |
| 1 July 2017 | Articles 23 and 24 (Stb. 2017, 159) |
| 1 July 2018 | Articles 29 and 30 (Stb. 2018, 122) |

`start_date` records the first, which is when the register itself became
operative in law.

## Correction

The third stage was previously dated **30 April 2018**. `wetten.overheid.nl`'s
commencement history (`Wijzigingenoverzicht`) records the actual date
articles 29 and 30 took effect as **1 July 2018** — 30 April 2018 is the date
the Eerste Kamer's own dossier records the commencement *decree* (Stb. 2018,
122, "publicatie inwerkingtreding artikelen 29 en 30") as published, not the
date those articles began applying. The original entry conflated a decree's
publication date with its own effective date.

Staged commencement by **provision** is the [[GB-DUAA]] pattern rather than
the [[CH-EMBAG]] one, which staged by *organisational scope*. The Atlas now
holds both.

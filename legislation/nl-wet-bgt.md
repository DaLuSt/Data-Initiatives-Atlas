## Description

The statutory basis of [[NL-BGT]].

## Staged commencement, in three steps

| Date | What came into force |
|---|---|
| **1 January 2016** | The register's content, and the obligations of the *bronhouders* and the register holder |
| 1 July 2017 | Articles 23 and 24 |
| 30 April 2018 | Articles 29 and 30 |

`start_date` records the first, which is when the register itself became
operative in law.

Staged commencement by **provision** is the [[GB-DUAA]] pattern rather than
the [[CH-EMBAG]] one, which staged by *organisational scope*. The Atlas now
holds both.

description: >
  Saint Lucia's Data Protection Act, Act 11 of 2011, as amended by Act 2 of 2015. It regulates the collection, processing, use and
  disclosure of personal data in a manner that recognises the right of privacy of individuals, binds the State, and provides for a
  Data Protection Commissioner (appointment, functions, independence, powers to obtain information, privacy impact assessment).
  Under section 1 as substituted in 2015 it comes into force on a date the Minister fixes by Order in the Gazette, with different
  dates allowed for different Parts or sections. The Revised Laws record it as in force from 31 January 2023 for Part I, sections 32
  to 43 of Part III, Part VI and paragraphs (a) to (g) of Schedule 2 (S.I. 4/2023); the other provisions are not recorded as in
  force.

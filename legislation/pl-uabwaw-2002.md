# Ustawa z dnia 24 maja 2002 r. o ABW oraz AW

> **Re-verified 2026-08-26.** Two of three cited pages were read directly:
> AW's own legal-framework page and ABW's own list of its legal acts. The
> Act's own text remains behind a CAPTCHA on `isap.sejm.gov.pl` — the whole
> `sejm.gov.pl` domain family blocks automated fetches this way, confirmed
> on multiple URLs this pass.

## Description

The act constituting [[PL-ABW]] and [[PL-AW]], Poland's two **civilian**
special services, splitting them on an internal/external axis and placing
both heads under the Prime Minister directly.

## The best-sourced statute in this batch

Three citations, from three independent official publishers:

- **ISAP**, the Sejm's own legal database, with the Dz.U. reference —
  genuinely CAPTCHA-blocked on every attempt this pass.
- **The ABW's own BIP**, listing this Act among its governing legislation,
  read directly this pass.
- **The AW's own *Ramy prawne* page**, read directly this pass, which
  quotes the Act's own Article 24 (AW personnel may "żądania niezbędnej
  pomocy od instytucji państwowych" — request necessary assistance from
  state institutions) and gives the agency's own motto: "pozyskujemy
  informacje — służymy państwu — chronimy naród" (we obtain information —
  we serve the state — we protect the nation).

Both agencies publish confirmation of their constituting act. Nothing else
in this batch is sourced that way — [[DE-BVERFSCHG]] and [[DE-MADG]] have
no official URL at all, and [[ES-LEY-11-2002]] has no BOE link.

The sources also give a **current consolidation**: Dz.U. 2025 item 902,
which tells us the act is live and has been amended, without telling us how
— unconfirmed this pass, since ISAP remains unreachable.

## Two agencies in the title

The act is named *o Agencji Bezpieczeństwa Wewnętrznego oraz Agencji
Wywiadu* — both bodies named in the statute's own title, where
[[BE-WIV-1998]] names a function in the singular and [[NL-WIV-2017]] names
services in the plural.

## Not modelled

- **CBA**, which the sources place alongside these agencies under the
  College for Special Services but which has its own statute.
- The act's **amendment history** between 2002 and the 2025 consolidation.
- Any **article-level** content.

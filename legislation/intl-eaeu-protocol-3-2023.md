# Third Major Protocol to the Treaty on the EAEU

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #543). Sourced from the Eurasian Economic Commission's own news item of 25 May 2023, read directly. **The Protocol's text was not read**, and whether it has entered into force was not found.

## What is sourced

Signed on 25 May 2023 by the Heads of State at the Supreme Eurasian Economic Council. It was developed "from July 2021 through June 2022" and aims "to eliminate legal regulation gaps identified during law enforcement practice, to clarify terminology used in the Treaty on the Union" and to give the Union's bodies powers on technical regulation, phytosanitary measures, the Observer State status and state procurement.

For digital trade: "The definition of the term 'transboundary space of trust' has been clarified in Annex No. 3 to the Treaty on the EAEU: apart from interaction between authorized authorities (G2G), interaction between economic entities and authorized authorities (B2G) is now possible during the preparation, sending, transmission and receipt of electronic documents and information."

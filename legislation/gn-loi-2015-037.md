# Law L/2015/037/AN on cybersecurity and personal data protection (Guinea)

> **Created 2026-11-06** (roadmap #476). Known from the regulator's listing (hosted, not necessarily administered, by the ARPT); the text was not read.

> **Number corrected 2026-11-23.** The law's own text, the 57-page scanned PDF that the ARPT embeds on its page (no text layer; the first page was read as an image), is headed 'Loi N° L/2016/037/AN, Relative à la cyber-sécurité et la protection des données à caractère personnel en République de Guinée'. The year in the number is 2016, as the page's address already suggested; the page title's 2015 is a misprint. The entity ID `GN-LOI-2015-037` is kept, since IDs are permanent (`metadata/ontology.md`), and the old number stays as an alternative name so that searches for it still find this entity. The supervising authority was not looked for in the remaining 56 pages.

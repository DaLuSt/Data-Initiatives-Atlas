# Data Security Law (China)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #482, China). The NPC's English translation was read in full; it is a translation, and only the Chinese text has legal effect. `start_date` is the date it went into effect, from the President's order printed with the translation.

## What the text says

- **Aim (art. 1):** to regulate data processing, ensure data security, promote the development and use of data, protect individuals' and organisations' lawful rights and interests, and safeguard the state's sovereignty, security and development interests.
- **Scope (art. 2):** data processing within China, and processing abroad that harms China's national security, public interests or the rights of its individuals and organisations.
- **Who supervises (art. 6):** every locality and department answers for the data it collects; competent departments for industry, telecommunications, transport, finance, natural resources, health, education, technology and others supervise data security in their sectors; public security and national security organs within their duties; and "the national cyberspace affairs department" is "in charge of the overall planning and coordination of network data security and the related supervision". The translation does not name the body, so none is modelled here.
- **Structure:** seven chapters: general provisions, data security and development, data security systems, protection obligations, security and openness of government data, legal liability, supplementary provisions.
- **Government data (arts. 41-42):** state organs disclose government data "in a timely and accurate manner", the state formulates a catalogue of open government data and builds "an open, uniform, standardized, interconnected, safe and controllable government data platform".

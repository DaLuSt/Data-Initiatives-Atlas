# USMCA Chapter 19: Digital Trade

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #544). The chapter's text on USTR's site, read directly. Annex 19-A and the side letters were only skimmed.

## What it says

- **19.8 Personal Information Protection:** each Party "shall adopt or maintain a legal framework that provides for the protection of the personal information of the users of digital trade", taking into account the APEC Privacy Framework and the OECD privacy guidelines (2013); it lists nine key principles (limitation on collection, choice, data quality, purpose specification, use limitation, security safeguards, transparency, individual participation, accountability); and it recognises "the APEC Cross-Border Privacy Rules system" as "a valid mechanism to facilitate cross-border information transfers".
- **19.11 Cross-Border Transfer of Information by Electronic Means:** "No Party shall prohibit or restrict the cross-border transfer of information, including personal information, by electronic means if this activity is for the conduct of the business of a covered person", unless a measure is necessary for a legitimate public policy objective and is neither arbitrary nor greater than necessary.
- **19.12 Location of Computing Facilities:** no Party shall require a covered person to use or locate computing facilities in its territory as a condition for doing business.
- **19.15 Cybersecurity:** the Parties "shall endeavor to" build incident response capabilities, strengthen collaboration and use risk-based approaches relying on consensus-based standards.
- **19.18 Open Government Data:** where a Party makes government information public, it "shall endeavor to ensure that the information is in a machine-readable and open format and can be searched, retrieved, used, reused, and redistributed".
- **19.17 Interactive Computer Services** is subject to Annex 19-A for Mexico (a delayed application).

The chapter's other articles (customs duties, digital products, electronic transactions and signatures, consumer protection, paperless trading, internet access principles, unsolicited messages, cooperation, source code) were read in outline and are not summarised here.

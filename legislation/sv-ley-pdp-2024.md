description: >
  El Salvador's Ley para la Protección de Datos Personales, Decreto No. 144 of the Legislative Assembly, passed in the Salón Azul on 12 November 2024, sanctioned by the President on 14 November 2024 and published in the Diario Oficial, Tomo 445, núm. 219, on 15 November 2024 (pages 24 to 62). Article 64: it enters into force eight days after its publication, which makes 23 November 2024. It sets rules for the processing of personal data by public and private controllers, with definitions (including the ARCO-POL rights of access, rectification, cancellation, opposition, portability, forgetting and limitation), principles, consent and its revocation, and infractions and sanctions.
  Its provisions refer to the ACE (the Agencia de Ciberseguridad del Estado) as the body that issues sanction resolutions (article 55) and sets protection measures (article 61), to a Director de Protección de Datos Personales whose impediments follow the Ley de Ciberseguridad y Seguridad de la Información, and to that law for the sanctioning procedure (article 53). Article 63 repeals the personal data provisions of the Ley de Acceso a la Información Pública (Decreto Legislativo 534 of 2010). The Law's other pages (about 25 of 39) were not opened.

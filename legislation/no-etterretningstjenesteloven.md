## Description

Confirmed by reading `lovdata.no` directly: **"Lov om Etterretningstjenesten
(etterretningstjenesteloven)"**, enacted **19 June 2020**, in force from
**1 January 2021**, with specific provisions phased in through
**2 September 2022**. It repeals **"lov 20. mars 1998 nr. 11 om
Etterretningstjenesten"** (the 1998 Intelligence Service Act).

Confirmed by reading `eos-utvalget.no` — the Norwegian Parliament's own
EOS Committee, which oversees all of Norway's intelligence and security
services — directly: the 2020 Act "clarif[ies] operational distinctions
between the Intelligence Service and the Police Security Service" (PST)
alongside "establishing stricter regulatory controls."

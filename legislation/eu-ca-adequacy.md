# European Commission adequacy decision for Canada

> **Created 2026-11-07** (data-sharing links beyond Europe). The decision’s own text and the Commission’s adequacy page were read through a fetch tool that returns a model’s summary unless stated in the evidence; the Brazil decision was read in full from the Commission’s PDF.

## What it says

Commission Decision 2002/2/EC of 20 December 2001 (C(2001) 4539), adopted under Article 25(6) of Directive 95/46/EC, finding that Canada provides an adequate level of protection for personal data transferred to recipients subject to the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). The Commission’s page says it covers commercial organisations only. It stays in force under Article 45(9) GDPR.

It predates the GDPR and survives under Article 45(9) GDPR, which keeps decisions taken under Article 25(6) of Directive 95/46/EC in force until they are amended, replaced or repealed.

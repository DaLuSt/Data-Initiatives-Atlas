# Decision No 235/2008/EC — European Statistical Governance Advisory Board

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #459, oversight bodies above statistical offices). The Official Journal text was read in full through the Publications Office's CELEX resolver.

## What it does

Decision No 235/2008/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of **11 March 2008**, published in OJ L 73 of 15 March 2008, establishes the **European Statistical Governance Advisory Board** ([[EU-ESGAB]]) (Article 1). It enters into force on the day after publication (Article 6). Done at Strasbourg; signed by the Presidents of the Parliament and the Council.

- **Purpose (Article 1):** to provide an independent overview of the European Statistical System as regards the implementation of the European Statistics Code of Practice.
- **Tasks (Article 2):** an annual report to the Parliament and the Council on the Code's implementation as it relates to the Commission (Eurostat), including an assessment of the European Statistical System as a whole; advice to the Commission on measures to facilitate implementation, on communicating the Code to users and data providers, and (with the Statistical Programme Committee) on updating it; and, on request, advice on user confidence in European statistics.
- **Membership (Article 3):** seven members including the chairperson, acting independently, with the Commission (Eurostat) as observer; the chairperson is selected by the Council after consulting the Commission and approved by the Parliament, and the Parliament and the Council each appoint three members; the term is three years, renewable once.
- **Proceedings (Article 4):** the Board adopts public rules of procedure, its annual report is made public, and its secretariat is provided by the Commission but must act independently of it.
- **Review (Article 5):** the role and effectiveness of the Board is to be reviewed three years after its establishment.

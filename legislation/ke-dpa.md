# Data Protection Act

> **Verified 2026-10-09.** The consolidated text on Kenya Law was read directly (Kenya Law answers 403 to the agent's own environment; it was read on a GitHub-hosted runner, see `discovery/unresolved.md` row #235).

## Description

The text states: "AN ACT of Parliament to give effect to Article 31(c) and (d) of the Constitution; to establish the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner; to make provision for the regulation of the processing of personal data; to provide for the rights of data subjects and obligations of data controllers and processors; and for connected purposes." Published in the Kenya Gazette on 15 November 2019, assented to on 8 November 2019, commenced on 25 November 2019. The page reads "This is the latest version of this Act" and shows revision to 31 December 2022 (Legal Notice 221 of 2023). Section 18 requires data controllers and processors to be registered with the Data Commissioner.

description: >
  Liberia's Telecommunications Act 2007, enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives. It creates the Liberia Telecommunications Authority (section 8), sets out the licensing regime, and in sections 49 and 50 requires service providers to keep customer information and communications confidential: no disclosure of customer information beyond name, address and listed number without written consent, a right to review and correct a service provider's records on the customer, and retention of customer-specific information only for billing and as the Authority or the law permits. Schedule B repeals Act No. 18 of 5 September 2005, which had set an interim framework.
  Section 85: it takes effect immediately upon publication into handbill; the publication date is not on the text, so `start_date` is empty. This is a telecommunications-sector confidentiality rule, not a general data protection law; none was found for Liberia.

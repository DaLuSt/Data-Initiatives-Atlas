# Orden PRE/1838/2014 (Cl@ve's founding order)

> **Created 2026-09-06**, closing part of a gap [[ES-CLAVE]]'s own entity
> flagged repeatedly across several re-verification passes: no legal
> basis or operator had ever been sourced for Cl@ve. The BOE's own text,
> read directly, supplies both.

## Description

Confirmed by reading the BOE's own text directly: this order publishes
the Council of Ministers' agreement of **19 September 2014**, which
approves Cl@ve — "la plataforma común del Sector Público Administrativo
Estatal para la identificación, autenticación y firma electrónica
mediante el uso de claves concertadas" — under Article 13.2.c) of **Ley
11/2007**, de 22 de junio. The order itself was published 8 October 2014
and took effect the following day.

## Operator, closed 2026-09-06

The BOE's own text names the operator directly: "El órgano responsable
del sistema Cl@ve será la Dirección de Tecnologías de la Información y
las Comunicaciones." Four further bodies share responsibility for their
respective areas — registration, identification, authentication and
electronic signature — per the order's own text: the Agencia Estatal de
Administración Tributaria, the Gerencia de Informática de la Seguridad
Social, the Dirección General de la Policía, and the Fábrica Nacional de
Moneda y Timbre-Real Casa de la Moneda (FNMT-RCM).

None of these five bodies is currently an Atlas entity, so no
`governed-by`-style edge is asserted from this order to any operator
beyond the relationship [[ES-CLAVE]] itself carries back to this act.

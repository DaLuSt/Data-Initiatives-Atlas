# Ley 11/2002, reguladora del Centro Nacional de Inteligencia

> **Verified 2026-08-26.** The act's own BOE citation — previously
> missing, this entity's self-flagged "weakest point" — was found and
> read directly: BOE-A-2002-8628. It replaces the commercial-database
> citation this entity previously relied on.

## Description

The act creating [[ES-CNI]] and regulating it. Confirmed by reading the
act's own text directly: Article 7.1 attaches the CNI organisationally
to the Ministry of Defence, and Article 7.2 gives it "autonomía
funcional" as a public body with its own legal personality and full
legal capacity.

## Article 12: an act that requires another act

Article 12 provides for prior judicial control of CNI activities and states
that **an organic law** will prescribe its form. It could not do the job
itself: establishing judicial control meant amending the *Ley Orgánica del
Poder Judicial*, which is reserved to organic legislation.

[[ES-LO-2-2002]] was passed the **same day** to do exactly that, and nothing
else. Two acts, one date, one ordinary and one organic — the constitutional
machinery working as designed.

## It also created the Atlas's Spanish cyber-security authority

This act entrusts to the CNI the functions relating to the **security of
information technologies**. Real Decreto 421/2004 then created [[ES-CCN]] to
discharge that mandate, *adscrito* to the CNI and governed by this act.

[[ES-CCN]] has been an Atlas entity since the Spain batch, as the authority
behind [[ES-ENS]]. Until this batch there was no way to see that its
statutory basis is an intelligence act.

## Not modelled

- **Real Decreto 421/2004**, which regulates the CCN — confirmed by
  reading its own BOE text directly this pass (BOE-A-2004-5051) for
  [[ES-CCN]]'s own file. A royal decree, not an act; the Atlas holds the
  statute it implements.
- The **Comisión Delegada del Gobierno para Asuntos de Inteligencia** and
  the annual intelligence directive.
- Any **article-level** content beyond Articles 1, 7 and 12.
- **CESID**, the CNI's predecessor (Centro Superior de Información de la
  Defensa) — es.wikipedia.org, read directly for [[ES-CNI]]'s file, names
  it but the 2002 reform itself was not researched. Also not modelled:
  the CNI's own attachment history — it moved to the Presidency Ministry
  in 2011 and back to Defence only in 2018, which this act's Article 7.1
  (Defence) captures only as it stands today.

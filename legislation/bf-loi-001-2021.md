# Loi n°001-2021/AN (Burkina Faso)

> **Created 2026-10-17** (roadmap #477, Burkina Faso). The text was read in full from the CIL's site (a scan with a text layer). `start_date` is the date of adoption in the text; the promulgation and entry-into-force dates are not in the text read. **Two laws of August 2026 on the same subject appear on the CIL's list (see [[BF-LOI-037-2026]]); the status here is `active` only because nothing read says the 2021 law has been repealed.**

## What the text says

- **Art. 1-2:** object (protect natural persons' fundamental rights in personal data processing) and scope (automated or manual processing of data in files).
- **Title III, art. 45 onwards:** the CIL, an independent administrative authority with administrative and management autonomy, regulatory and sanctioning powers specified by decree; nine members including one designated by the President of Faso, five-year terms renewable once, appointed by decree in the Council of Ministers.
- **Art. 82:** repeals law 010-2004/AN of 20 April 2004 on personal data protection.
- Principles listed on the CIL's own page: transparency (information, consent, lawfulness), purpose, relevance and proportionality, retention, security and confidentiality.

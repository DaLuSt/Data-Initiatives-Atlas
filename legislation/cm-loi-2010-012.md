# Loi n° 2010/012 (Cameroon)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #483, Cameroon). Known from the Agency's presentation page and its list of sector texts; the law's text was not opened. `start_date` is the date of promulgation given there.

## What is sourced

Promulgated 21 December 2010 together with the electronic communications law (2010/013) and, per the texts list, the electronic commerce law (2010/021). It gives [[CM-ANTIC]] the regulation of electronic security: electronic certification, security audit and security watch.

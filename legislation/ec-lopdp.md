# Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales (Ecuador)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #478, Ecuador). The text was read as published in the Registro Oficial, from a copy hosted by a public body. `start_date` is the publication date, which is the date of entry into force under the final provision; the transitional provision's two-year delay for the corrective and penalty regime puts that part at about 26 May 2023, which was not checked against any later amendment.

## What the text says

- **Dates.** Approved by the National Assembly on 10 May 2021 ("Dado y suscrito, a los diez días del mes de mayo"), sanctioned by President Lenín Moreno on 21 May 2021, published 26 May 2021.
- **Authority (arts. 75-77).** The Autoridad de Protección de Datos Personales is the "órgano de control y vigilancia" guaranteeing citizens' protection of their personal data. Its functions include supervising controllers and processors, the power to sanction, hearing complaints, technical audits, issuing general and technical rules, and creating and running the National Registry of Personal Data Protection ([[EC-SPDP]]). Its head, the Superintendent, is appointed from a shortlist the President sends, by speciality and merit, subject to public scrutiny; a professional in law, information systems, communication or technology with a postgraduate degree and ten years' experience.
- **Transition (first transitional provision).** The corrective measures and penalty regime take effect two years after publication; the rest on publication.
- Amends other laws in its provisions (the Law on Electronic Commerce, the Telecommunications Law and others, by registry number); not read in detail.

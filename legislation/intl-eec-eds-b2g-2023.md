# EEC Board decision on mutual recognition of electronic digital signature in transboundary B2G interactions (2023)

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #543). Sourced **only** from the Commission's own news item of 22 August 2023, read directly. **The decision's own text, its number and the rules it approves were not read**, so the name above is the news item's description, not an official title.

## What is sourced

"At its meeting held on August 22, the Eurasian Economic Commission's Board approved the rules for mutual recognition of digital signature (EDS) in transboundary B2G (Business-to-Government) interactions in the territory of the Eurasian Economic Union." The Board "determined 14 spheres that require EDS recognition in transboundary B2G interactions as a matter of priority". The item says that once the rules are implemented, companies "will be able to use the same EDS to interact with public authorities" in different Member States, "without a separate EDS for each country", and that this needs the "trusted third parties originally created only for intergovernmental G2G interaction" to be finalised.

No source read states how this decision relates to the Third Major Protocol's extension of the "transboundary space of trust" to B2G ([[INTL-EAEU-PROTOCOL-3-2023]]); the two are about the same interaction and are not linked by an edge.

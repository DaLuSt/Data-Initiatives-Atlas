## Description

Dated 20 November 2003 by its own title (Staatsblad 2003, 516 — confirmed
by search of official sources; the archived text itself could not be
fetched this pass), this act lays down the legal basis of [[NL-CBS]].
Confirmed by reading eerstekamer.nl and the bill's own explanatory
memorandum (kst-28277-3.html) directly: it effected the CBS's
externalisation into a zelfstandig bestuursorgaan, which it became on
1 January 2004, ending the CBS's status as part of the Ministry of Economic
Affairs and replacing it with governance split between a Director-General
(professional/statistical independence) and the Centrale Commissie voor de
Statistiek (work-programme approval and oversight).

The memorandum, read directly, also confirms the act establishes rules on
the acquisition, use and provision of data for statistical information
services; improves the CBS's business operations through an accrual
accounting system; expands the CCS's supervisory powers; and — per its own
stated aim — reduces administrative burden on businesses by giving the CBS
access to government registrations in place of separate surveys, subject to
strict confidentiality protections. It is described there as guaranteeing
the CBS's independent position relative to government and other public
institutions, and as charging the CBS with executing EU statistical
obligations and participating in European statistical governance.

`start_date` records 1 January 2004, when the CBS became a ZBO under the
act, rather than the act's own date of enactment (20 November 2003) or
commencement — the act's commencement clause allows different provisions to
take effect on different royal-decree-set dates, per eerstekamer.nl, so the
two dates are not necessarily the same.

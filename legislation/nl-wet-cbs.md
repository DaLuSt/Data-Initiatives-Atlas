## Description

Enacted on 20 November 2003, this act lays down the legal basis of
[[NL-CBS]]. It effected the CBS's externalisation into a zelfstandig
bestuursorgaan, which it became on 1 January 2004.

The act establishes rules on the acquisition, use and provision of data for
statistical information services; improves the CBS's business operations
through a revenue/cost system and strengthened integrated management;
expands the supervisory powers of the Centrale Commissie voor de Statistiek
(CCS); and clarifies the relationship between the responsible minister, the
CCS and the CBS. It is described as guaranteeing the CBS's independent
position relative to government and other public institutions.

`start_date` records 1 January 2004, when the CBS became a ZBO under the
act, rather than the act's own date of enactment or commencement — those
were not separately established and may differ.

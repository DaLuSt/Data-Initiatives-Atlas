# Закон за достъп до обществена информация (Access to Public Information Act)

> **Created 2026-09-25**, closing the Bulgarian half of the scoping gap
> `discovery/unresolved.md` item #44 flagged: Bulgaria (and Latvia,
> now [[LV-IAL]]) lacked a modelled transposing instrument for the
> [[EU-OPEN-DATA-DIRECTIVE]], even though both were named among the four
> member states referred to the CJEU over it in February 2023. Sourced
> from a Bulgarian NGO's own consolidated text of the law and a European
> Commission subdomain's own news page, both read directly.

## Description

Bulgaria's Закон за достъп до обществена информация (Access to Public
Information Act, sometimes abbreviated ZDOI) was originally enacted
**7 July 2000** (State Gazette No. 55). Confirmed directly on its own
consolidated text: a **2023 amendment (State Gazette No. 82, in force
29 September 2023)** added a new §1a stating in so many words that the
law **"въвежда разпоредбите на Директива (ЕС) 2019/1024"** — introduces
the provisions of Directive (EU) 2019/1024, the Open Data Directive.
This is the clearest possible transposition-clause citation this Atlas
has recorded: the amending provision itself names the directive.

The same 2023 amendment requires public-sector bodies to plan and
publish open-data releases annually and to implement APIs for
machine-readable access to registries and information systems. An
earlier §16, also confirmed directly, recorded the same law's 2007
transposition of the original PSI Directive 2003/98/EC.

## Referred to the CJEU before its own fix arrived

Confirmed directly on a European Commission subdomain's own news page:
the Commission referred **Belgium, Bulgaria, Latvia and the Netherlands**
to the Court of Justice of the European Union on **15 February 2023**
for failing to transpose the Open Data Directive, following the 17 July
2021 deadline, a September 2021 letter of formal notice, and an
April–June 2022 reasoned opinion. Bulgaria's own §1a transposition
clause was added over seven months later, on 29 September 2023 —
plausibly, though not confirmed by any source read, a direct legislative
response to the referral.

## Not modelled

- Whether the resulting CJEU case against Bulgaria was subsequently
  closed or withdrawn following the 2023 amendment — no source read
  confirms either way.
- The Access to Public Information Act's non-open-data provisions (its
  original 2000 remit on general access to public information).

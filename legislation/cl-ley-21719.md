# Ley 21.719 — protección y tratamiento de los datos personales (Chile)

> **Re-checked 2026-11-08** (re-verification of pending instruments). Two news reports read through a summarising fetch tool (PPC Land, 28 October; Emol, 28-29 October 2026): the Executive asked to postpone the entry into force by a year; the Senate Constitution Committee moved the date to 15 October 2027 (approved in general on 19 October and in particular on 26 October) and the Senate floor approved the bill on 28 October 2026 by 33 votes to 6; it goes to the Chamber of Deputies for its second constitutional stage (a search result from camara.cl says its Constitution Committee approved it on 5 November 2026; that page answers 403 and was not read). The bill also raises the Agency's council from three to five members. The Senate's and Chamber's own pages answer 403 here, so none of this is from a legislative record. `status` is `adopted` (published, main provisions not yet in force), which the earlier `active` did not say.

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #473, Chile). The law's own text was **not** read (`bcn.cl` returns a challenge page, and the Diario Oficial's 2024 issue is a 403). What follows is what the Diario Oficial of 17 June 2025 says about it in the recitals of a ministerial decree, plus the Academia Judicial's summary.

## What is sourced

- **Publication:** in the Diario Oficial on **13 December 2024**.
- **Entry into force:** "la que entrará en vigencia el día 1° de diciembre de 2026, de acuerdo con lo dispuesto en su artículo primero transitorio"; the Academia Judicial's page says the same.
- **What it does:** it amends Ley 19.628 on the protection of private life to raise the standard of protection of personal data and to regulate the rights arising from the constitutional guarantee of Article 19 N° 4; regulates processing by private entities and public bodies; sets new rules and principles for processing; "crea un régimen sancionatorio aplicable a todos los responsables de datos"; and creates a new institution for supervising processing and applying sanctions, the **Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales** ([[CL-AGENCIA-PDP]]). It assigns functions in its implementation to the Ministries of Finance (Hacienda) and of Economy, Development and Tourism.
- **Implementation:** a ministerial decree of 5 June 2025 (published 17 June 2025) created an advisory commission to the Ministry General Secretariat of the Presidency for implementing the law; its work is advisory only.

## What is not here

The law's articles, its transitional provisions beyond the first, and any later bill to postpone the entry into force (a secondary source reports a bill entered in the Senate on 31 August 2026 to move it to 1 December 2027; not read).

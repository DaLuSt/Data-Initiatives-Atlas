# Real Decreto-ley 19/2018

> **Verified 2026-10-09.** The consolidated text on the BOE was read directly. The BOE notes that the consolidated text has no legal value. The decree-law was later complemented by Real Decreto 736/2019 and Orden ECE/1263/2019, which EUR-Lex also lists; neither is modelled.

## Description

The preamble describes the Directive as the basis of the new regime and the need for a safer and more reliable environment for payment innovation. The consolidated text was last updated on 28 December 2023.

## Relationships

Applies in [[ES]]; implements [[EU-PSD2]].

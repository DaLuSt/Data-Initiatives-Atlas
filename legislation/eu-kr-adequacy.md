# European Commission adequacy decision for the Republic of Korea

> **Created 2026-11-07** (data-sharing links beyond Europe). The decision’s own text and the Commission’s adequacy page were read through a fetch tool that returns a model’s summary unless stated in the evidence; the Brazil decision was read in full from the Commission’s PDF.

## What it says

Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/254 of 17 December 2021 (published in OJ L 44 of 24 February 2022), adopted under Article 45(3) GDPR, on the adequate protection of personal data by the Republic of Korea under the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA; Act No. 10465 of 29 March 2011, as last amended by Act No. 16930 of 4 February 2020, per its recital 5), supervised by the Personal Information Protection Commission (recital 12). The Commission’s adequacy page says its first periodic review concluded on 23 July 2026.

It was adopted under the GDPR itself (Article 45(3)), so it carries a periodic review at least every four years (Article 45(3)).

## Description

The Kadasterwet, of **3 May 1989**, contains the rules on the public
registers for registered property and on the cadastre.

## It closes the weakest of the ten

`discovery/research-queue.md` recorded [[NL-BRT]] as *"the only one of the
ten where **no statute was found at all** … the weakest of the ten."*

This act is it. The sources state that **the cadastral base registration and
the topographic base registration** are maintained under the Kadasterwet as
authentic data, with database rights reserved to the Dienst voor het kadaster
en de openbare registers.

So one statute carries **two** of the ten base registrations —
[[NL-BRK]] and [[NL-BRT]] — which is why the gap was hard to find by
searching for a "Wet basisregistratie topografie" that does not exist.

That is the useful shape of this batch: seven statutes for nine registers,
not ten statutes for ten registers. The stelsel's legal underpinning is not
one-to-one, and the Atlas can now show that.

## Not modelled

- The **Organisatiewet Kadaster** (BWBR0006463), which constitutes the
  Dienst voor het kadaster en de openbare registers — [[NL-KADASTER]] — as an
  organisation. The Kadasterwet governs the registers; the Organisatiewet
  governs the body. That pair is the cleanest example in the Atlas of the
  distinction, and only one half is modelled.

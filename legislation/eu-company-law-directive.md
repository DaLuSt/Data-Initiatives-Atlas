## Description

Confirmed by reading EUR-Lex's own text directly: "DIRECTIVE (EU)
2017/1132 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL of 14 June 2017
relating to certain aspects of company law (codification)." It codifies
several earlier company-law directives into one text, including —
**Article 22** — the legal framework for the **system of interconnection
of registers**: "the registers of Member States, the platform, [and] the
portal serving as the European electronic access point." **Article 23**
gives the Commission authority to develop and/or operate the platform,
and **Article 25** funds the platform and portal from the EU's general
budget while member states bear the cost of adjusting their own
registers.

Per the European Commission's own blog, read independently, the
resulting system — now [[EU-BRIS]] — traces its **original mandate to
Directive 2012/17/EU**, later codified into this 2017 directive.

## Not modelled

- **Directive (EU) 2019/1151** (the Digitalisation Directive) and
  **Directive (EU) 2019/2121** (cross-border conversions, mergers and
  divisions), both of which amend this directive — named in secondary
  sourcing but not independently read this pass.
- The directive's substantive company-law content beyond Articles 22,
  23 and 25.

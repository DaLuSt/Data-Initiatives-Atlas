# Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 (Sri Lanka)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #472, Sri Lanka). Known from the Authority's own home page, which links the Act and an amendment in English, Sinhala and Tamil; the Act's text was not read.

## What is sourced

The Authority's site calls it Sri Lanka's independent legislation for personal data protection, says it "draws from international best practices across Europe, the United States and Asia", and offers for download both the Act and the **Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2025**. The page also shows a headline that "Enforcement Begins on March 18, 2025" (a headline, not the article, which is placeholder text). The Authority's background page, read directly the same day, says the draft was published in 2019 under the Ministry of Digital Infrastructure, the Act was **passed by Parliament on 19 March 2022** and certified by the Speaker, and its provisions are phased in: Part V (the Authority) on 17 July 2023, Parts VI, VIII, IX and X on 1 December 2023, and Parts I, II, III and VII on 18 March 2025. It also describes the Act as "tremendously inspired by" the EU's GDPR.

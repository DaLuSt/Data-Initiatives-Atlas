## Description

Confirmed by reading efta.int's own PDF directly (2026-09-19): "DECISION
OF THE EEA JOINT COMMITTEE No 22/2018 of 9 February 2018 amending Annex XI
(Electronic communication, audiovisual services and information society)
to the EEA Agreement [2019/2058]." It incorporates [[EU-EIDAS]] —
Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 — into the [[INTL-EEA-AGREEMENT]], replacing
the Annex XI entry that previously pointed to Directive 1999/93/EC (which
eIDAS itself repealed at EU level in 2014).

The Decision entered into force the day after adoption — **10 February
2018** — "provided that all the notifications under Article 103(1) of the
EEA Agreement have been made." Its publication in the Official Journal's
EEA Section, however, did not happen until **OJ L 323 of 12 December
2019** — a gap of nearly two years between legal effect and publication,
the same kind of administrative lag [[INTL-EEA-JCD-154-2018]] does not
show but which is unremarkable for the EEA Supplement's own backlog.

Two adaptations beyond incorporation itself, both confirmed verbatim by
reading the text directly:

- Article 14(1) is adapted so that EFTA States can conclude international
  agreements on trust-service recognition on the same footing as the EU
  itself does under TFEU Article 218, with the Union committing to
  "endeavour to obtain equal treatment for qualified trust services
  provided by qualified trust service providers established in the EFTA
  States" whenever it negotiates such an agreement;
- Article 51's transitional dates (originally 1 July / 2 July 2017,
  calendar dates that had already passed by the time of incorporation)
  are recast for the EFTA States as "six months after the date of entry
  into force of Decision of the EEA Joint Committee No 22/2018."

## Why this is the EEA route made visible, again

The same three-step structure [[INTL-EEA-JCD-154-2018]] documents for the
GDPR applies here for eIDAS:

```
   EU-EIDAS                                 ← the EU act
      │ referenced by
      ▼
   INTL-EEA-JCD-22-2018 ── amends ──▶ INTL-EEA-AGREEMENT
      │ applies-in
      ├────────────┬────────────┐
      ▼            ▼            ▼
     NO           IS           LI
```

[[NO-ID-PORTEN]]'s own entity already found the *effect* of this
incorporation — two Norwegian eID schemes (BankID, Buypass ID) reaching
formal eIDAS-notified status in 2022 — before this decision itself could
be read directly. That partial closure recorded the notification as
"strong indirect evidence the incorporation happened, even without a
source naming the decision directly." This entity supplies that source.

# Data Protection Act 2023 (Guyana)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #471, Guyana). The text read is the **Bill** as published by the Ministry of Legal Affairs; the Act as assented was not read. Secondary sources give the Act as No. 18 of 2023, assented on 16 August 2023 (not checked against the Gazette), and disagree on whether a commencement order has been issued.

## What the Bill provides

- **Commencement (section 1):** on the day the Minister appoints by order, with different days possible for different provisions.
- **Structure:** principles for processing personal data (Part II), rights of a data subject (Part III: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, profiling), transfers outside Guyana (Part IV), exemptions (Part V), registration of data controllers and processors (Part VI, "prohibition on processing without registration"), and a **Data Protection Commissioner** (Part VII).
- **Commissioner (section 72):** an office, a body corporate, responsible for the administration of the Act; the President appoints the Commissioner, a person "of eminence in public life" with knowledge of law, science and technology, management or governance, and may remove them on listed grounds after a hearing.

## Status

Entered as `active` because the Act is reported enacted; whether it is in force is **not established** (commencement is by order). A press letter by the Data Protection Commissioner reported by the Guyana Chronicle was found by search and not read.

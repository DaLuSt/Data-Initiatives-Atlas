# Privacy Act (Canada)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #472, Canada). The Justice Laws Website's title page was read directly; the text of the sections was not.

## What is sourced

R.S.C., 1985, c. P-21, current to 21 September 2026, with a section 2 titled "Purpose of Act" in its table of contents. The Office of the Privacy Commissioner ([[CA-OPC]]) says the Privacy Act governs how federal departments and agencies handle personal information and that the OPC was established in 1983 following its passage. Its provisions were not read, so none is described here.

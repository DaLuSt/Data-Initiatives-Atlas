# Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados Pessoais (Brazil)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #481, Brazil). Known from the Agency's pages; the law's text (planalto.gov.br did not answer) was not read. `start_date` is the date of the law; its entry into force was staged and is **not** recorded here.

## What is sourced

Law No. 13.709 of 14 August 2018. Art. 55-J lists the Agency's competences (the Agency's Competências page quotes it: protect personal data and trade and industrial secrets, guidelines for the National Policy, supervise and sanction, among others). Personal data protection is a fundamental right under Constitutional Amendment No. 115 of 2022 (same pages).

Applies in [[BR]]; [[BR-ANPD]] is `governed-by` it.

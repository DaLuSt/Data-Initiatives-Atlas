# Data Protection Act, 2023 (Seychelles)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #474, Seychelles). Known from the Information Commission's own page; the Act's text (on gazette.sc, which returned a 403 to the agent) was not read, so no section is described and no start date is recorded.

## What is sourced

The page says the Act "came into effect following its passage in December 2023", replaces "the earlier Data Protection Act" and names the Information Commission as the body that implements, monitors and enforces it. It lists the principles (fair and lawful collection, purpose limitation, minimisation, accuracy, security, limited retention) and the rights it describes.

## Not recorded (leads only)

Search summaries give 22 December 2023 as the Gazette date, Act 24 of 2023, and an 18-month transition to June 2025. None was read from a primary source.

  Guatemala's Ley de Acceso a la Información Pública, Decreto Número 57-2008 of the Congress, issued at the Legislative Palace on 23 September 2008 and sanctioned by the President on 22 October 2008. It guarantees access to public information held by the State (article 1) and, in its Chapter VI, 'Hábeas data' (articles 30 to 35), makes the bodies it covers responsible for the personal data they hold: procedures for access and correction, adequate, relevant and non-excessive data, security, no dissemination or sale of personal data without consent, and a ban on marketing sensitive data. Article 1(2) names the right to know and protect personal data in State files as one of its objects.
  Article 72: it enters into force 180 days after its publication in the Diario Oficial, except article 72 itself and articles 6 and 68, which enter into force the day after publication; the publication date is not in the text read, so `start_date` is empty. It covers State bodies and others the Law names, not private controllers; no general data protection law was found for Guatemala (search results describe bills such as initiative 6464 as pending in July 2026; that news item was not read).

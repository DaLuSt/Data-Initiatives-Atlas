# Data Protection Act 2018 (Ireland)

> **Verified 2026-08-22.** All three cited pages were read directly and
> confirmed the claims below, verbatim in places.

## Description

Ireland's Data Protection Act 2018 gives further effect to [[EU-GDPR]],
establishes [[IE-DPC]], and supplies the domestic procedural framework for
the Commission's inquiries — including those it conducts as lead supervisory
authority.

What distinguishes the Irish act in practice is not its type but what rests
on it: the DPC's Article 60 draft decisions — the ones that bind controllers
across the whole Union — are made "in accordance with the Data Protection
Act 2018 and Article 60 of the GDPR". A national procedural statute is doing
work in twenty-seven member states.

# Wet van 15 januari 1990 (KSZ act)

> **Verified 2026-08-26.** Both cited sources were read directly. The KSZ's
> own page quotes the act's Article 1 verbatim; Wikipedia corroborates the
> founding year and the decentralised-storage principle, but gives a
> different institution count from the KSZ's own current materials — see
> below. `verification: primary-source`.

## Description

The act of **15 January 1990** established and organised [[BE-KSZ]], the
Kruispuntbank van de Sociale Zekerheid. Confirmed by reading the KSZ's own
page directly: its **Article 1** establishes "a public institution with
legal personality" under the name Kruispuntbank van de sociale zekerheid.
The act remains the KSZ's current legal basis for processing personal data
under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR (necessity for a legal obligation), per the
KSZ's own GDPR register entry.

`confidence: medium` (raised from `low`) and `coverage: low` unchanged: the
act's founding provision is now confirmed, but its remaining substantive
content — governance, funding, the scope of "authentic sources" it defines
— is not recorded beyond what [[BE-KSZ]] describes in prose.

## The oldest instrument in the Atlas

At 1990 this is, by some margin, the earliest-dated entity the Atlas holds
— predating [[EU-GDPR]] by 26 years, [[EU-INSPIRE]] by 17 and the Dutch and
German once-only mechanisms by decades.

That is worth noticing rather than passing over: the architecture
[[BE-KSZ]] describes — decentralised authentic sources, a broker with no
access to the data, once-only collection — was legislated in Belgium in
1990, and is close to what [[EU-SDG]] asked of all member states in 2018.
The Atlas records both facts and **asserts no relationship between them**,
because no source read draws one and the direction of influence, if any, is
not something to infer from two dates.

## A conflicting institution count, flagged rather than resolved

[[BE-KSZ]]'s description says the network connects "roughly 2000
institutions." Wikipedia, read this pass, states the same figure —
"ongeveer 2000 verschillende instellingen." But the KSZ's **own** current
GDPR register entry, also read this pass, says the network "connects
approximately **3000** actors responsible for social protection." Both are
genuine, dated readings of the KSZ's own or KSZ-adjacent material; the
discrepancy is not resolved here and is noted on [[BE-KSZ]] as well.

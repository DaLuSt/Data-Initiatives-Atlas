## Description

The act of **15 January 1990** established and organised [[BE-KSZ]], the
Kruispuntbank van de Sociale Zekerheid.

`confidence: low` and `coverage: low`: the act's content is not recorded.
What is established is that it exists, what it is called, and that it is
the KSZ's constituting instrument — the KSZ publishes a page dedicated to
it.

## The oldest instrument in the Atlas

At 1990 this is, by some margin, the earliest-dated entity the Atlas holds
— predating [[EU-GDPR]] by 26 years, [[EU-INSPIRE]] by 17 and the Dutch and
German once-only mechanisms by decades.

That is worth noticing rather than passing over: the architecture
[[BE-KSZ]] describes — decentralised authentic sources, a broker with no
access to the data, once-only collection — was legislated in Belgium in
1990, and is close to what [[EU-SDG]] asked of all member states in 2018.
The Atlas records both facts and **asserts no relationship between them**,
because no source read draws one and the direction of influence, if any, is
not something to infer from two dates.

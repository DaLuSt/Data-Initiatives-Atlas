# Privacy Act 2020 (New Zealand)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #478, New Zealand). Known from the Privacy Commissioner's page; the Act's text (on legislation.govt.nz, which answers the agent with a challenge page) was not read, so no section is described. The Privacy Act 1993 it replaced is not modelled.

## What is sourced

In force 1 December 2020. It has "a set of principles that govern how agencies (organisations and businesses) can collect, store, use and share personal information", the 13 information privacy principles (IPPs); the Commissioner may issue codes of practice under it. The Privacy Amendment Act 2025 ([[NZ-PRIVACY-AMENDMENT-ACT-2025]]) added IPP 3A.

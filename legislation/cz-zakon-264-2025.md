# Zákon č. 264/2025 Sb., o kybernetické bezpečnosti

> **Created 2026-09-06**, closing a long-flagged gap: Czechia's NIS2
> transposition was previously unidentified in the Atlas (see
> [[CZ-NUKIB]]). NÚKIB's own hosted PDF of the Act's text was read
> directly (via its unofficial English translation, marked DRAFT v3),
> confirming both the NIS2 citation and the repeal of the predecessor
> Act in the statute's own words. The Chamber of Deputies' own
> legislative-history record (psp.cz) independently confirms the full
> passage timeline and Sbírka zákonů publication.

## Description

Czechia's new Cybersecurity Act, **264/2025 Sb.**, transposes the EU
[[EU-NIS2]] Directive. As a government bill (sněmovní tisk 759, "Vládní
návrh zákona o kybernetické bezpečnosti – EU"), it passed the Chamber of
Deputies' third reading on **25 April 2025**, was approved by the Senate on
**11 June 2025**, signed by the President on **26 June 2025**, and
published in the Sbírka zákonů as **264/2025 Sb.** on **4 August 2025** —
all confirmed directly from the Chamber of Deputies' own legislative-record
page.

Multiple independent secondary sources (Hospodářská komora, Svaz průmyslu a
dopravy ČR, EZÚ) agree the Act took **effect 1 November 2025**, expanding
the population of regulated entities from roughly 400 under the 2014 Act to
more than 6,000, under a higher/lower obligations regime.

## Read directly from the Act's own text

NÚKIB hosts its own unofficial English translation of the Act (marked
"DRAFT", version 3) at a stable portal URL. Reading it directly (via its
PDF, since WebFetch reports PDFs as binary/unreadable — the locally saved
copy's page images render legibly) confirmed two things in the statute's
own words rather than only in secondary commentary:

- **§1, footnote 2** cites, verbatim, "Directive (EU) 2022/2555 of the
  European Parliament and of the Council of 14 December 2022 on measures to
  ensure a high common level of cybersecurity in the Union ... repealing
  Directive (EU) 2016/1148 (NIS 2 Directive)" — the Act's own transposition
  citation.
- **§72 ("Repeal provisions")** lists 17 repealed instruments, the first of
  which is "Act No. 181/2014 Coll., on Cybersecurity and on Amendments to
  Related Acts (Act on Cybersecurity)" — the direct predecessor.

**Not confirmed from this copy**: §73 ("Effectiveness") is left blank in
this translation — "This Act shall enter into force on ..." — because the
hosted copy is an unofficial draft translation, not the enacted text with
the date filled in. The 1 November 2025 effective date is therefore
recorded from convergent secondary sources rather than read directly in the
statute itself.

## The predecessor Act is not itself modelled

Act No. 181/2014 Sb. (the 2014 Cybersecurity Act) was never added to the
Atlas as its own entity, so its repeal cannot be recorded as a `supersedes`
edge to a real node. The repeal is recorded as a scope anchor to [[CZ]]
instead, with the evidence field carrying the primary-source citation to
§72. A future pass could create `CZ-ZAKON-181-2014` purely as a
superseded/inactive entity if the repeal chain is judged worth modelling
explicitly; not done here as it would add a `status: inactive` entity with
no independent research value beyond the repeal fact already carried here.

## Relationships

# Ley 81 de 2019 (Panama)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #479, Panama). Known from ANTAI's pages; the law's text was not read. `start_date` is the date of the law as ANTAI cites it. The FAQ speaks of "la entrada en vigencia" but the date it took effect was not stated on the pages read, so it is not recorded.

## What is sourced

- Date and number: Ley No. 81 of 26 March 2019.
- Art. 4 defines sensitive data ("la esfera íntima de su titular"); art. 8 sets consent as the general rule with exceptions; art. 33 makes model contractual clauses a condition for lawful intra- and cross-border transfers.
- Its regulation is Executive Decree 285 of 2021 ([[PA-DE-285-2021]]).

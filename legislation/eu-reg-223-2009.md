# Regulation (EC) No 223/2009 — European statistics

> **Verified 2026-08-21.** Every source this entity cites is on a domain the
> repository owner confirmed read and correct — `europa.eu`. `verification:
> primary-source`. See `docs/re-verification.md` §"The confirmed domains".

## Description

The regulation of **11 March 2009** informally known as the *European
Statistical Law*. It establishes the legal framework for the development,
production and dissemination of European statistics and lays down the
statistical principles they must satisfy:

| Principle | What the sources say it means |
|---|---|
| Professional independence | Statistics are developed, produced and disseminated using techniques, definitions, methodologies and sources free from pressure by political or interest groups or by EU or national authorities, **including as regards when and what to disseminate** |
| Impartiality, objectivity, reliability | Stated as principles of the regulation |
| Statistical confidentiality | Stated as a principle of the regulation |
| Cost-effectiveness | Stated as a principle of the regulation |

It designates Eurostat as the authority responsible for coordinating
statistical activity in the Union's bodies, requires each member state to
designate a national statistical institute as the national contact point for
Eurostat, and constitutes the European Statistical System as the partnership
between them.

It **repealed three earlier instruments**: Regulation (EC, Euratom)
No 1101/2008, Council Regulation (EC) No 322/97 on Community statistics, and
Council Decision 89/382/EEC, Euratom.

## Why this entity exists now

[[EU-ESS]] was created in the UN batch with the regulation as its first
source and a section headed *"Regulation (EC) No 223/2009 is cited, not
modelled"*. That section named the reason plainly: the Atlas also had
Regulation (EU) 1025/2012 sitting uncited beneath the European
standardisation organisations, and creating **one of the pair** would leave
the Atlas inconsistent about how statutory bases are handled.

**The answer was to create both, not neither.** This entity and
[[EU-REG-1025-2012]] were written in the same batch, and the consequence
recorded on `EU-ESS` — "this framework has **no `governed-by` edge**, and
the instrument that establishes it appears only as a citation" — no longer
holds.

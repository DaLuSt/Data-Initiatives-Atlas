## Description

The statutory basis of [[NL-BRV]], the base registration of vehicles.

## A general act that happens to contain a base registration

Like the Wet WOZ, this is not a registration statute. It is the Dutch **road
traffic act** — licensing, vehicle requirements, traffic offences — and the
vehicle registration sits inside it.

Three of the seven acts in this batch are of this kind: general statutes with
a base registration inside them (Wegenverkeerswet, Wet WOZ, Kadasterwet), and
four were written to constitute a registration (BAG, BGT, BRO,
Handelsregisterwet). That split is invisible from the register entities and
visible from the statutes, which is a decent argument for having created
them.

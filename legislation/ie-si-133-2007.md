# European Communities (Access to Information on the Environment) Regulations 2007

> **Verified 2026-10-09.** The instrument's text was read directly on the Irish Statute Book. Later amendments (2011, 2014) are listed by EUR-Lex and are not modelled. `rank` is not set: `metadata/rank-basis.md` records the Irish basis for Acts only.

## Description

S.I. No. 133 of 2007. Article 2 revokes the Regulations of 1998 (S.I. No. 125 of 1998); article 12 provides an appeal to the Commissioner for Environmental Information and article 13 an appeal to the High Court on a point of law.

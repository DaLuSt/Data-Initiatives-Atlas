# Ley Estatutaria 1581 de 2012 (Colombia)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #483, Colombia). The text was read on the Senate's legal database (last updated 30 September 2026). `start_date` is the date of the law; its entry into force (the law's own date-of-effect article) was not checked. The page flags art. 19 as "condicionalmente exequible" (conditionally enforceable) following the Constitutional Court's review.

## What the text says

- **Art. 1:** the law develops the constitutional right of everyone to know, update and rectify the information collected on them in databases or files (art. 15 of the Constitution), and the right to information (art. 20).
- **Art. 19:** the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce, through a Delegatura for the Protection of Personal Data, supervises compliance. **Par. 1:** the Government was to set up a Delegate Superintendent's office within six months. **Par. 2:** supervision of data governed by Law 1266 of 2008 (financial habeas data) follows that law.
- Organised in nine titles (objects and definitions, principles, categories of data, data subjects' rights, duties of controllers and processors, the National Database Register, authority and procedure, international transfers, final provisions); not read in detail.

# Personal Data Protection and Privacy Bill, 2024 (The Gambia)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #472, The Gambia). The text read is the Bill as published by the National Assembly; it is filed as `proposed` because **assent was not established**. Secondary sources report the Assembly passing it on 29 September 2025 and disagree on whether it has become law; none was read.

## What the Bill provides

Parts on basic principles and lawful processing (including special categories and children's data and the conditions for consent), rights of the data subject (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, no solely automated decision), offences and penalties (unlawful processing, selling personal data, re-identification and others), and obligations of controllers and processors (transparency, joint controllers, data protection by design, impact assessments, breach notification, a data protection officer). The Bill defines "the Commission" as **the Information Commission established under the Access to Information Act**; it does not create a new authority in the passages read.

## Status

The interim framework, per a secondary source, is Part XIII of the Information and Communications Act 2009 with the Public Utilities Regulatory Authority as regulator; that was not read.

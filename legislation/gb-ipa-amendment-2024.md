# Investigatory Powers (Amendment) Act 2024

> **Created 2026-09-17**, closing a gap flagged on [[GB-IPA-2016]]'s
> own file. Both the Act's introductory text and its contents page were
> read directly at legislation.gov.uk this pass.

## Description

Confirmed by reading the Act's own introductory text directly at
legislation.gov.uk: it received **Royal Assent on 25 April 2024**, with
the long title "An Act to amend the Investigatory Powers Act 2016; to
make provision about information supplied by, or relating to, the
Judicial Commissioners; and for connected purposes."

## Structure, read from the contents page directly

The Act has six Parts and one Schedule:

- **Part 1** — Bulk personal datasets
- **Part 2** — Oversight arrangements
- **Part 3** — Communications data and internet connection records
- **Part 4** — Notices: retention, national-security and
  technical-capability notices, including a new power requiring
  operators of covered communications services to notify the
  government before making certain changes to their services
- **Part 5** — Miscellaneous: MPs' communications, equipment
  interference, freedom of information
- **Part 6** — General provisions
- **Schedule** — disclosure powers, restoration of certain powers, and
  consequential amendments

Every Part amends the 2016 Act's text rather than replacing it —
[[GB-IPA-2016]] continues to exist under its own name and date, which
is exactly the case the Atlas's `amends` relationship type was defined
for.

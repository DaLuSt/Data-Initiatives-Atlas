# European Commission adequacy decision for Argentina

> **Created 2026-11-07** (data-sharing links beyond Europe). The decision’s own text and the Commission’s adequacy page were read through a fetch tool that returns a model’s summary unless stated in the evidence; the Brazil decision was read in full from the Commission’s PDF.

## What it says

Commission Decision 2003/490/EC of 30 June 2003, adopted under Article 25(6) of Directive 95/46/EC (published in OJ L 168 of 5 July 2003), finding that Argentina provides an adequate level of protection for personal data transferred from the Community. It stays in force under Article 45(9) GDPR. Its Article 3 lets Member States' authorities suspend flows in specified cases and its Article 4 says it may be amended at any time in the light of experience.

It predates the GDPR and survives under Article 45(9) GDPR, which keeps decisions taken under Article 25(6) of Directive 95/46/EC in force until they are amended, replaced or repealed.

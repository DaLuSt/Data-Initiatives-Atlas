# Decision of the EEA Joint Committee No 55/2010

> **Created 2026-09-13**, closing `discovery/unresolved.md` row #108:

## Description

Confirmed via `europalov.no`'s own tracking page for the INSPIRE
directive, read directly: **EEA Joint Committee Decision No 55/2010**,
dated **30 April 2010**, amending **Annex XX (Environment)** to the EEA
Agreement, entering into force **1 July 2011**. It incorporates
**Directive 2007/2/EC** — [[EU-INSPIRE]] — into the Agreement, extending
it to Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway.

## Norway's implementing act, confirmed on its own text

Confirmed by reading `lovdata.no`'s own text of **LOV-2010-09-03-56**
directly: **"Lov om infrastruktur for geografisk informasjon
(geodataloven)"**, enacted **3 September 2010**, cites **"EØS-avtalen
vedlegg XX nr. 1j"** (EEA Agreement Annex XX point 1j) and incorporates
**"direktiv 2007/2/EF"** by its own Official Journal number — matching
`europalov.no`'s independent record of this Decision exactly. Now
modelled as [[NO-GEODATALOVEN]].

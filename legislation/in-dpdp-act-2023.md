# The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

> **Created 2026-11-01** (roadmap #470, country group 1). Known **only** from the Press Information Bureau's explainer of the Rules; **the Act's text was not read** (`meity.gov.in` is a JavaScript page, the Act's copy on its old address returns 404, and `indiacode.nic.in` and `egazette.gov.in` did not answer). `status: adopted` because the Rules give "an eighteen-month period for phased compliance" and commencement of each provision was not read; `start_date` is the enactment date the PIB gives, not a commencement date.

## What is sourced

"Parliament enacted the Digital Personal Data Protection Act on 11 August 2023." Key terms: Data Fiduciary, Data Principal (a parent or lawful guardian for a child, or the guardian of a person with a disability who cannot act independently), Data Processor, Consent Manager, and the Appellate Tribunal, the Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal (TDSAT), which hears appeals against the Board's decisions. Penalties: up to ₹250 crore for failing to maintain reasonable security safeguards; up to ₹200 crore each for not notifying the Board or affected individuals of a breach and for violating obligations relating to children; up to ₹50 crore for any other violation. The Act "follows the SARAL approach" (Simple, Accessible, Rational and Actionable).

# Law No. 058/2021 relating to the protection of personal data and privacy (Rwanda)

> **Created 2026-11-01** (roadmap #479, country group 10, Rwanda). The Gazette (82 pages, three languages) was read as extracted text: the contents, the definitions of 'supervisory authority' and 'competent authority', Chapter VI's heading and Article 70. `start_date` is the date of publication, 15 October 2021, which Article 70 makes the commencement; the Law is dated 13 October 2021. Articles 4 to 69 were not read closely: obligations, rights and transfer rules are not summarised beyond the headings. The Law may have been amended since; none was looked for.

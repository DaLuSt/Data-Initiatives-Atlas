# Wet structuur uitvoeringsorganisatie werk en inkomen (Wet SUWI)

> **Verified 2026-10-09.** The consolidated text on the Wettenbank (valid from 1 July 2026) was read directly. No date of entry into force is recorded: the Wettenbank text names the enacting act of 29 November 2001 but the commencement date was not read.

## Description

Article 2 establishes the UWV and article 3 the SVB, each with legal personality; article 4 makes the Kaderwet zelfstandige bestuursorganen applicable to both. Article 30 gives the UWV the work-disability, sickness-benefit and unemployment insurance and related acts; article 34 gives the SVB the general old-age pension, survivors' and child-benefit acts. Article 33 makes the UWV responsible for the *polisadministratie*, which records, per employee, the employment and benefit relationship including duration, wage and premiums, to base benefit decisions on.

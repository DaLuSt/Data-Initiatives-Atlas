# Directive on Open Government (Canada)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #472, Canada). The directive's page on the Treasury Board's policy suite was read directly on a runner. It is listed there under the "Policy on Service and Digital". **Type judgment:** it is filed as `subordinate-legislation` because it is a mandatory Treasury Board instrument for departments, not a statute; if the Atlas later wants a type for binding administrative policy, move it.

## What it says

It "takes effect on October 9, 2014" and applies to departments as defined in section 2 of the Financial Administration Act; other federal institutions are encouraged to use it. Its objective is "to maximize the release of government information and data of business value to support transparency, accountability, citizen engagement, and socio-economic benefits" — the page text read stops after "and socio..." and the rest of the sentence is not quoted. Its context section ties open government to the Access to Information Act (1983), the Federal Accountability Act (2006), Canada joining the Open Government Partnership in 2012 and endorsing the G8 Open Data Charter in June 2013.

The About Open Government page of `open.canada.ca` lists the directive beside the National Action Plan on Open Government, the Open Government licence and the Multi-Stakeholder Forum. The Open Government portal itself is not an entity here.

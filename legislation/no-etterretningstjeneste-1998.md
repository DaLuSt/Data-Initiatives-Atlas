# Lov om Etterretningstjenesten (1998)

## Description

Confirmed by reading lovdata.no's own record for LOV-1998-03-20-11
directly: **"Lov om Etterretningstjenesten"** was both enacted and
entered into force on **20 March 1998** — unlike its sibling
[[NO-FOREBYGGENDE-SIKKERHETSTJENESTE-1998]], passed the same day but not
commenced until 2001. It was **repealed 1 January 2021**, per resolution
of 10 November 2020 no. 2296, by the Act of 19 June 2020 no. 77 — now
modelled as [[NO-ETTERRETNINGSTJENESTELOVEN]], whose own file
independently cites the same repeal.

## What the Act covered

The Act's own seven sections, read directly:

1. **Purpose** — enabling the service to counter external threats and
   ensuring oversight control;
2. **Organisation** — integrated within Defence, its chief reporting to
   the Chief of Defence;
3. **Tasks** — intelligence collection on foreign threats to Norwegian
   interests, including terrorism, weapons proliferation, and support to
   military operations;
4. **Domestic scope limitations** — the Act's own text states the service
   "shall not on Norwegian territory monitor or otherwise covertly
   collect information" about Norwegian citizens or entities, except
   where directly connected to service operations;
5. **Archives** — secured, restricted access to collected intelligence;
6. **Oversight** — subject to the Storting's control committee under
   **separate 1995 legislation** (Norway's EOS Committee, not itself
   independently researched this pass, though [[NO-ETTERRETNINGSTJENESTELOVEN]]'s
   own file already cites `eos-utvalget.no` as a live source), with the
   King empowered to establish additional supervision mechanisms;
7. **Entry into force** — by Royal decree.

This is the founding statutory basis for the domestic/foreign
intelligence split the Atlas already records elsewhere for Norway
([[NO-ETTERRETNINGSTJENESTEN]] for foreign/military intelligence,
[[NO-PST]] for domestic security) — the 1998 Act's own §4 domestic-scope
restriction is the origin of that division, twenty-two years before the
current Act restated it.

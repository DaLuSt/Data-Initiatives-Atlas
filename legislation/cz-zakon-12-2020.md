# Zákon o právu na digitální služby a o změně některých zákonů (Act No 12/2020 Sb.)

## Description

Confirmed by reading `psp.cz` — the Chamber of Deputies' own legislative
record — directly: the Act's exact title is **"Zákon o právu na digitální
služby a o změně některých zákonů"** (Act on the Right to Digital
Services and on the Amendment of Certain Laws), published in **Sbírka
zákonů no. 5/2020** on **17 January 2020**, in force from **1 February
2020**. It was processed as **Bill No. 447** during the Chamber's 8th
electoral term (2017–2021).

Per its own title and [[CZ-DIA]]'s existing sourcing, the Act establishes
the right of natural and legal persons to obtain digital services from
public authorities and to perform digital acts, with a corresponding
obligation on public authorities to provide those services and accept
those acts.

## The amendment that created DIA

`psp.cz`'s own record lists the Act's amendment history, including
**Act 471/2022** — the same amendment [[CZ-DIA]]'s own file already cites
(via `cs.wikipedia.org`) as establishing the Digitální a informační
agentura with effect from 1 January 2023. This entity models the
original 2020 Act only; amendment 471/2022 is not separately modelled,
matching the threshold applied elsewhere to amending acts that are not
independently notable beyond the change they make.

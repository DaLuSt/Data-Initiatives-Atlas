# National Digital Identification Act 2024 (Samoa)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #471, Samoa). Known from the Samoa Bureau of Statistics' National ID page only. The Act's text (PacLII returns a 403 challenge) and the 2023 bill (the Parliament's PDF is a 404) could not be read, so its contents are not described.

## What is sourced

The Bureau's page says the National ID Division of the [[WS-SBS]] runs the system, whose purpose is "to establish and maintain the National Digital ID system for Samoan citizens and residents", and links the Act, giving its objective as providing "a modern national digital identification system". No commencement date is on the page.

## Not asserted

Search summaries say the Registrar General administers the Act and that it has an advisory board; neither was read. No privacy or data-protection provision of the Act is described here.

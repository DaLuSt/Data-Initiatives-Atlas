# Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 regarding the protection of personal data (UAE)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #475, United Arab Emirates). Read on the UAE Government's official portal, not in the legislation portal (`uaelegislation.gov.ae` returns a 403 challenge) — so the Atlas has the portal's description, not the articles.

## What is sourced

The portal gives the title "Federal Decree Law No. 45 of 2021 Regarding the Protection of Personal Data", states that it came into force on **2 January 2022**, and that it applies to the processing of personal data "whether in full or part through electronic systems", "inside or outside the country". It says "the UAE Data Office will act as the federal data regulator in the UAE" ([[AE-DATA-OFFICE]]), established by [[AE-DECREE-LAW-44-2021]]. The portal lists other data-related laws without describing them in detail: Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on consumer protection, Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 on ICT in health fields, Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021 on rumours and cybercrimes, and the Dubai Data Law. Secondary sources add that the law excludes free zones with their own data rules (DIFC, ADGM) and that executive regulations were expected; neither was read.

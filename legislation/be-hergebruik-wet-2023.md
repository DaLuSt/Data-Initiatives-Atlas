# Wet van 25 december 2023 (Belgian Open Data Directive transposition)

> **Verified 2026-08-26.** Read directly this pass: the act's own text
> (etaamb.openjustice.be), the consolidated Justel text of the 2016 act it
> amends (ejustice.just.fgov.be), the European Commission's own account of
> the Court of Justice referral, and the EUR-Lex register of national
> implementing measures. `verification: primary-source`. **One correction:**
> the act was **published in the Moniteur belge on 23 January 2024**, not
> 25 December 2023 as this entity previously stated — 25 December 2023 is
> the act's own date (promulgation), confirmed by the EC's implementing-
> measures register independently. news.belgium.be remains unread (bot-walled).

## Description

The Belgian **federal** transposition of [[EU-OPEN-DATA-DIRECTIVE]], enacted
not as a new act but as an amendment to [[BE-HERGEBRUIK-WET]], the act of
4 May 2016 that has been in the Atlas since the Belgium batch. Confirmed
directly from the consolidated text of the amended act: the amendment also
appears to have widened the 2016 act's own title, from "Wet inzake het
hergebruik van overheidsinformatie" to "Wet inzake open data en het
hergebruik van overheidsinformatie" — recorded here rather than changing
[[BE-HERGEBRUIK-WET]]'s entity name, which the Atlas keeps as originally
enacted.

## Why this entity exists

`discovery/research-queue.md` recorded, from the Belgium batch:

> *"The instrument transposing Directive (EU) 2019/1024 was **not
> identified**. [[BE-HERGEBRUIK-WET]] (2016) is not it. Belgium is the only
> Atlas country without one recorded."*

That was half right. The 2016 act is indeed not the transposition — but the
act that *amends* it is, and it did not exist when the Belgium batch ran.
Belgium's transposition was adopted on **20-21 December 2023** and finally
published on **23 January 2024**, more than two years after the Belgium
batch's sources were written.

## Twenty-nine months late by its own date, thirty by publication

The Directive's transposition deadline was **17 July 2021**. The sequence
that followed is unusually well documented — now confirmed directly from
the European Commission's own account — because Belgium is one of the four
member states that ran it to the end:

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 17 July 2021 | Transposition deadline |
| September 2021 | Commission sends letters of formal notice to the four member states, Belgium among them |
| April–June 2022 | Reasoned opinions issued |
| **15 February 2023** | Commission refers **Belgium, Bulgaria, Latvia and the Netherlands** to the Court of Justice |
| 20-21 December 2023 | Chamber of Representatives adopts this act |
| 25 December 2023 | Act dated (promulgated) |
| **23 January 2024** | **Published in the Moniteur belge** — confirmed this pass; corrects the entity's earlier claim that publication was 25 December 2023 |

Set against [[IE-PSI-REGULATIONS-2021]], which was five days late, the range
across the Atlas's member states for one directive is **five days to about
thirty months**.

## The federal state transposed last

Belgium's transposition is not one instrument but several, because the
competence is shared. The federal act recorded here is the **last** of them:

| Level | Instrument | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Flanders | [[BE-VL-BESTUURSDECREET-2021]] | 2 July 2021 (in force 17 July) |
| Brussels-Capital Region | [[BE-BRU-ORDONNANCE-2021]] | 10 December 2021 |
| Wallonia | [[BE-WAL-DECRET-2022]] (and an assent décret) | 24 November 2022 |
| **Federal** | **This act** | **25 Dec 2023 (dated) / 23 Jan 2024 (published)** |

**Flanders met the deadline.** Its decree was adopted on 2 July 2021 and in
force on 17 July 2021. Belgium was nonetheless referred to the Court of
Justice in February 2023, because a member state answers for the whole of
its territory and the federal legislator had not acted.

**All four are now modelled.** When this entity was written, only the federal
act was: the Atlas's `level` vocabulary used `regional` for the
**supra**-national tier — `level: regional` is what every EU instrument
carries — and had no value for a Belgian Region or Community.

`level: subnational` was added on 2026-08-21 for exactly that tier, and the
three sub-federal instruments are [[BE-VL-BESTUURSDECREET-2021]],
[[BE-BRU-ORDONNANCE-2021]] and [[BE-WAL-DECRET-2022]]. The Brussels
instrument amends [[BE-BRU-ORDONNANCE-2016]], which predates the directive by
two and a half years and is modelled too.

One correction the research produced: Flanders did not transpose *early*. Its
decree was **adopted** on 2 July 2021 and its open-data provisions **entered
into force on 17 July 2021** — the deadline itself, to the day.

# Data Protection and Privacy Act No. 13 of 2024 (Vanuatu)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #476, Vanuatu). The Act's English text was read in full from the CERT Vanuatu site (20 pages).

## What the text says

- **Dates.** The cover page gives *Assent: 05/12/2024* and *Commencement: 02/01/2025*. Section 32 says only that the Act "commences on the day on which it is published in the Gazette"; the Gazette itself was not seen, so 2 January 2025 is the cover page's date, not one checked against the Gazette.
- **Scope (s. 2).** Private and public sectors, automated or not, living individuals only; processing within Vanuatu's jurisdiction or of data generated or collected in Vanuatu wherever it is done; and processing of people in Vanuatu by a controller or processor not established there, when it relates to offering goods or services or monitoring behaviour in Vanuatu. Purely personal or household processing is excluded.
- **Structure.** Rules for processing (ss. 4-8: lawful purposes, special categories, children and vulnerable individuals, consent); data-subject rights (ss. 9-14: access, restriction, rectification and erasure, objection, no solely automated decisions, representation); transfers outside Vanuatu (ss. 15-17); enforcement (ss. 18-23: disclosure of documents, search warrants); offences (ss. 24-30); regulations by the Minister on the advice of the Deputy Commissioner (s. 31).
- **Who enforces it.** The Act's own definition: the Deputy Commissioner is "the Deputy Commissioner of Data Protection and Privacy appointed under the Digital Safety Authority Act No. 15 of 2024". That Act and the Authority it sets up were **not read**, so no organisation entity is created.

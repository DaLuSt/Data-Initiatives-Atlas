# Real Decreto-ley 24/2021 (Spanish Open Data Directive transposition)

> **Sourcing caveat.** Compiled from search-engine results only; the cited
> pages were confirmed to exist but were not read. `verification:
> search-only`.

## Description

Spain's transposition of [[EU-OPEN-DATA-DIRECTIVE]], carried in **Book
Three** of an omnibus decree-law that also transposes directives on covered
bonds, collective investment undertakings, copyright in online
transmissions, import exemptions, consumer protection and clean vehicles.

## Why this entity exists, when the edge already did

[[ES-LEY-37-2007]] has carried `implements-requirement-from`
[[EU-OPEN-DATA-DIRECTIVE]] since the Spain batch, with this decree-law named
in the evidence. The edge was right; the **instrument doing the
transposing** was not a node, so the graph attributed to a 2007 act
something no 2007 act could have done.

This entity separates the two. Ley 37/2007 is the standing Spanish re-use
regime; RDL 24/2021 is the act of transposition, dated after the Directive.
The `amends` edge between them says which is which.

## Four months late, under a letter of formal notice

The deadline was **17 July 2021**. Spain received a letter of formal notice
on **29 September 2021**, and the decree-law issued on **2 November 2021** —
five weeks later. Spanish commentary records the expiry of the deadline and
the letter of formal notice as the reason a *decree-law* was used rather
than an ordinary bill: the decree-law form exists for urgency, and the
infringement procedure supplied it.

Spain was one of the 19 member states served with letters of formal notice
that September. It did not reach the referral stage; [[BE-HERGEBRUIK-WET-2023]]
records the four member states that did.

## What Book Three changed in Ley 37/2007

Ten articles, one additional provision, the sole transitory provision and
the annex were modified, and three new articles introduced. The substantive
changes — scope extended to certain public undertakings, documents supplied
in open machine-readable formats with metadata, dynamic data through APIs
and bulk download, and the introduction of high-value datasets — are
recorded on [[ES-LEY-37-2007]], because that is the act that now contains
them.

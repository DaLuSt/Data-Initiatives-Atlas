# Decreto Legislativo N.° 1412 (Peru)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #478, Peru). The text was read from the Congress's copy (15 pages) and the PCM's page on gob.pe. `start_date` is the signing date in the text ("a los doce días del mes de setiembre del año dos mil dieciocho", President Martín Vizcarra); gob.pe lists 13 September 2018, probably the publication. A legislative decree issued under delegated powers has the force of law, so it is typed `act`. Commencement and the later amendments (for example Law 31686, on consular offices, seen in a search listing only) were not read.

## What the text says

- **Object (gob.pe):** the governance framework for digital identity, digital services, digital architecture, interoperability, digital security and data, and the legal regime for cross-cutting use of digital technologies by public bodies at the three levels of government.
- **Art. 8:** the Presidencia del Consejo de Ministros, through the Secretaría de Gobierno Digital, is the governing body ("ente rector") for digital government and "dicta las normas y establece los procedimientos". **Art. 9:** its functions include programming, directing, supervising and evaluating the application of digital government, proposing regulations and issuing guidelines and standards ([[PE-SGD]]).
- Public bodies adopt the standards the governing body sets for the exchange of data and information.

Its regulation is Supreme Decree 029-2021-PCM (seen in search results, not read).

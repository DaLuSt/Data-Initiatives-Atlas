# Ley N° 29733 (Peru)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #478, Peru). The law's text was read as published by Editora Perú in a consolidated edition on gob.pe. `start_date` is the date of promulgation in the edition read; Ministry sources and search listings give 3 July 2011 for publication, and the law's staged commencement was not checked, so entry into force may be later.

## What is sourced

- **Art. 32.** The Ministry of Justice, through the Dirección Nacional de Justicia, is the National Authority for Personal Data Protection ([[PE-ANPD]]); it may open offices across the country; it is governed by this law, its regulation and the Ministry's organisation rules. (The edition read says the directorate's denomination was changed by a later provision; the Ministry's own 2024 press note calls it "la Dirección General que ejerce la Autoridad Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales".)
- The regulation read in this edition is Supreme Decree 003-2013-JUS; it has been replaced by [[PE-DS-016-2024-JUS]].
- Not read in detail: the principles, the rights, the registry, the infringements and sanctions.

## Relationships

Applies in [[PE]]; names [[PE-ANPD]] as authority; its current regulation is [[PE-DS-016-2024-JUS]].

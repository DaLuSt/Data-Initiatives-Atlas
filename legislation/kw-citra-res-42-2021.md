# Resolution No. 42 of 2021 on the Data Privacy Protection Regulation (Kuwait)

> **Created 2026-11-01** (roadmap #479, Kuwait). `status` is `adopted`, not `active`: the Resolution was adopted in 2021, but a 2024 amendment reported only by search summaries may have changed its scope, and no page read says what is in force today. `start_date` is empty: the publication date was not read, and the date some summaries give (1 April 2021) is not on a page the Atlas read.

## What the text says

- **Preamble:** cites Law No. 37 of 2014 establishing the Authority as amended by Law No. 98 of 2015, a decree forming the Board of Directors, Cabinet Resolution No. 993 of 2015 issuing the Law's executive regulations, and the Board's approval at its meeting 4/2021 of 28 March 2021.
- **Article I:** the Regulation attached applies to the public and private sectors in the State of Kuwait.
- **Article II:** the competent authorities apply it, each within its purview; effective from publication in the Official Gazette and on the Authority's website.
- **Article III:** any contrary text or provision is void.

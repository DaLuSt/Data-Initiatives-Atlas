# Decreto Ejecutivo 285 de 2021 (Panama)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #479, Panama). Known only from ANTAI's FAQ page; the decree's text was not read. Panama has no row in `metadata/rank-basis.md`, so no `rank` is set.

## What is sourced

It is the regulation of [[PA-LEY-81-2019]] (date and number as ANTAI cites them). Art. 21: administrative complaints to ANTAI do not require a lawyer; the data subject can file, with contact details and a detailed account of the facts.

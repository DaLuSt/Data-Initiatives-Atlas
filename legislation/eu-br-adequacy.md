# European Commission adequacy decision for Brazil

> **Created 2026-11-07** (data-sharing links beyond Europe). The decision’s own text and the Commission’s adequacy page were read through a fetch tool that returns a model’s summary unless stated in the evidence; the Brazil decision was read in full from the Commission’s PDF.

## What it says

Commission Implementing Decision of 26 January 2026 (C(2026) 373 final), adopted under Article 45(3) GDPR, finding that Brazil ensures an adequate level of protection for personal data transferred from the European Union to controllers and processors in Brazil subject to the General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13.709 of 14 August 2018). The Commission’s adequacy page says the decisions were adopted as mutual ones on 27 January 2026. Article 3(4): the Commission evaluates the finding after four years from notification and at least every four years after; it can suspend, repeal or amend it (Article 3(5) and (6)).

It was adopted under the GDPR itself (Article 45(3)), so it carries a periodic review at least every four years (Article 45(3)).

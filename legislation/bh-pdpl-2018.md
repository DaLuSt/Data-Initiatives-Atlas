  Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Law, announced in July 2018 and in effect from 2019 according to the National Portal, whose
  summary says it bars obtaining or processing personal data without the data owner's express written consent unless the law
  provides otherwise, requires approval for transfers of personal data outside Bahrain, restricts automated linking and
  biometric processing without prior written approval, and gives data owners rights to information, correction, blocking and
  erasure. The Ministry of Justice, Islamic Affairs and Waqf is the authority designated to carry out the tasks of the Personal
  Data Protection Authority. The Law's text is published in Arabic only and was not read; the law number is not stated on the
  page read.

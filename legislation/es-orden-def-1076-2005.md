## Description

Confirmed by reading the BOE's own text directly: this order develops the
structure of the Estado Mayor de la Defensa (EMAD), under the broader
framework of Real Decreto 1551/2004 (which developed the Ministry of
Defence's basic organisational structure). Its **Article Sexto** is
dedicated entirely to the **Centro de Inteligencia de las Fuerzas
Armadas (CIFAS)**, now its own entity, [[ES-CIFAS]].

The order describes CIFAS as responsible for providing the Defence
Minister, through the Jefe de Estado Mayor de la Defensa (JEMAD), with
military intelligence, and states it has "carácter conjunto, será único
en materia de información e inteligencia militar en los niveles
estratégico y operacional" — a joint character, the sole authority on
military intelligence at the strategic and operational levels. Article
Sexto's own text presents CIFAS as an organisational restructuring under
the new defence command framework rather than as an entirely new body.

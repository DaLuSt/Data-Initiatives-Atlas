# Ley 21.855 (Chile)

> **Created 2026-11-23** (roadmaps #768 and #502). The law's full text was read directly in the Diario Oficial (two pages), and its passage in the Senate's public web service. **No `start_date`**: the law has no clause of its own on when it takes effect, and the general rule that sets it was not read; it was published on 23 November 2026.

## What the law does

- Sets the entry into force of [[CL-LEY-21719]] at **15 October 2027**, replacing "the first day of the 24th month after publication" (1 December 2026).
- Raises the council of [[CL-AGENCIA-PDP]] from three to five members; terms of six years, not renewable, with partial renewal every two years.
- The first proposal of councillors to the Senate is to be made at the latest nine months before Ley 21.719 takes effect. If fewer than twelve months remain at publication, it must be made within ten days of publication (second transitional article).

## Passage

From the Senate's web service (boletín 18623-07): introduced in the Senate on 1 September 2026 as a government bill; approved by the Senate in general and particular on 28 October 2026; approved by the Chamber of Deputies without changes on 17 November 2026; sent to the Executive the same day; promulgated 19 November and published 23 November 2026.

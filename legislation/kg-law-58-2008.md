# Law No. 58 of 14 April 2008 'On personal data' (Kyrgyz Republic)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #474, Kyrgyzstan). The English text on the agency's own register was read for its heading, its purpose clause and Article 3; the rest of the articles were not.

## What is sourced

Law No. 58 of 14 April 2008, amended by Law No. 129 of 20 July 2017 and Law No. 142 of 29 November 2021 (the amendments the register lists). It "is aimed at legal regulation of work with personal data", to protect the rights and freedoms of the person in collecting, processing and using it, in line with international principles and the Constitution. Article 3 defines "the authorized state body for personal data" ([[KG-DPA]]). The register's title for it is "On Personal Information"; the heading of the text is "On personal data". Secondary sources report later amendments in 2022 and 2025; they were not read.

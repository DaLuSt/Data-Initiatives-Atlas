# CEMAC directive on the rights of users of electronic communications (2008)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #551). The text of eight pages on the site of Cameroon's telecommunications regulator, read directly in full (French). **The copy is undated and unnumbered**: the reference reads "DIRECTIVE N° ___/08-UEAC-133-CM-18", the session date and the signature date are blank ("BANGUI, le"), and it is signed in print by the Council's President, Emmanuel Bizot. So `start_date` is empty and `status: adopted` rests on the text's own words ("ADOPTE LA DIRECTIVE"). Filed as `decision`, the nearest type for a binding act of a regional body (the Atlas has no CEMAC-directive type; see #457).

## What it says

Nine chapters, 23 articles. Chapter 2 (privacy of users): confidentiality of communications and traffic data (Article 3), caller identification (4), automatic call forwarding (5), unsolicited communications (6), directories (7). Chapter 7, **processing of personal data**: traffic data (Article 16) and location data other than traffic data (17). Chapter 8, **cybersecurity and cybercrime**: protection of electronic communications networks (18). Final provisions: Member States implement it (19) and report (20); revision (22), and within three years of entry into force Member States are to consider replacing it with a new directive; Article 23: it enters into force on the date of its signature and is published in the Community's Official Bulletin. Its recitals cite the World Summit on the Information Society (Tunis, November 2005) commitments on network security, cybercrime and privacy.

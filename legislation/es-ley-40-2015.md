# Ley 40/2015 — Régimen Jurídico del Sector Público

> **Added 2026-09-04, `verification: primary-source` from creation.**
> `discovery/research-queue.md` had queued this law, alongside
> [[ES-LEY-39-2015]], as Spain's current legal base for much of what
> [[ES-AEAD]] does. Both `boe.es`'s own consolidated text and [[ES-AEAD]]'s
> own founding decree were read directly this pass, and together they
> establish exactly that relationship as a sourced fact, not an
> inference.

## Description

Ley 40/2015 governs the organisation and internal functioning of the
Spanish public sector — published in the BOE (no. 236) on **2 October
2015**, in force from **2 October 2016**, the same dates as its
procedural counterpart [[ES-LEY-39-2015]]. It consolidated provisions
previously scattered across **Ley 30/1992** and **Ley 6/1997** (LOFAGE,
the general state administration organisation act).

## The legal form behind ES-AEAD

This law's **Título II, Capítulo III, Sección 4.ª** defines **"agencias
estatales"** — state agencies — as entities of public law with their
own legal personality, assets and management autonomy. Reading
[[ES-AEAD]]'s own founding decree directly, **Real Decreto 1118/2024**,
Article 1.1 states in as many words: **"La Agencia Estatal de
Administración Digital... es una entidad de Derecho público regulada en
la sección 4.ª del capítulo III del título II de la Ley 40/2015"** (the
State Agency for Digital Administration is a public-law entity
regulated under section 4 of chapter III of title II of Ley 40/2015).
The decree's own preamble adds that this section "configura las
agencias estatales como entidades de Derecho público, dotadas de
personalidad jurídica pública, patrimonio propio y autonomía en su
gestión" (configures state agencies as public-law entities, endowed
with public legal personality, their own assets and management
autonomy). This is the legal-basis gap `discovery/research-queue.md`
had recorded: [[ES-AEAD]] now carries a `governed-by` edge to this law.

A narrower finding worth recording: the "agencias estatales" category
was not continuously available under this law. Secondary legal sources
report that Ley 40/2015 originally **suppressed** state agencies from
the institutional public sector, and that a later amending act, **Ley
11/2020**, reintroduced Section 4.ª to bring the category back — not
independently confirmed by a direct read of Ley 11/2020 this pass, so
recorded as a caveat rather than a fully sourced fact.

[[ES-AESIA]] is the same legal form under this law's slightly
differently-cited range: its own founding decree, Real Decreto
729/2023, places it under "los artículos 108 bis a 108 sexies" of Ley
40/2015 rather than citing the section number by name, and states
directly "La Agencia se rige por la Ley 40/2015." Two of the Atlas's
Spanish national agencies are now sourced to the same statutory chassis.

## Interoperability, and the still-unlinked ENI

Article 3.2, read directly on `boe.es`, requires that public
administrations relate to one another **"a través de medios
electrónicos, que aseguren la interoperabilidad y seguridad de los
sistemas"** (through electronic means that ensure interoperability and
system security). [[ES-ENI]]'s own entity separately found, via
anabad.org, that its **Article 156** specifically contemplates the
Esquema Nacional de Interoperabilidad's existence. No relationship is
asserted from this entity to [[ES-ENI]], for the reason [[ES-ENI]]'s own
file already gives: "contemplated by" is not a distinction the Atlas's
relationship vocabulary or the source read supports as a typed edge.

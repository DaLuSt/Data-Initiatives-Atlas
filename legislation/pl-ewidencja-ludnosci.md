# Ustawa o ewidencji ludności (2010)

> **Sourcing caveat.** `isap.sejm.gov.pl` — the Sejm's own legal-text
> database, and the Atlas's usual source for an exact Dziennik Ustaw
> promulgation citation — returns an Incapsula "Pardon Our Interruption"
> JavaScript challenge page to every fetch tried in this environment,
> with a browser-spoofing User-Agent and an honest, identifying one
> alike. This is a genuine block, unlike the `efta.int` finding earlier
> the same day: no User-Agent tried got past it. The Act's **original**
> 2010 promulgation citation (Dz.U. 2010 nr 217 poz. 1427, guessed from
> ISAP's own numbering convention but never read) is therefore **not
> asserted** — this entity carries only the **consolidated-text**

## Description

Confirmed verbatim by reading gov.pl's own "Czym jest numer PESEL" page
directly (2026-08-22): the legal basis ("Podstawa prawna") for the PESEL
number is "Ustawa z dnia 24 września 2010 r. o ewidencji ludności (Dz. U.
z 2015 r. poz. 388)" together with a 2012 ministerial regulation on
assigning and changing the number. Confirmed independently by reading
pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/PESEL directly: "Od 1 marca 2015 podstawę prawną
funkcjonowania systemu stanowi ustawa o ewidencji ludności z 24 września
2010" — since 1 March 2015, this Act has been the legal basis for the
system.

## An eleven-year gap, closed twice

Confirmed by reading pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/PESEL directly (2026-08-22):
"Dla okresu od 1970 do 2000 roku niejasna jest ustawowa podstawa dla
funkcjonowania systemu" — for the period 1970–2000, the statutory basis
for the system's operation is unclear. A 1997 amendment (in force
1 January 2001) to an earlier 1974 act on population records and identity
cards first introduced language referencing the PESEL number. This
2010 Act, in force as the operative basis since **1 March 2015**,
replaced that arrangement.

## Launched alongside two companion acts

Confirmed by reading pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/PESEL directly (2026-08-22):
"1 marca 2015 weszły w życie trzy nowe ustawy: o dowodach osobistych, o
ewidencji ludności oraz Prawo o aktach stanu cywilnego" — three new acts
took effect the same day, on identity cards, population records and
civil-status records. Together they underpin the **System Rejestrów
Państwowych** (State Registers System), launched the same day and run by
[[PL-COI]], which integrates the PESEL register, identity-card records
and civil-status records.

## Not modelled

- The **1974 act** on population records and identity cards, and its
  **1997 amendment** — the arrangement this Act replaced.
- The companion **identity-card act** and **Prawo o aktach stanu
  cywilnego** (civil status act), which took effect the same day.
- The **System Rejestrów Państwowych** itself as a separate entity, as
  opposed to [[PL-COI]], the body that runs it.

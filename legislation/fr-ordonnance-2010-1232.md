## Description

An omnibus ordinance adapting French law to several unrelated pieces of
EU environmental law at once. Reading `legifrance.gouv.fr`'s own text
directly: **Article 1** transposes [[EU-INSPIRE]] (Directive 2007/2/EC),
adding a new **Chapter VII** — "De l'infrastructure d'information
géographique" — to the **Code de l'environnement**, introducing
**Articles L. 127-1 to L. 127-10** covering geographic data series,
metadata obligations, interoperability standards for geographic
services, and public access through internet portals, for the data
themes listed in the directive's Annexes I, II and III.

The ordinance's other articles are unrelated to INSPIRE: **Article 2**
extends the EU emissions trading system to aircraft operators, **Article
3** updates chemical-substance classifications under Regulation (EC) No
1272/2008, and **Article 5** creates provisions on geological CO2
storage and third-party access to storage sites. Only Article 1 is
relevant to this Atlas.

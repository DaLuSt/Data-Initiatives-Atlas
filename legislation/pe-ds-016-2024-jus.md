# Decreto Supremo N° 016-2024-JUS (Peru)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #478, Peru). Known from two Ministry of Justice press notes; the decree's text was not read, so no article is described beyond what the notes quote. Peru has no row in `metadata/rank-basis.md`, so no `rank` is set.

## What the notes say

- Published 30 November 2024 in El Peruano; in force 31 March 2025.
- A "profunda actualización" of the rules: impact assessment as a preventive mechanism, codes of conduct, reporting of security incidents involving personal data (48 hours in principle), security aligned with the most recent ISO standards (NTP-ISO/IEC 27001), and the duty to inform data subjects of their level of exposure.
- Art. 26: a company may make a single call to ask for consent for advertising calls, under strict conditions; use of data from unlawful sources is fined 50 to 100 UIT.
- Issued on the initiative of the Ministry of Justice, from the directorate that exercises the Authority ([[PE-ANPD]]).

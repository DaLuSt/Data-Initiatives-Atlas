# Single Digital Gateway Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2018/1724)

## Description

Regulation (EU) 2018/1724 of 2 October 2018 establishes a single digital
gateway providing online access to information, administrative procedures,
and assistance and problem-solving services. It aims to improve the
functioning of the internal market by addressing fragmentation in online
services for citizens and businesses operating across the EU. It has applied
since **12 December 2020**, recorded as `start_date`.

## Relevance assessment

The brief asks that relevance be assessed rather than assumed. The SDG
Regulation earns inclusion through the **once-only principle** it operates
on: cross-border administrative procedures require member states to exchange
evidence directly between authorities rather than asking citizens to
re-supply it, which makes it a data-sharing instrument and not merely a
portal mandate.

That said, the once-only mechanism was **not researched in detail** here,
and `coverage: low` reflects it. The Dutch implementation of the gateway is
likewise unresearched — a plausible connection to [[NL-GDI]] and
[[NL-LOGIUS]] exists but is not sourced, so no relationship is asserted.

# Data Protection Act, 2011 (Trinidad and Tobago)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #478, Trinidad and Tobago). The text was read from a copy hosted by the Central Bank. Its assent line is blank ("Assented to , 2011"), so the copy may be the Bill as passed, and **no date is recorded**. It is `adopted`, not `active`, because the Act says it "shall come into operation on such day as is fixed by the President by Proclamation" and the proclamation was not read; following the precedent of the Swiss e-ID law, **no `applies-in` edge to [[TT]] is asserted**, only a `related-to` edge to the anchor, which says it is an Act of that country's Parliament and nothing about whether it applies.

## What the text says

- Long title: an Act "to provide for the protection of personal privacy and information".
- Section 1(2): commencement by Presidential Proclamation.
- Part II sets up the Office of the Information Commissioner (s. 8): an Information Commissioner, head of the Office, appointed by the President, with prescribed qualifications; Deputy Information Commissioners.
- The Act defines `data` broadly (documents, correspondence, plans and so on). The rest was not read.

## Not recorded (leads only)

Search summaries say the Act was proclaimed in part: sections 1 to 6, 7 to 18, 22, 23, 25(1), 26, 28 and 42(a) and (b), by Legal Notices 2 of 2012 and 220 of 2021 (one source says only Parts I and II), and that the Office of the Information Commissioner is not yet established.

# Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #483, Malaysia). Known from the Department's home page; the Act's text was not read, so **no date is recorded** (the page does not give one) and later amendments are not described.

## What the page says

Applies to anyone who "memproses dan mempunyai kawalan terhadap atau membenarkan pemprosesan apa-apa data peribadi berkenaan dengan transaksi komersial"; it is one of the cyber laws accompanying the Multimedia Super Corridor; it sets seven principles of personal data protection.

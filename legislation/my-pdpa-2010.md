# Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #483, Malaysia). Known from the Department's home page. **Dated 2026-11-28** (roadmap #775): the Act's bilingual text on the Department's site gives Royal Assent (2 June 2010) and Gazette publication (10 June 2010), and section 1(2) leaves commencement to the Minister; the Department's page on the commencement order gives 15 November 2013. Later amendments, including the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024, are not described.

## What the page says

Applies to anyone who "memproses dan mempunyai kawalan terhadap atau membenarkan pemprosesan apa-apa data peribadi berkenaan dengan transaksi komersial"; it is one of the cyber laws accompanying the Multimedia Super Corridor; it sets seven principles of personal data protection.

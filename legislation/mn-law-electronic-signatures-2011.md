# Law of Mongolia on electronic signatures

> **Created 2026-11-01** (roadmap #482). Known from the Commission's list only; the date is presumed to be the adoption date. `start_date` is empty: no commencement date was read.

> **Answered 2026-11-22** (roadmap #775, closing `discovery/unresolved.md` row #286). legalinfo.mn's own page for this law, read directly, is headed 'МОНГОЛ УЛСЫН ХУУЛЬ 2011 оны 12 дугаар сарын 15-ны өдөр' and prints the repealing law: Article 1, '2011 оны 12 дугаар сарын 15-ны өдөр баталсан Цахим гарын үсгийн тухай хуулийг хүчингүй болсонд тооцсугай' (the law adopted on 15 December 2011 is declared invalid); Article 2, it applies from the day the revised edition takes effect, which its own Article 35.1 sets at 1 May 2022. So this entity is `superseded` with `end_date` 2022-05-01, and [[MN-LAW-ELECTRONIC-SIGNATURES-2021]] is the law now in force. The Commission's list still shows this law's 2011 date with a link to the revised edition.

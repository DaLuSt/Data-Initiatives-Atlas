# Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (Tanzania)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #483, Tanzania). The gazetted Official English Version was read as text (about 1,800 lines). `start_date` is the commencement date printed under the long title (`[1st May, 2023]`, by GN No. 326 of 2023); the Act was enacted in 2022, in Kiswahili, as Act No. 11 of 2022, and its enactment date is not on the page read. The text read was not compared line by line with the Kiswahili original, and only the Kiswahili text is the one enacted.

## What the text says

- **Long title:** principles of protection of personal data "so as to establish minimum requirements for the collection and processing of personal data"; establishment of the Personal Data Protection Commission; improved protection of data processed by public and private bodies.
- **Application (s. 2):** Mainland Tanzania and Zanzibar, except non-union matters in Zanzibar.
- **Commission (Part II, ss. 6-13):** established as a body corporate with perpetual succession (s. 6); its functions include monitoring compliance by data controllers and processors and registering them (s. 7); it has staff (s. 13), powers of investigation (s. 42) and its own sources of funds (s. 51). See [[TZ-PDPC]].
- Data protection officers, codes of ethics (s. 65) and the rest of the Act were not read in detail.

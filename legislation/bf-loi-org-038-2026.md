# Loi organique n° 038-2026/ALP (Burkina Faso)

> **Read 2026-11-09** (re-verification). The decree was downloaded from the CIL's own list and read as page images: decree of 22 September 2026; articles 1 to 5 name the body ARCOD and set its mission; article 72 keeps the CIL and the Conseil supérieur de la communication competent until the ARCOD is effectively set up; article 73 repeals organic law 041-2023/ALT on the Conseil supérieur. Its entry into force and the ARCOD's actual set-up date were not read. `status` stays `adopted`.

> **Created 2026-10-17** (roadmap #477, Burkina Faso). Known only from the title on the CIL's "Lois" page; the promulgation decree (No. 2026-1035/PF) is a scanned PDF with no text. **Nothing about its content, its commencement or whether it merges the CIL with a communications regulator is recorded**; the title speaks of one "organe de régulation de la communication et de la protection des données à caractère personnel".

## Description

The Archiefwet 1995 governs Dutch government record-keeping. Its central
requirement is that government organisations put and keep their information
in good, orderly and accessible condition — findable and consultable. It
determines which information must be archived (everything arising from
government tasks), who is responsible (the administration responsible for
those tasks), retention periods (set per organisation in a selection list),
when transfer occurs (in principle within twenty years), and who may consult
the material after transfer (in principle everyone, unless someone's
interests would be harmed).

Records are transferred to [[NL-NATIONAAL-ARCHIEF]] or to a local or
regional archive service.

It is being repealed and replaced by [[NL-ARCHIEFWET-2026]] — no longer just
a scheduled bill. Confirmed by reading the successor act's own Staatsblad
text directly this pass (as part of re-verifying that entity): the
replacement act was signed 13 May 2026 and published as Stb. 2026, 149 on
19 June 2026, titled "Wet van 13 mei 2026 tot intrekking van de Archiefwet
1995 en vervanging door een nieuwe Archiefwet." Its own text defers the
exact entry-into-force date to a royal decree still to be issued;
nationaalarchief.nl's and rijksoverheid.nl's own pages, both read directly
this pass, both state the intended date as 1 January 2027, at which point
the transfer period shortens from twenty years to ten.

## Relationships

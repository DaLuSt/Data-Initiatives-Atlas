# Central European Free Trade Agreement (CEFTA 2006)

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #542, Southern Europe's regional bodies). Sourced from CEFTA's own site and from the Joint Committee's own decisions, all read directly. The Agreement's text and its entry-into-force date were not read.

## Description

CEFTA 2006 is the free trade agreement of seven economies of the Western Balkans and Moldova. The Joint Committee's decisions cite it as "the Agreement on Amendment of and Accession to the Central European Free Trade Agreement, done in Bucharest on 19 December 2006". CEFTA's own page says it "aims at enhancing trade in goods and services", "seeks to eliminate trade barriers between the Parties and attract investment" and aims "to harmonize its Parties' regulatory framework". Its home page says the Parties "have committed to establishing a regional economic area on the basis of EU compliance".

## Parties

Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, North Macedonia, Moldova, Montenegro, Serbia and UNMIK/Kosovo. The site's own footnote: this designation "is without prejudice to positions on status, and is in line with UNSCR 1244 and the ICJ Opinion on the Kosovo declaration of independence". Romania, Bulgaria and Croatia withdrew on joining the EU.

## What it carries in the Atlas

Its Joint Committee ([[INTL-CEFTA-JC]]) has adopted three decisions on digital trade: [[INTL-CEFTA-JC-2-2024]] (electronic commerce), [[INTL-CEFTA-JC-10-2025]] (a working group on digital cooperation) and [[INTL-CEFTA-JC-11-2025]] (recognition of advanced electronic signatures and seals). Additional Protocol 6 on Trade in Services, on which they rest, was not read.

## Description

The act constituting [[PL-SKW]] and [[PL-SWW]], Poland's two **military**
special services — the military mirror of [[PL-UABWAW-2002]], four years
later and on the same internal/external axis.

## The official citation, found 2026-09-05

[[PL-UABWAW-2002]] is the best-sourced statute in this batch: ISAP, the
ABW's own BIP, the AW's own legal-framework page. This act, from the same
legislature four years later, previously had **none of that** — no
official Polish government URL had been found by search.

A fresh search located one. `eli.gov.pl`'s own European Legislation
Identifier API, read directly, opens with the exact title this entity
already carried: "Ustawa z dnia 9 czerwca 2006 r. o Służbie Kontrwywiadu
Wojskowego oraz Służbie Wywiadu Wojskowego" — the same act, confirmed
from a government-run legislative-identifier service rather than a third
party. `isap.sejm.gov.pl`'s own page for the same act (document ID
WDU20061040709, Dz.U. 2006 Nr 104 poz. 709) is confirmed genuinely
CAPTCHA-blocked, as it was for [[PL-UABWAW-2002]] on earlier attempts —
the Dz.U. citation is taken from that page's own URL/ID structure rather
than a direct read of its content, which the eli.gov.pl direct read does
not itself state.

## The article-level content, found 2026-09-18

`dziennikustaw.gov.pl`'s own scanned copy of Dziennik Ustaw 2006 Nr 104,
poz. 709, read directly, gives the Act's own text from Article 1 onward:

- **Article 1** creates SKW as a special service and state-protection
  service responsible for internal threats to the defence, security and
  combat capability of the Polish Armed Forces (SZ RP) and other
  organisational units subordinate to or supervised by the Minister of
  National Defence.
- **Article 2** creates SWW in mirror-image terms, for **external**
  threats to the same interests.
- **Article 3.1** makes the Head of SKW and the Head of SWW central
  organs of government administration, each acting with the assistance
  of their respective service, which is a government administration
  office.
- **Article 3.2** subordinates (*podlegają*) both Heads to the **Minister
  of National Defence** — not modelled as its own Atlas entity, matching
  the project's convention of not modelling ministries — subject to
  certain powers reserved to the Prime Minister or a Minister-Coordinator
  of Special Services where one is appointed.
- **Article 3.3** states plainly: *"Działalność Szefa SKW oraz Szefa SWW
  podlega kontroli Sejmu"* — the activity of the Head of SKW and the Head
  of SWW is subject to **Sejm control** — the same parliamentary
  oversight layer already modelled for [[PL-UABWAW-2002]]'s two civilian
  services via the Sejm's Committee for Special Services.
- **Article 5** lists SKW's tasks in detail (counter-espionage,
  counter-terrorism, protection of classified information, cooperation
  with the Military Gendarmerie, radio-electronic counter-intelligence,
  and others).

This is the first time either [[PL-SKW]] or [[PL-SWW]] has been sourced
from the Act's own words rather than from pages describing the service.

# Wet basisregistratie adressen en gebouwen

> **Verified 2026-08-21.** Every cited source was read and confirmed to
> support what this entity says. `verification: primary-source`.

## Description

The statutory basis of [[NL-BAG]], the base registration of addresses and
buildings. BWBR0023466 and the official abbreviation `Wet BAG` are both
confirmed on the `wetten.overheid.nl` metadata page.

## The 2018 modernisation

The act of **10 February 2017** (Stb. 2017, 60) amended the Wet BAG and other
acts *"regarding the modernisation and simplification of registration and
supervision"*, and entered into force on **1 July 2018** — the change usually
called **BAG 2.0**. Confirmed on the geobasisregistraties.nl page, which
cites the same act by the same title, date and Staatsblad number, and on the
statute text itself, where the pre-2018 provisions are marked *"Vervallen per
01-07-2018"*.

## Correction

`start_date` was previously left `null`, on the reasoning that only the 2017
amendment's date was known. The original act's own "Origineel opschrift en
aanhef" on `wetten.overheid.nl` reads *"Wet van 24 januari 2008, houdende
regels omtrent de basisregistraties adressen en gebouwen"* — the original
Wet BAG was enacted 24 January 2008, which is now recorded as `start_date`.

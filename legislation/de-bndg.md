# BND-Gesetz (BNDG)

## Description

The BNDG constitutes [[DE-BND]] and defines its tasks and powers. It is one
of Germany's **three service-specific acts**, with [[DE-BVERFSCHG]] and
[[DE-MADG]]; [[DE-G10]] cuts across all three.

## The most amended act in this batch

The Bundestag's own archive records substantive revisions in **2021** and
again in **2023**, the latter described as a reform of the legal bases of
the intelligence services generally rather than of the BND alone.

A third change came from the courts rather than the legislature: following
the Federal Constitutional Court's decision of **28 September 2022**, the
provisions on the [[DE-UKR]] were to be **removed from this act**. Where
they were relocated could not be established, which is why [[DE-UKR]]
carries no `governed-by` edge and `confidence: low`.

Nothing about the *content* of the 2021 or 2023 amendments was established
here. This entity records that the act has moved recently and does not
describe how.

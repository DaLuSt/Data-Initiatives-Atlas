# BND-Gesetz (BNDG)

> **Verified 2026-08-22.** The statute text at gesetze-im-internet.de and
> the Bundestag's 2021 and 2023 textarchiv articles were read directly.
> Egress to gesetze-im-internet.de is not blocked in this environment —
> the earlier caveat about that was wrong.

## Description

Confirmed by reading the BNDG statute text (2026-08-22), § 1(1): "Der
Bundesnachrichtendienst ist eine Bundesoberbehörde im Geschäftsbereich des
Bundeskanzleramtes ... Der Bundesnachrichtendienst sammelt zur Gewinnung
von Erkenntnissen über das Ausland, die von außen- und sicherheitspolitischer
Bedeutung für die Bundesrepublik Deutschland sind." The BNDG constitutes
[[DE-BND]] and defines its tasks and powers. It is one
of Germany's **three service-specific acts**, with [[DE-BVERFSCHG]] and
[[DE-MADG]]; [[DE-G10]] cuts across all three.

## The most amended act in this batch, and now the reason why

The Bundestag's own archive records substantive revisions in **2021** and
again in **2023**, the latter described as a reform of the legal bases of
the intelligence services generally rather than of the BND alone.

The 2021 amendment's origin is now confirmed rather than merely dated.
Reading the Bundestag's 2021 textarchiv article (2026-08-22): the Federal
Constitutional Court, by decision of **19 May 2020** (1 BvR 2835/17), found
several BNDG provisions on *Ausland-Ausland-Fernmeldeaufklärung* (foreign-
to-foreign signals-intelligence) incompatible with Articles 5 and 10 of the
Basic Law, and set a deadline of end-2021 for a constitutional replacement.
The government's response created the [[DE-UKR]]: "ein neu einzurichtender
Unabhängiger Kontrollrat ... erhält [die] Kompetenz zur umfassenden
Kontrolle der Rechtmäßigkeit der gesamten technischen Aufklärung durch den
BND."

A third change came from the courts rather than the legislature: following
a **further** Federal Constitutional Court decision of **28 September
2022**, the provisions on the [[DE-UKR]] were to be **removed from this
act**. [[DE-UKR]]'s own re-verification (2026-08-22) found where they are
headed — a dedicated "Gesetz über den Unabhängigen Kontrollrat" — though
not whether that transfer has yet completed.

Nothing about the *content* of the 2023 amendment was established here.
This entity records that the act has moved recently and only partially
describes how.

## Description

Regulation (EU) 2024/1083, the **European Media Freedom Act**, was
**adopted on 11 April 2024** and published in the Official Journal on
**17 April 2024**. Confirmed by reading its own text directly on
EUR-Lex: it establishes "a common framework for media services in the
internal market," addressing an internal market for media services the
Regulation's own recitals describe as "insufficiently integrated" due to
digitalisation, platform gatekeeping and divergent national rules.

## Creates the European Board for Media Services

The Regulation establishes the **European Board for Media Services**,
described in its own text as "an independent advisory body at Union
level gathering national regulatory authorities or bodies and
coordinating their actions" — **replacing ERGA** (the European
Regulators Group for Audiovisual Media Services). Confirmed
independently on the Board's own official site (`media-board.europa.eu`,
read directly): it was **officially established on 10 February 2025**.

## Main obligations applicable from 8 August 2025

Confirmed directly on the Board's own site: the Regulation's "core
provisions... officially enter into application" on **8 August 2025**,
with one exception — the provision on users' right to customise their
media offering — **deferred to 2027**.

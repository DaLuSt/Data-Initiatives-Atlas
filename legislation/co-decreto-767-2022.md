# Decreto 767 de 2022 (Colombia)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #483, Colombia). The decree was read on the ministry's legal compilation, which warns that its validity must be checked article by article against the provisions it amends; later changes were not checked. `start_date` is its date and the date it took effect (art. 2: on publication in the Diario Oficial). Colombia has no row in `metadata/rank-basis.md`, so no `rank` is set.

## What the text says

- **Object:** the general guidelines of the Digital Government Policy, "el uso y aprovechamiento de las Tecnologías de la Información y las Comunicaciones" to improve citizens' quality of life and competitiveness through public value and the digital transformation of the State.
- **Who is bound:** the public administration bodies of art. 39 of Law 489 of 1998 and private parties performing administrative functions; in the legislative and judicial branches, control bodies and autonomous bodies the policy is implemented through a scheme of coordination (paragraph, truncated in the read).
- **Monitoring:** through the FURAG survey (or its successor) under Decree 1083 of 2015, with continuous-improvement evaluation; a Digital Government Manual consolidates guidance and is coordinated with CONPES guidelines.
- **Basis:** recitals cite art. 147 of Law 1955 of 2019 (the 2018-2022 National Development Plan) on digital transformation projects.

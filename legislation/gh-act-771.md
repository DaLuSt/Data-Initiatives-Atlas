# Act 771 (Ghana, 2008)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #481, Ghana). Known from one sentence on NITA's About page; neither the Act's short title nor its text was read, so the name is a description, and **no date is recorded** beyond the month ("December 2008").

## What is sourced

It established [[GH-NITA]], succeeding the Ghana ICT Directorate (GICTeD), "to further government's ICT policy initiative, regulate and promote information and communications technologies and digital services". NITA's role also covers licensing and certifying ICT service providers and professionals and enforcing standards.

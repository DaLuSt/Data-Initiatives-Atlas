# Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744)

## Description

Confirmed by reading EUR-Lex's own text directly: the full title is
**"Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 of the European Parliament and of the
Council of 8 July 2026 amending Regulations (EU) 2024/1689, (EU)
2018/1139 and (EU) 2023/1230 as regards the simplification of the
implementation of harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Digital
Omnibus on AI)."** It amends three regulations: the [[EU-AI-ACT]]
(2024/1689), the EASA Regulation (2018/1139, civil aviation) and the
Machinery Regulation (2023/1230).

Adopted **8 July 2026**, published in the Official Journal **24 July
2026**, in force from **27 July 2026** — six days before the AI Act's
original 2 August 2026 high-risk deadline. It defers that deadline
(Annex III standalone high-risk AI systems) to **2 December 2027**; the
Annex I product-embedded AI deadline (2 August 2028) is unchanged, as are
the Article 50 transparency duties and the general-purpose-AI and
prohibited-practices regimes already in force.

## Not the same instrument as EU-DIGITAL-OMNIBUS

This regulation originated as **COM(2025) 836** ("Digital Omnibus on
AI"), published 19 November 2025 the same day as the Commission's
broader Digital Omnibus proposal, **COM(2025) 837** — the entity this
Atlas models as [[EU-DIGITAL-OMNIBUS]], covering the Data Governance Act,
Open Data Directive and GDPR. The two proposals were tracked, negotiated
and adopted independently. [[EU-DIGITAL-OMNIBUS]] does not amend the AI
Act; this entity does.

## A corrected date

[[EU-AI-ACT]]'s own entity, in a prior pass, recorded this regulation's
adoption as 29 June 2026 (following a European Parliament plenary vote of
16 June 2026), sourced via WebSearch cross-checks of legal trackers since
EUR-Lex itself was then unreadable. EUR-Lex's own text, read directly
this pass, gives the regulation's own date as **8 July 2026** — the date
recorded here and the one that should be treated as authoritative.

# Cyber and Data Protection Act (Zimbabwe)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #478, Zimbabwe). The text was read as published by the regulator (12 pages). `start_date` is the commencement date printed under the long title. The copy is the Chapter 12:07 edition after the Law Reviser's re-gazetting; the Act was originally titled the Data Protection Act (the regulator's legislation page lists it as "Data Protection Act 5 of 2021").

## What the text says

- **Long title:** to provide for data protection with regard to the Declaration of Rights and the public and national interest; to establish a **Cyber Security Centre** and a **Data Protection Authority** and their functions; to create a technology-driven business environment; to amend sections 162 to 166 of the Criminal Law (Codification and Reform) Act [Chapter 9:23] on investigating and collecting evidence of cyber-crime and unauthorised data collection and on admissibility of electronic evidence.
- **Object (s. 2):** to increase cyber security and build confidence and trust in the secure use of ICT by data controllers, their representatives and data subjects.
- **Parts:** I preliminary; II the Data Protection Authority (s. 5 designates POTRAZ, s. 6 its functions: regulating lawful processing, enforcing fair processing, issuing opinions, advising the Minister, inquiries and investigations, receiving and investigating complaints, research); III quality of data; IV general rules on processing (including sensitive, genetic, biometric and health data); V duties of controllers and processors (rights of data subjects, disclosures, security and breach notification, notification to the Authority, authorisation); VI data subject (automated decisions, children, incapacitated persons); VII transborder flow (ss. 28-29); VIII code of conduct; and later parts to s. 34 (regulations, offences and penalties, appeals).

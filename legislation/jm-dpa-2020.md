# Data Protection Act, 2020 (Jamaica)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #476, Jamaica). Known from the Office of the Prime Minister's page on the Information Commissioner; the Act's text and its commencement notices were not read, so no section is described.

## What is sourced

The page says the Information Commissioner's Office ([[JM-OIC]]) "operates in accordance with the Data Protection Act (DPA) enacted in June 2020", ensures adherence to the Act and its Regulations, advises the Minister, may prepare good-practice guidelines and must raise public awareness. It also says registration began on **1 December 2023** and that the first data protection impact assessment was due on 31 March 2024. Search summaries give 1 December 2021 for the provisions establishing the Office and 30 November 2023 for the end of the controllers' two-year transition; neither was read.

Applies in [[JM]] (anchor edge). [[JM-OIC]] is `governed-by` this Act.

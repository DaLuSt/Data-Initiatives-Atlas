## Description

Poland's founding public-statistics law, in force since **29 June
1995**, originally published as **Dziennik Ustaw 1995, No. 88, item
439**. Reading GUS's own BIP (Biuletyn Informacji Publicznej) legal-acts
page directly: the act **"określa zasady i tworzy podstawy rzetelnego,
obiektywnego, profesjonalnego i niezależnego prowadzenia badań
statystycznych"** (determines the principles and creates the foundations
for reliable, objective, professional and independent conduct of
statistical research), establishing the organisation, procedure and
scope of obligations for statistical research — the legal basis
[[PL-GUS]]'s own description already summarised without a linked
entity.

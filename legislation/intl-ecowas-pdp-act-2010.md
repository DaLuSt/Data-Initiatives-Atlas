# ECOWAS Supplementary Act A/SA.1/01/10 on Personal Data Protection (2010)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #549, Western Africa's regional bodies). The Act's text, as a copy on Statewatch's site (the official ECOWAS PDF was not found; the scan's text layer is noisy, so the quoted articles were checked by eye), and ECOWAS's own release on the revision, read directly. `status: adopted` because entry into force needs publication in the Community's Official Journal and each Member State's gazette (Article 48), which was not read. `start_date` is the date of signature.

## What it says

- **Article 2:** "Each Member State shall establish a legal framework of protection for privacy of data relating to the collection, processing, transmission, storage, and use of personal data without prejudice to the general interest of the State."
- **Article 14:** "each Member State shall establish its own data protection Authority"; any State without one "shall be encouraged to establish one"; the Authority is an independent administrative authority (Articles 14 to 22 cover its members' incompatibilities, immunity, secrecy, responsibilities, sanctions and budget).
- **Articles 23 to 27 and 30 to 31:** principles of consent and legitimacy, legality and fairness, purpose, relevance and preservation, accuracy and transparency; specific rules for sensitive data and their exceptions.
- **Articles 39 to 41:** rights of access, objection and rectification; **Articles 43 to 45:** security, preservation and durability obligations.
- **Article 46:** amendments are proposed by a Member State, the Council of Ministers or the Commission, adopted by the Council of Ministers and approved and signed by the Authority of Heads of State and Government. **Article 48:** in force on publication; the Act is "an integral part" of the ECOWAS Treaty. **Article 49:** deposited with the Commission.
- Recitals cite Supplementary Act A/SA.1/01/07 of 19 January 2007 on harmonising the ICT sector's policies and regulatory framework, the opinion of the ECOWAS Parliament of 23 May 2009 and the recommendation of the 63rd Ordinary Session of the Council of Ministers (20-21 November 2009).

## The revision

ECOWAS's release of 22 November 2024: experts from the ministries responsible for telecommunications, ICT and digitalisation and the national data protection authorities met in Accra on 18-19 November 2024 "to validate the draft revised Supplementary Act A/SA.1/01/10". It was coordinated by the Directorate of Digital Economy and Post with EU and German (BMZ) support under the Data Governance in Africa initiative, and "will be presented at the next meeting of the ECOWAS Ministers responsible for Telecommunications, ICT and Digitalisation for their approval". Whether it has been approved was not found.

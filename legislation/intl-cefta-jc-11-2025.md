# Decision No. 11/2025 of the CEFTA Joint Committee on the recognition of advanced electronic signatures and advanced electronic seals

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #542). The Decision's own PDF, read directly in full (three pages).

## Description

"Adopted in Pristina on 16 December 2025", entering into force on adoption. It "seeks to facilitate electronic transactions within CEFTA through the recognition of advanced electronic signatures and advanced electronic seals" issued by trust service providers established in a CEFTA Party, and defines both by reference to Articles 26 and 36 of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 ([[EU-EIDAS]]) "as amended by the European Digital Identity (EUDI) Regulation" ([[EU-EIDAS2]]).

It does not yet recognise anything. The Sub-Committee on Trade in Services is to recommend that the Joint Committee recognise a signature or seal from another Party as equivalent, on the report of the Working Group ([[INTL-CEFTA-WG-DIGITAL]]), which is to draft the requirements and methodology, including "a CEFTA List of Trusted Lists" published on the CEFTA website. Recognition applies only to a Party for which Additional Protocol 6 has entered into force.

The Joint Committee's Ministerial Conclusions of 2 December 2025 speak of "swiftly finalizing the technical discussions on the recognition of advanced electronic signatures and seals and qualified trust services"; a companion decision on qualified trust services (numbered 12/2025 in a search result) was not found on CEFTA's site and is not modelled.

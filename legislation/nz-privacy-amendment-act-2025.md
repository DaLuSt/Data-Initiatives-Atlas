# Privacy Amendment Act 2025 (New Zealand)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #478, New Zealand). Known only from the Privacy Commissioner's page. `start_date` is when IPP 3A came into force; the Act's own passage date, and whether other parts commence on other dates, were not read.

## What is sourced

IPP 3A is "a new notification obligation, covering the collection of personal information other than from the individual concerned (that is, indirect collection)". It sits beside IPP 3, which covers collection from the individual, and the principles are still numbered 1-13 with 3A inserted after 3. A number of codes of practice were amended after consultation to include a new rule 3A.

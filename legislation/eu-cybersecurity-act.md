## Description

Regulation (EU) 2019/881 concerns ENISA, the European Union Agency for
Cybersecurity, and ICT cybersecurity certification. It established the
European Cybersecurity Certification Framework, intended to improve the
functioning of the internal market by raising the level of cybersecurity in
the Union and enabling a harmonised approach to European cybersecurity
certification schemes.

[[EU-ENISA]] was added in Batch 9, closing the gap this entity carried when
created. The `governed-by` relationship is recorded on the agency.

`coverage: low`: the certification framework's schemes and mechanisms were
not researched.

## Relevance assessment

Included on the same reasoning applied to the AI Act: the brief warns
against treating every digital regulation as a data initiative. This one
qualifies because certification of ICT products and services is a
precondition for trusted data infrastructure, and because it sits in the
same policy package lineage as [[EU-NIS2]], which the Atlas already carries
through to Dutch implementation. A reader tracing EU cybersecurity law would
find a hole without it.

# Proyecto de Ley Orgánica para el buen uso y la gobernanza de la inteligencia artificial

> **Verified 2026-10-10.** The bill's publication in the Boletín Oficial de las Cortes Generales (Serie A, núm. 97-1, 12 June 2026) was read directly, including the explanatory memorandum and article 1. This closes `discovery/unresolved.md` row #95: the stage is now sourced. It is **not law**: `status: proposed`, no `rank` (an organic law only once enacted), no start date.

## Stage, from the Boletín

The Mesa of the Congreso, meeting on 8 June 2026, entrusted the opinion (*Dictamen*) to the Comisión de Economía, Comercio y Transformación Digital and published the bill with a period for amendments of fifteen working days, ending on 30 June 2026. What happened after that date was not read: the Congreso's follow-up page for file 121/000096 was not checked, so the Atlas does not say where the bill stands now.

## Content, from the explanatory memorandum

The memorandum describes a law of forty-one articles in five chapters, two additional provisions, one repealing provision and eight final provisions. Article 1 gives its object: to adapt Spanish law to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, by regulating the governance and supervision regime through the designation of the national competent authorities (the Regulation's article 70.1 requires at least one notifying authority and one market surveillance authority), and further matters listed in that article. The memorandum also recalls the statute of the Agencia Española de Supervisión de Inteligencia Artificial ([[ES-AESIA]]), Real Decreto 729/2023, and the sandbox Real Decreto 817/2023. Which authority the bill designates for which role was not read.

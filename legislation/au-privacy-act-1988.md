# Privacy Act 1988 (Australia)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #479, Australia). Read on the Federal Register of Legislation (the page's metadata and table of contents) and its API title record; the Act's sections were not read. `start_date` is the register's making date, 14 December 1988; the Act's commencement sections (s. 2) were not read, so entry into force may differ.

## What is sourced

- Title and number: Privacy Act 1988, No. 119, 1988; status in force; administered by the Attorney-General's Department and the Department of the Treasury.
- Latest compilation read: C2026C00227, compilation C104, 4 June 2026.
- The table of contents shows an objects clause (s. 2A), extra-territorial operation (s. 5B), definitions including small business operators (s. 6D) and a "registered CR code" (s. 6BA), Division 2 "Australian Privacy Principles" (ss. 14-15), Part IIIC "Notification of eligible data breaches" and Part IV "Functions of the Information Commissioner" (s. 27 and following).
- The Act has been amended by the Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024, which the register also lists as amending the Data Availability and Transparency Act ([[AU-DATA-ACT-2022]]).

# Law of Mongolia on electronic signatures (revised edition, 2021)

> **Created 2026-11-22** (roadmap #775, closing `discovery/unresolved.md` row #286). Read directly on legalinfo.mn: the law's own text, its Article 1.1 and Article 35.1, and the repealing law printed on the page of the 2011 law. The Communications Regulatory Commission's list of laws gives this law's link under the 2011 date. Which body supervises the law, and how it treats certification service providers, was not read closely enough to record edges for them.

## What is sourced

- Dated 17 December 2021; a revised edition (Шинэчилсэн найруулга) of the law on electronic signatures.
- Applies from 1 May 2022 (Article 35.1).
- Purpose (Article 1.1): the legal basis for using electronic signatures in the digital environment, the legal and technical requirements for them, and the operation of the public key infrastructure.
- A separate law of the same date declares the law of 15 December 2011 invalid from the day this edition takes effect.

# Lov om forebyggende sikkerhetstjeneste (sikkerhetsloven, 1998)

> **Created 2026-09-17**, narrowing `discovery/unresolved.md` row #111

## Description

Confirmed by reading lovdata.no's own record for LOV-1998-03-20-10
directly: **"Lov om forebyggende sikkerhetstjeneste (sikkerhetsloven)"**
was enacted **20 March 1998** but did not enter into force until
**1 July 2001** — a gap between enactment and commencement not previously
recorded anywhere in the Atlas's Norway security-law layer. It was
**repealed 1 January 2019**, per resolution of 20 December 2018 no. 2052,
by the Act of 1 June 2018 no. 24 — now modelled as [[NO-SIKKERHETSLOVEN]],
whose own §12-2 independently cites the same repeal.

## What the Act covered

Confirmed by reading its own Chapter 3 ("Nasjonal sikkerhetsmyndighet")
directly: the Act defined [[NO-NSM]]'s operational role — coordinating
preventive security measures, monitoring the security situation, acting
as Norway's executing body in relations with foreign countries and
international organisations, and (per §§8–10) gathering and assessing
security-relevant information, supervising organisations' security
compliance, and holding unrestricted access rights to classified areas
and protected objects. The Act's own text **presumes NSM already
exists** rather than establishing it — it defines the authority's powers,
not its creation — so no `start_date` for [[NO-NSM]] itself is inferred
from this Act.

The Act's other chapters, named in search results but not read directly
this pass, covered information security, object security, personnel
security clearance, and security-classified procurement — the same
substantive domains [[NO-SIKKERHETSLOVEN]] carries forward.

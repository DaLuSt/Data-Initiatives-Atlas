# The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025

> **Created 2026-11-01** (roadmap #470). Known **only** from the Press Information Bureau's explainer of 17 November 2025; **the Rules' text and the gazette notification were not read**. `status: adopted`: the Rules were notified on 14 November 2025 but, per the PIB, compliance is phased over eighteen months, and which rules came into force on which date was not read. Secondary sources (law-firm pages seen in a search, not recorded) give 13 November as the notification date and a gazette number; the PIB's date is used.

## What is sourced

Consent: "Every Data Fiduciary must issue a separate consent notice that is clear and easy to understand"; Consent Managers "must be companies based in India". Breaches: affected individuals are informed "without delay", in plain language, with contact details. Significant Data Fiduciaries: independent audits, impact assessments, stricter checks on new or sensitive technologies and, in some cases, "government directions on restricted categories of data, including local storage where needed". Data Principals: access, correction, update and erasure, and nominating someone to act for them; "Data Fiduciaries must respond to such requests within ninety days". The Board is "fully digital" with four members; appeals go to TDSAT.

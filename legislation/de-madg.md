## Description

The MADG constitutes [[DE-BAMAD]], Germany's military counter-intelligence
service, and completes the triad with [[DE-BNDG]] and [[DE-BVERFSCHG]].

Confirmed directly on bfdi.bund.de's "Kontrolllandschaft Nachrichtendienste
des Bundes" page (2026-08-22): the MADG is named alongside the BVerfSchG,
BNDG, G10G and TKG as one of "die wesentlichen Rechtsgrundlagen für
Datenverarbeitungen der Nachrichtendienste des Bundes."

## Germany is the only country here with a dedicated military-intelligence act

Poland comes closest — [[PL-USKWSWW-2006]] covers **two** military services
in one act. Everywhere else, the military service shares its statute with
the civilian one:

| Country | Military service | Its act |
|---|---|---|
| Germany | [[DE-BAMAD]] | **MADG — its own** |
| Poland | [[PL-SKW]], [[PL-SWW]] | [[PL-USKWSWW-2006]] — a military pair |
| Netherlands | [[NL-MIVD]] | [[NL-WIV-2017]] — shared with [[NL-AIVD]] |
| Belgium | [[BE-ADIV]] | [[BE-WIV-1998]] — shared with [[BE-VSSE]] |
| France | [[FR-DRM]], [[FR-DRSD]] | [[FR-LOI-RENSEIGNEMENT-2015]] — shared with all |
| United Kingdom | *not modelled* | — |

previously been returned by search. This pass found one — the act now
appears on `gesetze-im-internet.de` under a re-numbered URL

this pass, consistent with intermittent unavailability noted elsewhere in

recently amended by "Art. 2 G v. 7.12.2011 I 2576". This is the

enactment date, and this pass's WebSearch summary of that same official
page independently returned the identical 1990-12-20 date.
`start_date` is now recorded as **20 December 1990**.

# Personal Information Protection Law (China)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #482, China). Known from one Xinhua report on the NPC's English site; the law's text was not read. `start_date` is the effective date the report gives (1 November 2021); the adoption date is 20 August 2021.

## What the report says

- Adopted at the closing meeting of a regular session of the Standing Committee, on Friday 20 August 2021; takes effect 1 November.
- It "clarifies rules on the processing and cross-border providing of personal information". Reported provisions: a ban on excessive collection and on big-data-enabled price discrimination against existing customers; large internet platforms must set up an independent body, mainly outsiders, to supervise how personal information is handled; automated decision-making must offer options not based on personal characteristics or a way to refuse; signs are required where image-capture and identification equipment is installed in public places, and the images may be used only to safeguard public security; and personal information of minors under 14 is sensitive and needs special processing rules.

# SGB X – Sozialverwaltungsverfahren und Sozialdatenschutz

> **Verified 2026-10-09.** The table of contents, citation and § 1 were read directly on gesetze-im-internet.de. The date of entry into force is not recorded: the site gives the issue date (18 August 1980) and a text record from 1 July 1983.

## Description

The act's chapter 2, "Schutz der Sozialdaten", starts at § 67 with definitions and covers the collection, storage, use, transmission and other processing of social data. Chapter 1 regulates the administrative procedure of the authorities acting under the Social Code. The act is part of the Social Code, which the Atlas models here only through this book.

# Data Protection Act No. 005 (2023)

> **Verified 2026-10-09 through the Authority's own site only.** The site links the Act as a PDF; the PDF was not read, so no date, article or content is recorded beyond what the site says. Secondary sources give conflicting signing dates (21 or 22 March 2023); that is recorded in `discovery/unresolved.md`.

## Description

The Authority's site names it "Data Protection Act No. 005 (2023)" and "Law No. 005 (2023)", and cites Article 26 for the right to complain about unlawful, unfair or abusive processing.

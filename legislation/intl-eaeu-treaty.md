# Treaty on the Eurasian Economic Union

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #543), as the target of the Third Major Protocol and the instrument that establishes [[INTL-EAEU]]. **The Treaty's text, its signature and entry-into-force dates and its annexes were not read**; only what the Union's own pages say about it is recorded.

## What is sourced

The Union's home page says the Union "is established by the Treaty on the Eurasian Economic Union" and carries a news item that the Presidents of the Customs Union and Single Economic Space Member States "signed the Treaty on the Eurasian Economic Union (EEU) at the session of the Supreme Eurasian Economic Council". The Commission's news item on the Third Major Protocol says Annex No. 3 to the Treaty defines the "transboundary space of trust" ([[INTL-EAEU-PROTOCOL-3-2023]]).

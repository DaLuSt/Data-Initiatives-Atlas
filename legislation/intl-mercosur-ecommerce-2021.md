# MERCOSUR Agreement on Electronic Commerce (2021)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #545). The agreement's text (Uruguay's official copy, a scan with an OCR layer that is partly garbled, so quotes were checked by eye) and the subgroup's minutes of 22 May 2026, read directly. `start_date` is the signature date; the entry-into-force date for the bloc was not found.

## What it says

- **Art. 6, personal data:** the Parties "deberán adoptar o mantener leyes, regulaciones o medidas administrativas para la protección de la información personal de los usuarios", taking international standards into account (Art. 2.5(f) names general principles such as consent, purpose, quality, security and accountability in a footnote); they publish how individuals exercise access, rectification and erasure; they commit to apply "un nivel de protección adecuado" to personal data received from another Party; and they "arbitrarán los medios necesarios para establecer medidas comunes para la protección de los datos personales y su libre circulación en el MERCOSUR".
- **Art. 7, cross-border transfer:** each Party "permitirá la transferencia transfronteriza de información por medios electrónicos" for the business of a person of a Party, subject to Art. 6, with a legitimate public policy exception; it does not apply to financial services.
- **Art. 8, computing facilities:** no Party may require a person of another Party to use or locate computing facilities in its territory as a condition for doing business (same exception; not for financial services); hosting personal data abroad counts as an international transfer under Art. 7.
- **Art. 4:** no denial of the legal validity of a signature only because it is electronic; Parties are to "arbitrar los medios necesarios para la suscripción de acuerdos de reconocimiento mutuo de firma electrónica avanzada o firma digital".
- **Art. 12(f), (h):** cooperation on cybersecurity and on exchanging structured, standardised data between the Parties' cross-border trade authorities.
- **Art. 13:** may be revised every two years. **Art. 14:** in force 30 days after the second State Party deposits its instrument of ratification, and for later ratifiers 30 days after their own deposit; Paraguay is the depositary (Art. 17). **Art. 16:** denunciation takes effect 90 days after notice.
- **Art. 2.3:** where it conflicts with other MERCOSUR rules, those prevail.

## Where it stands (22 May 2026, per the subgroup's minutes)

Argentina: approved by Law 27768, instrument deposited with Paraguay in December 2025, in force for Argentina from January 2026. Paraguay and Uruguay: "incorporado". Brazil: parliamentary approval done, in the promulgation phase. The decision number (a law-firm page says CMC 15/2021) was not found in the text read.

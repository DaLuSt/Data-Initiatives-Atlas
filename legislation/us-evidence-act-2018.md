# Foundations for Evidence-Based Policymaking Act of 2018 (United States)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #482, United States). The enrolled text was read on govinfo.gov (table of contents, Titles I and II in part, Title III's repeal clause, and the chief data officer provisions); not every section was. `start_date` is the date of approval printed on the law. The act's effective-date section (Sec. 403) was not read in detail, so the dates on which its duties began to apply are not recorded.

## What the text shows

- **Title I, Federal evidence-building activities.** Adds a subchapter to chapter 3 of part I of title 5: agencies' evidence-building plans in their strategic plans, with definitions of evaluation, evidence and statistical activities.
- **Title II, OPEN Government Data Act** ("Open, Public, Electronic, and Necessary Government Data Act"). Amends chapter 35 of title 44: a *comprehensive data inventory*, definitions of *data* and *data asset*, **Chief Data Officers** in agencies and a **Chief Data Officer Council** "established in the Office of Management and Budget".
- **Title III, Confidential information protection and statistical efficiency.** Adds a subchapter III to chapter 35 of title 44 on confidentiality and designated statistical agencies and units, and repeals Title V of the E-Government Act of 2002 ([[US-E-GOV-ACT-2002]]), which held the earlier version of the same subject.
- Data.gov's page says the portal's data are collected under Title II ([[US-DATA-GOV]]).

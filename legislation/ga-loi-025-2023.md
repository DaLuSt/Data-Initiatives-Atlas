# Loi n° 025/2023 du 12 juillet 2023 modifiant la loi n° 001/2011 sur la protection des données à caractère personnel (Gabon)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #472, Gabon). The text was read in the Journal officiel de la République gabonaise n° 218 Bis (15 July 2023), through a copy hosted by the AFAPDP (the Francophone association of data-protection authorities), not on a Gabonese government site. Only the opening articles were read closely.

## What is sourced

Adopted by the National Assembly and the Senate and promulgated by decree n° 166/PR of 12 July 2023, the law is "relative à la protection des données à caractère personnel" and sets the rules for the collection and processing of personal data and privacy (Article 2). It reprints the whole regime of the 2011 law with new definitions (accountability, algorithms, machine learning, annotation and others) and, in **Article 7**, creates "une Autorité chargée de veiller à la Protection des Données Personnelles et de la Vie Privée en République Gabonaise", abbreviated **APDPVP** ([[GA-APDPVP]]). The same issue prints Law n° 027/2023 of 12 July 2023 on cybersecurity and the fight against cybercrime.

## Not read

The 2011 law (n° 001/2011 of 25 September 2011) itself, the body it replaced (secondary sources name it the CNPDCP), and any implementing decree appointing the Authority's members.

# Lei nº 15.352 (Brazil, 2026)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #481, Brazil). Known only from one sentence on the Agency's Institucional page; the law's text was not read. The Provisional Measure it converted (No. 1.317 of 17 September 2025) is not a separate entity.

## What is sourced

Converted Provisional Measure 1.317 into law and inserted the Agency into the list of agencies in Law No. 13.848 of 25 June 2019.

# The Re-use of Public Sector Information Regulations 2015

> **Verified 2026-10-07.** The instrument's contents page, Explanatory Note and
> regulation 18 were read directly on legislation.gov.uk. Every regulation was
> **not** read; the claims below are limited to the pages named in `sources`.

## Description

SI 2015/1415 was made on **24 June 2015**, laid before Parliament the next day
and came into force on **18 July 2015**. Its regulations cover citation and
commencement, interpretation, public sector bodies, re-use of documents,
exclusions, requests for re-use and how they are answered, format, conditions,
non-discrimination, the prohibition of exclusive arrangements, charging,
information a body must publish, an internal complaints procedure, enforcement and
appeals (regulations 18 and 19), information sharing (20), the amendments to the
Freedom of Information Act 2000 (21) and the revocation of the 2005 Regulations
(22). It implements [[EU-PSI-DIRECTIVE]] as amended in 2013, the directive this
Atlas holds as `superseded` by [[EU-OPEN-DATA-DIRECTIVE]].

## Why this answers the gap on `GB-DATA-GOV-UK`

The Atlas recorded that [[GB-DATA-GOV-UK]] connected to
[[EU-OPEN-DATA-DIRECTIVE]] not at all, while four other countries had a sourced
transposition. The UK instrument that governs re-use is this one, and it
implements the **earlier** directive. The UK left the Union before the Open Data
Directive's transposition deadline of 17 July 2021, so no UK transposition of
it exists to model. The Atlas therefore records no edge to
[[EU-OPEN-DATA-DIRECTIVE]]; the absence is correct, not a gap.
No typed edge is asserted from this instrument to [[GB-DATA-GOV-UK]]: the
sources read here do not say the portal operates under it.

## Status

`status: active`. legislation.gov.uk shows the latest available (revised) text
with no revocation notice and "no known outstanding effects" on 2026-10-07. That
is the register's own view, not a statement by a government department that the
Regulations remain the operative law; a later pass could confirm it from the
Department for Culture, Media and Sport or The National Archives' policy pages.

## Enforcement and the Information Commission

Regulation 18 applies "the relevant enforcement and appeals provisions" of the
Freedom of Information Act 2000 to re-use. In regulation 18(2)(a), where the Act
speaks of an application for a decision, the text reads "Commission": a footnote
on legislation.gov.uk records that the word was substituted with effect from
**30 September 2026** by SI 2026/386 and SI 2026/1015 (read 2026-10-07). That is
the date the Information Commission succeeded the Information Commissioner
([[GB-INFORMATION-COMMISSION]], [[GB-ICO]]). The Atlas records the link in
`related_entities` rather than as a typed edge: the substitution is read, but
the whole enforcement chain through the 2000 Act was not.

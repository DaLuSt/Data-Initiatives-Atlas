# Revised Treaty of Basseterre (2010)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #547). Known **only** from the OECS's About page. **The Treaty's text was not read**; `start_date` is the signature date, and the page gives each Member State's date of membership of the Revised Treaty by protocol (18 June 2010 for Antigua and Barbuda, Dominica and Grenada, among others) without saying when it came into force.

## What is sourced

"The 1981 Treaty was replaced in 2010 with a Revised Treaty of Basseterre ... signed on 18th June 2010 in Saint Lucia during the 51st Meeting of the Authority." It "establishes a unified financial and economic space, facilitating the free movement of goods, people, and capital" and "enables the harmonisation of monetary and governmental policies, relating to taxes and revenue". The Director General has said the Treaty "speaks to several imperatives that presuppose and require the standardisation, systematic collection, effective management and intelligent utilisation of data" (launch of the data governance council, 2023). No Member State has a country anchor, so there are no `applies-in` edges.

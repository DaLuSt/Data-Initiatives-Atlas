# Шахсга доир маълумотлар тўғрисида (Law on personal data)

> **Verified 2026-10-09.** The consolidated text on Lex.uz was read directly (in Uzbek, Cyrillic script; the English title is a translation by the Atlas). The page lists revisions up to 12 September 2026.

## Description

Law No. ЎРҚ-547 of 2 July 2019, in force 1 October 2019. Its chapters cover general provisions, state regulation of the field, processing of personal data, protection of personal data and the rights and obligations of participants. Article 8, "Шахсга доир маълумотлар соҳасидаги ваколатли давлат органи" (the authorised state body in the field of personal data), names [[UZ-SPC]].

## Description

The regulation of **25 October 2012**, in force from 1 January 2013, that
gives European standardisation its statutory basis. It establishes rules on:

- cooperation between the European standardisation organisations, the
  national standardisation bodies, the member states and the Commission;
- the establishment of European standards and European standardisation
  deliverables for **products and for services**, in support of Union
  legislation and policies;
- the identification of **ICT technical specifications**;
- the **financing** of European standardisation;
- **transparency and participation** obligations on the European
  standardisation organisations and the national standardisation bodies.

A **harmonised standard** is a European standard developed by one of the
three recognised organisations — [[EU-CEN]], [[EU-CENELEC]] or [[EU-ETSI]] —
following a request from the [[EU-COMMISSION]].

## The other half of a deliberately deferred pair

[[EU-CEN]] carries a section headed *"Regulation 1025/2012 not modelled"*,
and [[EU-ESS]] carries one headed *"Regulation (EC) No 223/2009 is cited, not
modelled"*. Both deferrals had the same stated reason: modelling one
statutory base and not the other would make the Atlas inconsistent about
statutory bases in general.

This entity and [[EU-REG-223-2009]] were written together, in the same batch,
so the pair is closed in one move rather than half-closed in two.

## What is deliberately not asserted

The Atlas holds seven **national** standardisation bodies — [[NL-NEN]],
[[DE-DIN]], [[GB-BSI]], [[BE-NBN]], [[FR-AFNOR]], [[ES-UNE]] and
[[PL-PKN]] — each of which `participates-in` [[EU-CEN]]. The regulation
places obligations on national standardisation bodies as a class, but **no
`applies-to` edge is asserted to any individual one**: [[GB-BSI]] is the
body of a state that has left the Union, and asserting the regulation
against each named body one at a time would multiply edges without adding
information the three organisation-level edges do not already carry.

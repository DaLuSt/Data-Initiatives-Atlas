# Act on the Protection of Personal Information (Japan)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #482, Japan). Known from the Commission's pages; the Act's text was not read. **No `start_date`**: the pages give the Act's number and year (No. 57 of 2003) and a series of amendments, not a date of entry into force, and the Act has been amended more than once (the Commission's outline names the 2020 Amendment; only the 2017 and 2023 effective dates appear on the pages read).

## What is sourced

- Act No. 57 of 2003; the Commission's legal page lists the consolidated text "as of April 1, 2023. All the amended Acts entered into full force", an earlier text "fully put into effect on May 30, 2017", a Cabinet Order, Enforcement Rules and supplementary rules.
- The Act has a triennial review provision (from the 2020 amendment), which led to [[JP-APPI-TRIENNIAL-REVIEW-2026]].
- Scope per the Commission's role page: the private sector, administrative agencies, incorporated administrative agencies and local governments.

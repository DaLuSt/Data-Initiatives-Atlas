# European Union (Open Data and Re-use of Public Sector Information) Regulations 2021

> **Verified 2026-08-22.** The instrument itself, on irishstatutebook.ie,
> and both data.gov.ie pages were read directly and confirmed the claims
> below verbatim. The instrument was signed 21 July 2021 and came into
> operation 22 July 2021; the description's "made on 22 July 2021" refers
> to the latter, confirmed on both irishstatutebook.ie and data.gov.ie.

## Description

Confirmed by reading the instrument itself on irishstatutebook.ie
(2026-08-22): "These Regulations shall come into operation on 22 July
2021." **S.I. No. 376/2021**, transposing
[[EU-OPEN-DATA-DIRECTIVE]] into Irish law, signed under the Minister's
Official Seal on 21 July 2021.

## Five days late, and the only one of the five identified precisely

The Directive's transposition deadline was **17 July 2021**. Ireland made
this instrument on **22 July 2021** — five days after.

That is a trivial margin and it is worth recording, because Ireland's *other*
transposition in the Atlas is late by rather more: [[IE-NCS-BILL]] is still
`proposed` against a NIS2 deadline of 17 October 2024. One member state, two
directives, two very different outcomes.

## A statutory instrument, not an act

Ireland transposed by **secondary legislation** — a statutory instrument
made under the European Communities Acts — where [[IE-DPA-2018]] is an Act of
the Oireachtas.

That difference is normal in Irish practice and is worth noting because the
Atlas's `type: law` flattens it. Both are `law`, and only the names and this
paragraph record that one is primary and one is secondary legislation.

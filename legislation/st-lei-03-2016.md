# Lei n.º 03/2016 — Proteção de Dados Pessoais (São Tomé and Príncipe)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #472, São Tomé and Príncipe). The text read is a **French rendering** hosted by the AFAPDP, not the Portuguese original on a government site; it carries no publication date, so the date of the law is not asserted.

## What is sourced

A general personal-data law built on three lines its preamble names: the holder's consent and limited situations of necessity as the basis of processing, conditions on transferring data outside the national territory, and liability for those who fail to protect the data they hold. **Article 44** provides for the **Agência Nacional de Protecção de Dados Pessoais (ANPDP)**: its organic law and staff are to be approved by a law of the National Assembly, its members' status is to guarantee independence, and it has its own technical and administrative support ([[ST-ANPDP]]).

## Not read

The Portuguese original, the date of the law (secondary sources say 10 May 2016) and the ANPDP's organic law (reported as Law 7/2017; the numbers disagree across sources).

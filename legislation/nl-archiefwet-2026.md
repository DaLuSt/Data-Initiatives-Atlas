# Archiefwet 2026

> **Verified 2026-08-27, major update.** This bill is no longer a bill: the
> Staatsblad's own text, read directly this pass and added as a new source,
> confirms it was **signed 13 May 2026 and published as Stb. 2026, 149 on
> 19 June 2026** — enacted, not merely "planned" in the legislative-process
> sense the entity's prior text implied. Three of five cited pages (now
> five, with the Staatsblad added) were read directly. The
> wetgevingskalender.overheid.nl page returned a 503 this pass.

## Description

The new Dutch archives act repeals [[NL-ARCHIEFWET-1995]] and replaces it.
Confirmed by reading its own Staatsblad text directly this pass: the Act
was **signed 13 May 2026** and **published as Stb. 2026, 149 on 19 June
2026**, under the official title "Wet van 13 mei 2026 tot intrekking van de
Archiefwet 1995 en vervanging door een nieuwe Archiefwet." Its own Article
12.14 leaves the exact entry-into-force date to a royal decree not yet
issued — it does **not** fix 1 January 2027 in the Act's own text. That date
is the intended commencement stated consistently by nationaalarchief.nl's
own knowledge base and by rijksoverheid.nl, both read directly this pass,
and is recorded here as the best-available estimate rather than a date the
Act itself confirms.

It modernises archival law to match digital information management
practice: concepts are adapted to the digital environment,
responsibilities are clarified, and the act takes account of how
information is now created, shared and stored.

Its most concrete change is temporal — public bodies must transfer important
information to an archive service after **ten** years rather than twenty. The
diploma requirement for archivists is also modernised, with more attention to
training and professionalisation. The act applies to all Dutch public
organisations, including ministries, provinces, municipalities, water
authorities and the High Councils of State.

## Naming ambiguity — now resolved in the Atlas's favour

This instrument previously appeared under **at least three names** across
sources: the Eerste Kamer dossier titled "Archiefwet 2026", the
rijksoverheid document and Raad van State advice referring to "Archiefwet
2021", and the Nationaal Archief knowledge base hedging with "Nieuwe
Archiefwet 20xx". The bill number 35968 was the one stable identifier
across all of them.

The Act's own official text, read directly this pass, settles this: its
own title does not use any year at all ("een nieuwe Archiefwet"), but its
Staatsblad citation is **Stb. 2026, 149** — published in 2026. That
independently supports the Atlas's prior choice of `NL-ARCHIEFWET-2026` as
the ID and "Archiefwet 2026" as the name, which is no longer provisional in
the way `discovery/unresolved.md` previously recorded it; the alternatives
remain in `alternative_names` for anyone arriving via an older reference.

The Eerste Kamer's own dossier, read directly this pass, confirms the
bill passed the Eerste Kamer as a hamerstuk on **12 May 2026** — one day
before the King's signature.

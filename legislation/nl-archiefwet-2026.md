# Archiefwet 2026

> **Sourcing caveat.** This entity was compiled from search-engine results
> only; the cited pages were confirmed to exist but were not read. See
> `discovery/unresolved.md` and `progress/current-batch.md`.

## Description

The new Dutch archives act repeals the [[NL-ARCHIEFWET-1995]] and replaces
it with effect from 1 January 2027. It modernises archival law to match
digital information management practice: concepts are adapted to the digital
environment, responsibilities are clarified, and the act takes account of how
information is now created, shared and stored.

Its most concrete change is temporal — public bodies must transfer important
information to an archive service after **ten** years rather than twenty. The
diploma requirement for archivists is also modernised, with more attention to
training and professionalisation. The act applies to all Dutch public
organisations, including ministries, provinces, municipalities, water
authorities and the High Councils of State.

## Naming ambiguity

This instrument appears under **at least three names** across sources: the
Eerste Kamer dossier is titled "Archiefwet 2026", the rijksoverheid document
and Raad van State advice refer to "Archiefwet 2021", and the Nationaal
Archief knowledge base hedges with "Nieuwe Archiefwet 20xx". The bill number
35968 is the stable identifier across all of them.

The Atlas uses `NL-ARCHIEFWET-2026` as the ID and "Archiefwet 2026" as the
name, following the parliamentary dossier, with the alternatives recorded in
`alternative_names`. **This choice is provisional** and recorded in
`discovery/unresolved.md`: if the act is promulgated under a different year
designation, the `name` should be corrected — but the ID must not change,
since IDs are permanent once assigned (`metadata/ontology.md` §2.2).

The Eerste Kamer reportedly approved the bill on 12 May, but the year was not
established from the search result and is therefore not recorded.

# Data Protection Act, 2019-29 (Barbados)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #474, Barbados). Known from the government's page on the Commission; the Act's text and its commencement proclamations were not read, so no section is described and no start date is recorded.

## What is sourced

The page ties the individual's data-protection rights to "an individual's constitutional right to privacy" and says the rights, obligations and responsibilities are set out in this Act. [[BB-DPC]] is the unit that administers it.

## Not recorded (leads only)

Search summaries say the Act passed in 2019 and took effect on 31 March 2021, with the registration provisions (ss. 50-52, 55-57) held back for a later proclamation. None of this was read from a primary source, and whether that proclamation issued is unknown (`discovery/unresolved.md`).

## Relationships

Applies in [[BB]] (anchor edge). [[BB-DPC]] is `governed-by` this Act.

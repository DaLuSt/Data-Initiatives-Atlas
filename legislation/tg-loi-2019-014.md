# Loi n° 2019-014 du 29 octobre 2019 relative à la protection des données à caractère personnel (Togo)

> **Created 2026-10-11** (roadmap #473, Togo). Read in the Journal officiel's special number through a copy hosted by the AFAPDP, not on a Togolese government site.

## What is sourced

Published in the special number of 29 October 2019 of the *Journal officiel de la République togolaise* (64th year, n° 26 ter), the law regulates the collection, processing, transmission, storage, use and protection of personal data (Article premier). It creates the **Instance de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (IPDCP)** ([[TG-IPDCP]]), "une autorité administrative indépendante chargée de veiller à ce que les traitements des données à caractère personnel soient mis en œuvre conformément aux dispositions" of the law; its direction committee has a mandate (Article 59), incompatibilities (Article 60) and a president appointed by presidential decree from among its members (Article 61). The text also covers a security obligation and a retention rule for controllers (Articles 52-53).

## Not read

The articles on the IPDCP's powers in full, the penalties, and any amendment. The promulgation line in the text reads "Fait à Lomé, le 30 octobre 2019", which sits beside the law dated 29 October; the Atlas uses the law's own date.

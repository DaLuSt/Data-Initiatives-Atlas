# Umweltinformationsgesetz (UIG)

> **Verified 2026-10-09.** The consolidated federal text on RIS (version of 9 October 2026) was read directly. No provision is marked *(Verfassungsbestimmung)*, so `rank` is `ordinary` by the Austrian basis in `metadata/rank-basis.md`. The date of entry into force is not recorded: the RIS text gives the promulgation reference, not a date.

## Description

Goal, § 1: "die Information der Öffentlichkeit über die Umwelt", by guaranteeing the right of access to environmental information held by the obliged bodies and promoting its systematic availability and dissemination, preferably electronically. Original act BGBl. Nr. 495/1993; amended, among others, by BGBl. I Nr. 6/2005 (for Directive 2003/4/EC), BGBl. I Nr. 95/2015 and BGBl. I Nr. 74/2018. EUR-Lex lists 15 Austrian measures for the Directive, mostly Länder acts; none of those is modelled.

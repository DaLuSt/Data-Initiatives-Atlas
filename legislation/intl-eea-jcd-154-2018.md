## Description

The decision of **6 July 2018** by which [[EU-GDPR]] entered the
[[INTL-EEA-AGREEMENT]]. It amends **Annex XI** (Electronic communication,
audiovisual services and information society) and **Protocol 37**, and was
published in the EEA Section of the Official Journal at **OJ L 183 of
19 July 2018, p. 23**.

Two consequences beyond incorporation itself:

- the **supervisory authorities of the EFTA States participate in the
  activities of the [[EU-EDPB]]**;
- the Protocol 37 entry for the **Article 29 Working Party** — the body the
  GDPR replaced with the Board — was deleted.

## Why this is the EEA route made visible

The EEA EFTA states are not bound by an EU regulation because the EU adopted
it. They are bound because the Joint Committee decided to add it to an annex
of the EEA Agreement, and then because national law gave it effect. That is
**three steps**, and until now the Atlas showed only the first and the third:

```
   EU-GDPR                                  ← the EU act
      │ referenced by
      ▼
   INTL-EEA-JCD-154-2018 ── amends ──▶ INTL-EEA-AGREEMENT
      │ applies-in
      ├────────────┬────────────┐
      ▼            ▼            ▼
     NO           IS           LI
      │            │            │
      ▼            ▼            ▼
  NO-PERSON-   IS-PERSONU-    LI-DSG      ← national implementing acts
  OPPLYSNINGS- VERNDARLOG
  LOVEN
```

The **two-month gap** [[NO-PERSONOPPLYSNINGSLOVEN]] documents — GDPR
applicable in the EU from 25 May 2018, in force in Norway from 20 July 2018 —
is the space between the first step and the third, and this decision is what
sits in it.

## Relationships

- `amends` [[INTL-EEA-AGREEMENT]] — the target continues to exist under its
  own name and date, which is exactly the distinction `amends` was added for.
- `references` [[EU-GDPR]], the act incorporated. `implements` would reverse
  the direction and `applies-to` would misstate a decision that acts on an
  agreement, not on the regulation.
- `applies-in` [[NO]], [[IS]] and [[LI]] — the three EEA EFTA states.
- `applies-to` [[EU-EDPB]], whose composition it extends to the EFTA
  supervisory authorities.

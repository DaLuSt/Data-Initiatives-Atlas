## Description

The TNO-wet, dated 30 October 1930 and in force from 1 May 1932, established
[[NL-TNO]] as a central organisation for applied scientific research in the
Netherlands. It sets out the organisation's task, organisational form, legal
status and relationship with government, and underpins TNO's holding of its
own financial, personnel, commercial and research policy.

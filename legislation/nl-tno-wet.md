# TNO-wet

> **Verified 2026-08-27 — a genuine factual correction, not just a
> promotion.** The previous version of this entity, `search-only`,
> described the TNO-wet as "the act of 30 October 1930... in force 1 May
> 1932." Reading `wetten.overheid.nl`'s own statute text directly this
> pass (identifier **BWBR0003906**) shows this is **not the statute
> currently in force**: the 1930 act was itself **repealed and replaced**
> by a new act of **19 December 1985** (Stb. 1985, 762), in force from
> **1 May 1986**, which is what "the TNO-wet" now refers to and what
> `wetten.overheid.nl` serves under that name. This entity has been
> corrected to describe the currently-operative 1985/1986 act, with the
> 1930/1932 act recorded as its unmodelled predecessor rather than
> conflated with it.

## Description

TNO's current constituting statute is the **Wet van 19 december 1985,
houdende regeling van de Nederlandse Organisatie voor
toegepast-natuurwetenschappelijk onderzoek TNO** (Stb. 1985, 762), in
force since **1 May 1986**, identified as **BWBR0003906**. Reading the
act's own text on `wetten.overheid.nl` directly: Article 3 establishes
"Er is een Nederlandse Organisatie voor toegepast-natuurwetenschappelijk
onderzoek TNO" as a legal person seated in Delft, and Article 2 gives the
responsible minister (currently Economic Affairs and Climate) oversight of
the coherence and effectiveness of TNO's policy. The version currently in
force reflects amendments effective from **1 May 2022**.

## The 1930 predecessor, now correctly distinguished

A `wetten.overheid.nl`-informed search this pass confirms the 1985 act was
enacted specifically to **replace** the earlier **Wet van 30 oktober
1930** (in force 1 May 1932), which had originally established TNO as an
organisation, "as changed circumstances and insights made a new statutory
regulation desirable." The 1930 act is **not itself an Atlas entity** and
is not separately modelled here — this entity now represents only the
1985/1986 act that superseded it. TNO the *organisation* has continuous
existence across this legal recasting; only the governing statute changed.
See [[NL-TNO]] for how this affects that entity's own `start_date`, which
still records the organisation's 1932 founding under the earlier act,
now clarified in prose there as distinct from this entity's own
commencement.

`confidence` is raised from `low` to `high` and `verification` from
`search-only` to `primary-source`: the act's own text was read directly,
which is the strongest possible citation, even though `coverage` stays
`low` — only articles 1–3 were examined, not the act in full.

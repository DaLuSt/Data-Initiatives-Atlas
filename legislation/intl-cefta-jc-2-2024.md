# Decision No. 2/2024 of the CEFTA Joint Committee on Facilitation of Electronic Commerce

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #542). The Decision's own PDF, read directly (44 pages); articles 1 to 43 were read in full, the rest by their headings.

## Description

Adopted on 9 October 2024. Its purpose: "to facilitate trade of information society service between the Parties by harmonising" the legal framework on electronic commerce "with a view to ensuring the free movement of information society services among Parties" and "key aspects of consumer protection related to electronic commerce". It is built on the CEFTA "Electronic Commerce: Roadmap for dialogue on regulatory issues", endorsed by the Joint Committee on 16 December 2020 in Sarajevo, and says inter-party e-commerce and consumer protection "should be harmonised as close as possible with standards adopted by the European Union". The preamble names Directive (EU) 2015/1535, Directive 2000/31/EC and Regulation (EU) 2019/1150 as baseline standards for e-commerce, and Directive 2011/83/EU and Directive (EU) 2019/771 for consumer protection. Its definitions include "personal data" and the access of business users to data.

It contains rules on information society services, on the transparency of online intermediation services, on consumer contracts and on contracts for the sale of goods and the supply of digital content, with deadlines for the Parties to harmonise, generally three years after adoption.

## Status: adopted, not yet implemented everywhere

The Joint Committee's Ministerial Conclusions of 2 December 2025 call on the Parties "to finalize the ratification, acceptance, or approval procedures for the JC Decision on Facilitation of Electronic Commerce ... in order to enable their full implementation without delay". The Atlas therefore records it as `adopted`. Which Parties have completed their procedures was not found.

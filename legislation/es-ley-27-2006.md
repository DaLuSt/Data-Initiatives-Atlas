# Ley 27/2006

> **Verified 2026-10-09.** The consolidated text on the BOE was read directly. The BOE notes that the consolidated text has no legal value; the official publication does.

## Description

Article 1 sets the law's three objects: the right to access environmental information held by public authorities, the right to take part in decisions affecting the environment, and the right to administrative and judicial review. The consolidated text's last update was published on 26 January 2008. It also incorporates Directive 2003/35/EC, which the Atlas does not hold.

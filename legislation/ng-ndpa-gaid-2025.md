# GAID 2025 (Nigeria)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #475, Nigeria). The PDF was read as text. `start_date` is the issue date on the last page ("Issued under my hand this 20th Day of March, 2025", signed by the National Commissioner/CEO); the text read does not state a separate commencement date. Nigeria has no row in `metadata/rank-basis.md`, so no `rank` is set.

## What the table of articles shows

Article 1 objectives and application; Arts. 2-6 priority of the Act, overlapping regulators, cooperation with public authorities, exemptions and household processing; Arts. 7-14 compliance measures, controllers and processors of major importance (designation, registration, audit returns) and the data protection officer; Arts. 15-26 principles and lawful bases (consent, contract, legal obligation, vital and public interest, legitimate interest); Arts. 27-35 information to data subjects, impact assessment, security, breach notification, processing agreements; Arts. 36-40 rights to rectification, portability, erasure and complaint. Later articles and schedules (fee and risk-level tables, templates) were skimmed, not summarised.

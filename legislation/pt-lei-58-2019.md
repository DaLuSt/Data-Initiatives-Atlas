# Lei n.º 58/2019

> **Verified 2026-08-26.** Both cited pages were read directly. The act's
> own Article 1 confirms the "executes" framing verbatim, and Article
> 2(3) adds a scope carve-out (Portugal's intelligence-service files)
> this entity did not previously carry.

## Description

Lei n.º 58/2019, of **8 August 2019**, *executes* [[EU-GDPR]] in the
Portuguese legal order. Confirmed by reading the act's own Article 1
directly: "A presente lei assegura a execução, na ordem jurídica
interna, do Regulamento (UE) 2016/679... doravante designado
abreviadamente por Regulamento Geral de Proteção de Dados (RGPD)."

## What the Act carves out

Article 2(3), read directly, excludes files held under the
responsibility of the **Sistema de Informações da República Portuguesa**
— Portugal's intelligence system — from this Act's scope; those are
governed by separate, unspecified provisions instead. No Atlas entity
models that system.

## "Executes", and why the word matters

Portugal's act uses a verb none of the others do. The Atlas now holds seven
national GDPR instruments and the language differs each time:

| Instrument | What it does to the Regulation |
|---|---|
| [[NL-UAVG]] | *implements* (uitvoering) |
| [[DE-BDSG]] | *supplements and concretises* through opening clauses |
| [[ES-LOPDGDD]] | adapts and adds a rights chapter |
| [[IE-DPA-2018]] | gives *further effect* |
| [[NO-PERSONOPPLYSNINGSLOVEN]] | gives effect after **EEA incorporation** |
| [[GB-UK-GDPR]] | *is* the text, assimilated |
| **PT-LEI-58-2019** | **executes** it |
| [[CH-REVDSG]] | is `aligned-with` it, obliged by nothing |

All but the last two carry `implements-requirement-from`, which is the type
for what these instruments **do**; the varied verbs are how each legal
tradition describes the same operation. Recording the words is how the Atlas
avoids implying the traditions are identical.

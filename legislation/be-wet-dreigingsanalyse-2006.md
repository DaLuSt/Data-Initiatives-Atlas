# Wet van 10 juli 2006 betreffende de analyse van de dreiging

> **Created 2026-09-06**, closing a gap [[BE-COMITE-I]] flagged
> explicitly: "The 1991 act was amended 10 July 2006, when OCAD... was
> established... That joint arrangement is recorded here but not
> modelled, because OCAD is not an Atlas entity." Two independent
> sources — VSSE's own page and etaamb's official text of the Act —
> were read directly and agree verbatim.

## Description

Confirmed by reading etaamb.openjustice.be's own text of the law directly:
**Article 5** establishes an organ called "Coördinatieorgaan voor de
dreigingsanalyse" (OCAD), stating verbatim: "Dit orgaan staat onder het
gemeenschappelijke gezag van de minister van Justitie en de minister van
Binnenlandse Zaken" (this body is placed under the joint authority of the
Minister of Justice and the Minister of the Interior). VSSE's own page,
read independently, confirms the same law "opgericht" (established) OCAD
as successor to the Antiterroristische Gemengde Groep (AGG), and gives
its mission: evaluating the threat regarding terrorism and extremism.

## Not modelled

- The **Royal Decree of 28 November 2006** implementing the Act.
- The Act's full substantive content beyond Article 5's establishment
  provision.

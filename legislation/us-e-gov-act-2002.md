# E-Government Act of 2002 (United States)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #482, United States). The enrolled text was read on govinfo.gov (long title, table of contents, findings); the sections were not. `start_date` is the date printed on the law; the effective dates (Title IV, Sec. 402) were not read. **Title V of the act was repealed by the Evidence Act in 2019** ([[US-EVIDENCE-ACT-2018]], Sec. 302(c)(1)); the rest of the act is not shown as repealed by anything read here, so it stays `active`.

## What the table of contents shows

Title I, the Office of Management and Budget's electronic government services (the Federal Chief Information Officer); Title II, federal management and promotion of electronic government (agency responsibilities, electronic signatures, the Federal Internet portal, accessibility, usability and preservation of government information, **Sec. 208 Privacy provisions**, workforce development, common protocols for geographic information systems); Title III, information security (NIST, the Information Security and Privacy Advisory Board); Title V, confidential information protection and statistical efficiency (repealed 2019).

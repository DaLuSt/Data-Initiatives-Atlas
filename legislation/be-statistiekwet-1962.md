## Description

Belgium's founding public-statistics law, in force since **4 July
1962**. Reading `etaamb.openjustice.be`'s own text, carried over from
[[BE-STATBEL]]'s own sourcing: the act establishes the **Nationaal
Instituut voor de Statistiek** (National Statistical Institute) — the
name [[BE-STATBEL]] carried before two later renamings, in 2003 and
2014, brought it to its current identity — alongside a **Coordination
Committee** for statistical programmes, a **Statistical Control
Committee** for data-protection oversight, and a **High Council for
Statistics** as an advisory body.

# European Commission adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework

> **Created 2026-11-07** (data-sharing links beyond Europe). The decision’s own text and the Commission’s adequacy page were read through a fetch tool that returns a model’s summary unless stated in the evidence; the Brazil decision was read in full from the Commission’s PDF.

## What it says

Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795 of 10 July 2023 (C(2023) 4745; published in OJ L 231 of 20.9.2023), adopted under Article 45(3) GDPR, on the adequate level of protection of personal data under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The Commission’s adequacy page says it covers only commercial organisations participating in the Framework, and lists a report on its first review.

It was adopted under the GDPR itself (Article 45(3)), so it carries a periodic review at least every four years (Article 45(3)).

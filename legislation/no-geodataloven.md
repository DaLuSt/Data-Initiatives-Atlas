# Lov om infrastruktur for geografisk informasjon (Geodataloven)

> **Created 2026-09-13**, closing `discovery/unresolved.md` row #108:

## Description

Confirmed by reading `lovdata.no` directly: **"Lov om infrastruktur for
geografisk informasjon (geodataloven)"**, enacted **3 September 2010**.
Its own text cites **"EØS-avtalen vedlegg XX nr. 1j"** (EEA Agreement
Annex XX point 1j) and incorporates **"direktiv 2007/2/EF"** — [[EU-INSPIRE]]
by its Official Journal number. Its stated aim: to "bidra til god og
effektiv tilgang til offentlig geografisk informasjon" (contribute to
good and efficient access to public geographic information) for public
and private purposes.

Confirmed via `europalov.no`'s own tracking page: the Act is Norway's
implementing measure for [[INTL-EEA-JCD-55-2010]] (30 April 2010,
entering into force 1 July 2011), supplemented by implementing
regulations, including the **geodataforskriften** (FOR-2012-08-08-797).

## Relationships

- `implements-requirement-from` [[EU-INSPIRE]] — the directive
  incorporated via [[INTL-EEA-JCD-55-2010]].

## Not modelled

- The **geodataforskriften** (Regulation on Infrastructure for
  Geographic Information, 2012) and earlier implementing regulation
  (FOR-2010-09-03-1247), named above only in prose.
- Whether [[NO-KARTVERKET]] administers this Act — plausible given its
  role as Norway's mapping authority, but not stated on any page read
  this pass.

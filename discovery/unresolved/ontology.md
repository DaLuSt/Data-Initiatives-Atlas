## Row 5 — No sub-national `level`

*Area: Ontology.*

**The single highest-value ontology finding across the whole project.** Confirmed lossy for Germany (16 Länder), Belgium (Regions/Communities — and `regional` was already taken by the supra-national meaning), and Spain (17 Comunidades Autónomas) — three different constitutional shapes, same failure. Belgium's instance was **resolved 2026-08-21**: `level: subnational` was added to the schema and used for [[BE-OSLO]], [[BE-DIGITAAL-VLAANDEREN]], [[BE-AGENCE-NUMERIQUE]], [[BE-PARADIGM]]. **Narrowed 2026-09-20**: Germany and Spain each now have sub-national bodies modelled — for Germany, [[DE-DATAPORT]] (a multi-Land IT public-law institution spanning six Länder, unlike Belgium's one-per-region pattern), [[DE-AKDB]] (Bavaria's municipal-association-owned IT provider) and [[DE-IT-NRW]] (North Rhine-Westphalia's own Landesbetrieb, formed 2009 from the state statistical office and regional computing centres); for Spain, [[ES-AOC]] (Catalonia's interoperability consortium), [[ES-EJIE]] (the Basque Government's wholly-owned IT company), [[ES-IZFE]] (Gipuzkoa's own provincial IT company) and [[ES-CCASA]] (Álava's own provincial IT company, founded 1969 — the oldest of the three). **Narrowed further 2026-09-20**: three of the Basque Country's four NISAE companies are now modelled (EJIE, IZFE, CCASA); the fourth, LANTIK (Bizkaia's provincial IT company), was attempted but blocked — see the new `bizkaia.eus` entry below. **Narrowed once more 2026-09-20**: two more German Länder now have their own IT provider modelled — [[DE-HZD]] (Hesse, established 1970, a Landesbetrieb since 1989 like [[DE-IT-NRW]]) and [[DE-BITBW]] (Baden-Württemberg, established 1 July 2015, succeeding the dissolved IZLBW). **Narrowed again 2026-09-20**: Madrid now has its own regional IT agency modelled, [[ES-MADRID-DIGITAL]] — a single continuous institution renamed six times since 1980 (SERPI→EPIMSA→ICM→Agencia→Madrid Digital), modelled once per the rename-not-succession convention already applied to [[DE-BUNDID]]. **Narrowed further still 2026-09-20**: two more Länder now have their own IT provider modelled — [[DE-LDI]] (Rhineland-Palatinate, established 2003) and [[DE-IT-NIEDERSACHSEN]] (Lower Saxony, established 2014, succeeding the dissolved LSKN). This surfaced a genuine open question rather than closing cleanly: Lower Saxony appears under **both** [[DE-DATAPORT]] (joined as a Träger in 2010) and its own [[DE-IT-NIEDERSACHSEN]] Landesbetrieb — no source read this pass states how the two divide responsibility, or whether IT.Niedersachsen's 2014 founding narrowed Dataport's role there. Not investigated further this pass. That is 6 of 16 Länder covered by a single-Land provider (Bavaria/AKDB, NRW/IT-NRW, Hesse/HZD, Baden-Württemberg/BITBW, Rhineland-Palatinate/LDI, Lower Saxony/IT.Niedersachsen) plus Dataport's separate multi-Land coverage of six more (one of which, Lower Saxony, overlaps), and 4 of 17 Comunidades (Catalonia, Basque Country, Madrid, plus two sub-Comunidad provincial bodies in Gipuzkoa and Álava) — a meaningful start, not full coverage; most sub-national units in both countries remain unresearched. **Narrowed 2026-09-25**: Andalusia now has its own digital agency modelled, [[ES-AGENCIA-DIGITAL-ANDALUCIA]], created by a single decree (Decreto 128/2021) rather than Madrid's decades of renaming — 5 of 17 Comunidades now covered. **Basque quartet completed 2026-09-25**: [[ES-LANTIK]] (Bizkaia) is now modelled too, closing the four-way NISAE set alongside [[ES-EJIE]], [[ES-CCASA]] and [[ES-IZFE]] — `bizkaia.eus` was retried (five URLs) and still returned HTTP 503 on every attempt, so it was sourced instead from an independent civic-tech site and a commercial company registry, at `confidence: medium` rather than its siblings' `high`.

## Row 11 — Codes vs. single statutes, and a few unranked instruments

*Area: Ontology.*

**Declined 2026-10-09 (owner's decision, roadmap #449)**: a code ([[IT-CAD]]) versus a single statute is a difference of form that `rank` does not capture, and one example does not justify a new field; the existing rule ranks a legislative decree `ordinary` and the entity's own body explains that it is a code. Revisit if a second code (a French or German one) is added. **Closed earlier**: the German, French-ordinance and Portuguese gaps (2026-10-02), Portugal's reinforced-value category and the UK row (2026-10-07: the Data Protection Act 2018 is a "UK Public General Act", "Primary Legislation" on legislation.gov.uk; the UK has no written constitution, so no further check exists). **Unset on purpose**: five bills until enacted ([[ES-LCGC]], [[GB-CSRB]], [[IE-NCS-BILL]], [[NL-ARCHIEFWET-2026]], [[FR-NIS2-LOI]]), two EU proposals ([[EU-DIGITAL-OMNIBUS]], [[EU-FIDA]]), the EEA Joint Committee Decisions (no state hierarchy), [[GB-UK-GDPR]] (its UK rank is a question of UK law its file does not answer) and the seven agreements.

## Row 12 — Relationship vocabulary for "how data actually moves"

*Area: Ontology.*

**Narrowed 2026-09-20**: the authorised-use ("afnemer") and key-sharing identifier-coupling gaps are now closed — two new types, `uses-data-from` and `carries-identifier-of` (metadata/relationship-types.md §2.1), applied to all four named sourced connections ([[NL-BELASTINGDIENST]]→[[NL-WOZ]], [[NL-RDW]]→[[NL-BRP]], [[NL-BRK]]→[[NL-NHR]], [[NL-BRP]]→[[NL-BAG]]) plus the UN batch's two (already closed earlier via `cooperates-with` and the `publication` entity type). ## Row 231 — `level: local`

*Area: Ontology.*

**No use found; kept in the vocabulary, unused.** Recounted 2026-10-03: 0 uses against 528 `national`, 102 `regional`, 85 `international` and 23 `subnational` (`sectoral` was retired the same day, `metadata/ontology.md` §4). `local` once seemed to block Flanders, the Comunidades Autónomas and the German Länder, because `regional` means *supra*-national here; `level: subnational` (added 2026-08-21) cleared that. What `local` could still gate is the Dutch municipalities, and that was **declined 2026-09-05**: there are hundreds of them, so no single "the municipalities" entity is right, and [[NL-VNG]] is their association, a different thing. Moved here from the candidates page on 2026-10-03.

*Area: Ontology.*

**Deliberately not given a new type, 2026-09-05.** [[LI-DSG]]'s "modelled on the German BDSG" comes from a law-firm commentary describing legislative style, not a sourced statement that the legislature adapted a specific text: the kind of single, weakly sourced instance a new type should not be built around.

# Nordic-Baltic Co-operation Programme for Digitalisation 2025-2030

> **Created 2026-10-17** (roadmap #541, Northern Europe). The publication was read in full (PolitikNord 2024:754, published October 2024; DOI 10.6027/politiknord2024-754). `start_date` is its first day of validity; the end (31 December 2030) is in the future and not recorded as `end_date`. It is a *programme*, so typed `strategy`. The links to the eight country anchors are low-confidence interpretation.

## What it says

- **Priorities:** a public sector that meets the needs of people and businesses; harmonised implementation of EU regulation; regional mobility and connectivity; digital skills and responsible use; the twin digital and green transition; "cooperate on agreed European standards, infrastructure, data sharing, data spaces and interoperability, as well as connectivity" in line with EU initiatives.
- **Goal 1** (front runner in the digital and green transition): subgoal 1.1, competences and research on how digitalisation, data use and data sharing speed the green transition (digital twin pilots on "harmonized, interoperable, and shared data", FAIR principles); 1.2, greening ICT.
- **Goal 2** (secure, connected, integrated): 2.1 a common area for cross-border digital services (interoperable digital identities, frameworks for sharing data, key services for mobility); 2.2 secure, efficient, inclusive public services; 2.3 coordination of digital infrastructure efforts and a regional voice at EU and international level; **2.4 enhanced sharing and re-use of data across borders**, by common understanding, shared knowledge and aligning on key frameworks, linking national regulatory, technical and semantic considerations.
- **Goal 3** (secure, inclusive, human-centric): 3.1 digital divide and skills; 3.2 cyber- and information security; 3.3 trustworthy, secure, responsible and sustainable use of technology, including AI.
- **Evaluation:** two three-year work plans (2025-2027 and 2028-2030) with a mid-point evaluation.

# CARICOM Single ICT Space: Vision and Roadmap

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #547). From the Caribbean Telecommunications Union's page and a CARICOM news item, read directly. **The Vision and Roadmap document was not read**; `start_date` is empty because only months are given (March 2014 for the mandate, February 2017 for the roadmap).

## What is sourced

Vision: "an ICT-enabled borderless space that fosters economic, social and cultural integration for the betterment of Caribbean citizens". The Space "will be characterised by": regionally harmonised ICT policy, legal and regulatory regimes; robust national and regional broadband infrastructure; common frameworks for governments, ICT service providers and consumers; effective, secure technology and management systems. The ICT Cluster of Institutions that worked on it: the CTU, the CARICOM Secretariat, CBU, CARICOM IMPACS and CARICAD. On 11 February 2022 ministers of ICT approved Secretariat-led action plans to fast-track digital transformation (a draft action plan, a Girls in ICT Partnership Action Plan and a Digital Skills Task Force action plan), discussed a single regional telecommunications regulator that Heads were to consider in July 2022 and set 2022 priority initiatives for the Space; what Heads decided in July 2022 was not found.

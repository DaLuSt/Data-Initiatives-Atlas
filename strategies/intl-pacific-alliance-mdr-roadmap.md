# Pacific Alliance Roadmap for the Regional Digital Market

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #545). From a one-page summary (a PDF uploaded to the Alliance's site in July 2021), read directly, in Spanish. **The roadmap itself was not read**; the summary gives no date for it, only the presidential mandate of 11 December 2020, so `start_date` is empty.

## What the summary says

The Presidents, at the XV Presidential Summit in Santiago, Chile, on 11 December 2020, signed "una Declaración Presidencial sobre el desarrollo del Mercado Digital Regional y el impulso hacia la transformación digital de la región". The roadmap "tiene por objetivo identificar las acciones que permitirán avanzar hacia una transformación digital de la región, en particular, a favor del comercio digital y electrónico". Pillar 2's actions include: promote the use and interoperability of the electronic signature; online consumer protection; "asegurar la privacidad, la protección de datos personales y el libre flujo de datos"; cybersecurity; interoperability of the foreign trade single window; net neutrality; the role of intellectual property in the digital economy. Pillar 1: high-speed networks, roaming elimination, spectrum harmonisation, IXPs, IPv6. Pillar 3: entrepreneurship, e-commerce, emerging technologies, digital talent.

## Description

The European Commission published the Communication *A European strategy for
data* on 19 February 2020. Its aim is to create a single European data space
so that businesses and public authorities can more easily access
high-quality data, boosting growth and creating value.

Three core objectives are reported: promoting free flow of data, respecting
European rules and values, and establishing fair and practical rules for
data access and use.

Its four pillars:

1. A cross-sectoral governance framework for data access and use.
2. Investment in data, and strengthening Europe's capabilities and
   infrastructures for hosting, processing and using data, plus
   interoperability.
3. Empowerment of individuals, investing in skills and in SMEs.
4. **Common European data spaces** in strategic sectors and domains of
   public interest — see [[EU-COMMON-DATA-SPACES]].

## Position in the graph

This strategy is the policy root of much of the EU data layer. The
cross-sectoral governance framework in pillar 1 is what the Data Governance
Act and Data Act give legal effect to; neither is yet an Atlas entity (Batch

well known. That is the same discipline applied throughout: widely

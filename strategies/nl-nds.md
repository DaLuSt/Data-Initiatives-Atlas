# Nederlandse Digitaliseringsstrategie (NDS)

> **Sourcing caveat.** This entity was compiled from search-engine results
> only; the cited pages were confirmed to exist but were not read. See
> `discovery/unresolved.md` and `progress/current-batch.md`.

## Description

The NDS is a joint digitalisation strategy of municipalities, provinces,
water authorities, public service providers and central government,
published on 4 July 2025 under the motto *"Samen versnellen is de enige
optie"*. Its stated purpose is to connect existing digitalisation plans and
set priorities as one government.

Reported priorities include: acting as one government within a "digital
house of Thorbecke" with joint governance; investment in digital
fundamentals such as data, cloud, AI and cybersecurity; mandatory
implementation of standards and shared solutions across government;
data-driven working via a federative data system; and strategic autonomy
pursued in cooperation with the EU, industry and knowledge institutions.

`status: active` with `confidence: low`. The strategy was formally published
and has not been withdrawn, but search results indicate the NDS was an
initiative of the previous cabinet and that a subsequent cabinet was
developing its own digital policy plans, with the form of continuation — and
the division of responsibility between ministries — not settled. That
uncertainty is genuine and is recorded in `discovery/unresolved.md` rather
than resolved by assumption.

## Relationship to earlier agendas

The NDS is the leading candidate successor to [[NL-DIGIBETER]], whose most
recent actualisation found in research dates from 2020. **No `supersedes`
relationship is asserted**, because "a later strategy exists and connects
existing plans" is not the same as "this strategy formally replaced that
one". Establishing or ruling out that link is an open question in
`discovery/unresolved.md`.

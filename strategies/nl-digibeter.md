## Description

NL DIGIbeter is the Dutch government's agenda for the digital government,
launched in July 2018 with 71 actions of varying type and size. It was
conceived as a 'rolling agenda', updated annually; actualisations were
published for 2019 and 2020, each laid before parliament as an annex to
Kamerstuk 26643.

The agenda emphasises the government's exemplary role in handling data
effectively and responsibly, and foregrounds public values — privacy,
self-determination, equality — within the digital transition. [[NL-BZK]]
holds the coordinating role, from its responsibility for the digital
government and for safeguarding fundamental rights.

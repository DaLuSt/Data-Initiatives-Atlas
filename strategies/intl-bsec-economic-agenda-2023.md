# BSEC Economic Agenda Towards a Sustainable Future of the wider Black Sea area

> **Created 2026-10-19** (roadmap #543). Known **only** from two of the Organization's working group pages. **The Agenda's text was not read**, so nothing beyond what those pages say about Goals 12 and 13 is recorded.

## What is sourced

The ICT page: the key priorities in ICT "are defined by Goal 12 'Sustainable information society with advanced Information and Communication Technology infrastructure'" of this Agenda, adopted on 15 December 2023; since then BSEC actions have focused on helping members build ICT infrastructure, "a safer information communication network" and the exchange of experience. The statistics page calls the framework "BSEC Economic Agenda Towards and Enhanced BSEC Partnership", updated in 2023, and names Goal 13 "Harmonization and Exchange of Economic and Social Statistical Data and Information". The two pages name the document differently; whether these are one document is not established.

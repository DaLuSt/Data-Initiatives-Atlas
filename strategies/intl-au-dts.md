# Digital Transformation Strategy for Africa (2020-2030)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #548 and the other African sub-regions). Known **only** from a footnote and the opening of the AU Data Policy Framework. **The strategy was not read**; dates are empty because the endorsement session ran over two days.

## What is sourced

Endorsed by the Executive Council at its 36th Ordinary Session (6-7 February 2020) "as the master plan that will guide" the continent's digital economy and society; "critical to the achievement of the African Union Agenda 2063". The Executive Council asked the AU Commission to lead a continental data policy framework for the 4th Specialized Technical Committee on Communication and ICT in 2021: [[INTL-AU-DPF]].

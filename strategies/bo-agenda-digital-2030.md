# Agenda Digital 2030 (Bolivia)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #474 group, Bolivia). The approving decree (DS 5367, signed by President Luis Arce Catacora on 2 April 2025) was read in full; its annex, the Agenda itself, was **not** read, so no goal or measure of the Agenda is described. Whether later decrees changed it was not checked (AGETIC's home page shows a newer Decreto Supremo 5519, unread).

## What the decree says

Art. 1 sets the aim quoted above; art. 2 approves the Agenda, which forms an annex; art. 3 applies it to the Executive Organ and public companies; the additional provision gives [[BO-AGETIC]] its monitoring and evaluation; the final provision says no additional Treasury resources are needed.

## Relationships

Applies in [[BO]]; related to [[BO-AGETIC]], which follows it up.

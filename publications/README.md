# publications/

Entities of `type: publication` — reports or studies with independent
significance. Per README §25, publications should generally *support* other
entities via `sources:`/`references` rather than become entities themselves;
only add one here when it has standing of its own (e.g. a foundational
report that other initiatives explicitly build on).

Filename: `<id-lowercased>.md`. Template: `templates/entity-template.md`.

## The three that qualified

This folder was empty until 2026-08-21. The README's own bar — *"only add
one here when it has standing of its own"* — is why, and it is why these
three cleared it:

- [[EU-DESI]] and [[EU-EGOV-BENCHMARK]] are not evidence for other entities;
  they are the instruments by which the Commission measures member states,
  cited *by* policy rather than citing it. They give the Atlas its first
  comparative-measurement layer.
- [[EU-VOLUNTARY-REVIEW-2023]] is the Union's own report on its
  implementation of [[UN-2030-AGENDA]], presented at the UN High-level
  Political Forum. It exists here because the alternative — an edge between
  the Commission and something UN-shaped — could not be stated honestly. See
  `metadata/relationship-types.md` §2.4.

**Oversight reports remain out.** CTIVD's and [[GB-ISC]]'s annual reports and
the *Verfassungsschutzbericht* were considered and left as citations on the
bodies that publish them, which is what §25 asks for.

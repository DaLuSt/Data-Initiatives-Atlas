# Draft Model Law on Data Protection and Privacy (EGEE-ICT, 2025)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #550 and the other East and Southern African items). The 141 000-character PDF on `comesa.int`, read in part: the title page, disclaimer, contents, the preliminary provisions on legal authority and Article 4.1. It is a **draft by a consultancy** whose disclaimer says it "is intended for use by the COMESA Secretariat under the EGEE-ICT Programme and any parties the Secretariat may expressly authorize". No adoption by any Council of Ministers was found, hence `status: proposed`. Filed as `publication` because the Atlas has no type for a draft model law; it is not an instrument of any REC yet.

## What it says

Eight chapters and eight schedules. Legal authority is argued from each REC's founding instrument (EAC Treaty Articles 8, 14 and 44; COMESA Treaty Articles 4, 6, 10, 139 and 164; SADC Treaty Article 5 and trade protocols; IGAD Agreement Article 7; the IOC's objectives). Article 4.1: personal data may leave a Member State only where the recipient ensures comparable protection; adequacy may be decided by a national Data Protection Authority or by the RECs through their Secretariats and an established Data Protection Forum; without adequacy, standard contractual clauses "adopted or approved by the REC Secretariat" or binding corporate rules. Chapter 4 also covers data sovereignty and localisation and regional coordination; Chapter 5 an independent Authority and cooperation with other authorities and RECs; Schedules include recognised adequate jurisdictions, contractual clause and impact assessment templates and a regional dispute resolution mechanism.

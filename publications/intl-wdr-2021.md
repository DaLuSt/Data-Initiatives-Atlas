# World Development Report 2021: Data for Better Lives

> **Created 2026-11-01** (roadmap #511). From the Bank's page for the report (its main messages), read directly; **the report itself was not read**. The year is in the title; the publication date is not on the page, so none is recorded. The page's banner shows the following year's report (WDR 2022, on finance); the text read is the 2021 report's.

## Main messages (as the page states them)

"Today's unprecedented growth of data and their ubiquity in our lives are signs that the data revolution is transforming the world. And yet much of the value of data remains untapped ... many barriers stand in the way, ranging from misaligned incentives and incompatible data systems to a fundamental lack of trust." The report "calls for a new social contract that enables the use and reuse of data to create economic and social value, ensures equitable access to that value, and fosters trust that data will not be misused". "Renewed efforts are required to improve data governance domestically, as well as through closer international cooperation." "Major inequities in the ability to produce, utilize, and profit from data can be found across both rich and poor countries."

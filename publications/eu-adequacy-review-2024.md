# Report on the first review of the adequacy decisions adopted under Directive 95/46/EC (COM(2024) 7)

> **Created 2026-11-07** (data-sharing links beyond Europe). Read on EUR-Lex through a fetch tool that returns a model's summary; the country-by-country conclusions are the tool's reading of the report's section 4 and were not checked against the full text.

## What it does

It reviews the eleven adequacy decisions that predate the GDPR and finds that all eleven countries and territories continue to ensure an adequate level of protection. This is the review that keeps them in force under Article 45(9) GDPR, which [[EU-CH-ADEQUACY]] already describes for Switzerland.

## Relationships

`measures` the five decisions the Atlas holds for [[EU-AD-ADEQUACY]], [[EU-AR-ADEQUACY]], [[EU-CA-ADEQUACY]], [[EU-NZ-ADEQUACY]] and [[EU-UY-ADEQUACY]], and [[EU-CH-ADEQUACY]]. The decisions for the Faroe Islands, Guernsey, the Isle of Man, Jersey and Israel are not modelled (row #282).

# UfM Ministerial Declaration on the Digital Economy (2014)

> **Created 2026-10-14** (roadmap #542). **Read: a draft.** The Secretariat's PDF is headed "Draft Declaration [Version 5a – 18.09.14]", twelve days before the meeting. The adopted wording was not found; what is known to have been adopted comes from the Secretariat's press release of 30 September 2014 and from the working group's Rules of Procedure, both read directly. Where the two differ, the press release is followed.

## What was adopted

Digital economy ministers and representatives of ministries, the Secretariat, telecoms regulators, development banks and EU institutions met in Brussels on 30 September 2014, co-chaired by the EU and Jordan. The press release says they committed to: build closer ties on open data and e-government, "leaning on the principles already developed in the 2013 G8 Open Data Charter such as open data by default"; boost e-infrastructure connectivity between research communities; set up a regular dialogue between independent telecoms regulators and ICT ministries; promote cross-border business through electronic transactions and e-signatures; work on a long-term e-health cooperation strategy; and support global Internet-governance discussions. The Declaration "calls for the set-up of a 'Digital Economy and Internet Access Working Group'", which became [[INTL-UFM-DEWOG]].

## What the draft adds

The draft names the EU's eIDAS Regulation ([[EU-EIDAS]]) as "the common reference" for an EU and Southern Mediterranean interoperability framework on trust services, refers to the "Charter on Open Data (adopted by the G8 on June 18, 2013)", the EU's Re-use of Public Sector Information Directive, data protection in e-health, and the Euromed Regulator Group (EMERG, founded in Malta in July 2008). Whether these passages survived in the adopted text is unknown.

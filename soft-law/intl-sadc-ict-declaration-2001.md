# SADC Declaration on Information and Communication Technologies (2001)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #552). Known **only** from SADC's own ICT page. **The Declaration was not read**; the year is the only date given, and whether it is still in force or was replaced was not found.

## What the page says

It "established a coherent policy on information and communication technologies for SADC's Member States as they endeavour to bridge the digital divide", advocates "an improved regulatory environment" and community participation, "highlights the importance of technological infrastructure" and urges Member States to give special note to rural and remote areas, underprivileged urban areas and institutions of learning.

# AU Data Policy Framework (2022)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #548 and the other African sub-regions). The framework's PDF (about 260 000 characters of text) was read in part (foreword, executive summary, mandate, principles and the implementation section headings); the enablers and governance chapters were not read in full. `start_date` is empty: the endorsement session ran over two days (2-3 February 2022) and the document page is dated 28 July 2022.

## What is sourced

Endorsed by the Executive Council "during its 40th Ordinary Session held on 2 – 3 February 2022 through Decision with reference EX.CL/ Dec.1144(XL)". "This framework sets out a common vision, principles, strategic priorities and key recommendations to guide African countries in developing their national data systems and capabilities." Principles: trust, accessibility, interoperability, security, quality and integrity, representivity and non-discrimination, within broader principles of transparency, accountability, inclusion, equity and fair competition. Recommendations include developing national personal data protection legislation, establishing or maintaining independent, well-resourced Data Protection Authorities, and ratifying the Malabo Convention. It derives from the Digital Transformation Strategy for Africa (2020-2030) and builds on the AfCFTA agreement, PRIDA, PIDA, Smart Africa, the Malabo Convention and others. It is not a treaty; whether Member States have implemented it was not found.

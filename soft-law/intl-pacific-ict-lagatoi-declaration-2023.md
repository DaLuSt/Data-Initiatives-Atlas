# Pacific ICT Ministerial Declaration (Lagatoi), 2023

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #558). Known from the Forum's Technology and Connectivity page and the file name of the PDF on the website of Papua New Guinea's ICT department ("Pacific ICT Ministerial Declaration. Monday 28 August 2023. APEC Haus"). **The PDF is a 1.6 MB scan with no text layer, so the declaration was not read** (it needs OCR). The name "Lagatoi" comes from the Forum's link text; the full title "Lagatoi Declaration on Digital Transformation of the Pacific" comes from a search result and is not recorded as read. `start_date` is the date in the file name.

## What is sourced

The six priority areas, from the Forum's page: Digital Transformation; Digital Innovation and Entrepreneurship; Digital Infrastructure; Digital Security and Trust; Digital Capacity Building and Skills Development; Regional Cooperation and Representation. Who signed it and whether it binds anyone were not read.

# Global Cross-Border Privacy Rules (CBPR) Declaration (2022)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #556). The Declaration's text, read directly. Whether it binds anyone was not stated; a declaration of intent establishing a forum, so `soft-law`.

## What it says

Signatory economies: "Canada, Japan, the Republic of Korea, the Philippines, Singapore, Chinese Taipei, and the United States of America, as current economies participating in the APEC CBPR System". It declares (1) the establishment of a Global CBPR Forum "to promote interoperability and help bridge different regulatory approaches to data protection and privacy" and (2) its objectives: an international certification system based on the APEC CBPR and PRP systems; support for "the free flow of data and effective data protection and privacy"; and a forum for information exchange and cooperation. Recitals: trusted cross-border data flows matter to all sectors including small firms, and regulatory barriers threaten the digital economy.

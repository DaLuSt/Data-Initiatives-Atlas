# GCC Guiding Framework for the Protection of Personal Information of GCC Citizens (2023)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #553). Known **only** from the headline and first paragraphs of the GCC General Secretariat's news item of 5 February 2023. **The framework's text was not read.** Whether it binds the member states, what it requires and whether it has been revised were not found; its name ("guiding") suggests a non-binding instrument, so `soft-law`.

## What is sourced

The Committee "Adopt[ed] the Guiding Framework for the Protection of Personal Information of GCC Citizens" at its 27th meeting on 5 February 2023 in Riyadh. A search result also reports that Qatar submitted a draft proposal for the framework and that a GCC workshop on it followed (secondary, not recorded).

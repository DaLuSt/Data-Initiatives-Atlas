# APEC Privacy Framework (2015)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #556). The 31-page PDF on apec.org, read in part (the foreword, preamble, principles' headings and the CBPR box). `start_date` is empty: no date of endorsement appears in the text; the 2015 update's endorsement date and the 2005 original's were not read.

## What it says

"Information flows are vital to conducting business in a global economy. The APEC Privacy Framework promotes a flexible approach to information privacy protection across APEC member economies, while avoiding the creation of unnecessary barriers to information flows." Part III, the APEC Information Privacy Principles: I Preventing Harm, II Notice, III Collection Limitation, IV Uses of Personal Information, V Choice, VI Integrity of Personal Information, VII Security Safeguards, VIII Access and Correction, IX Accountability. Part IV covers domestic implementation (including remedies, technical measures, education, cooperation) and international implementation (information sharing, cross-border cooperation in enforcement, cross-border privacy mechanisms, interoperability between privacy frameworks). The APEC Cross Border Privacy Rules system, "endorsed by APEC Leaders in 2011, is a voluntary accountability-based scheme", with accountability agents, certification of information controllers, assessment criteria and enforcement through complaints processes; it is described in the Framework and run at `cbprs.org` (not read).

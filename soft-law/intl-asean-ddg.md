# ASEAN Framework on Digital Data Governance

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #557). The 9-page text on `asean.org`, read in part (background, objectives, priorities, the cross-border principle and the implementation paragraphs). It ends "ADOPTED AT []" with no date, so `start_date` is empty; the adoption date and the status of the initiatives (the Model Contractual Clauses and the certification work that followed are not in this text) were not found.

## What it says

"This Framework sets out the strategic priorities, principles and initiatives to guide ASEAN Member States in their policy and regulatory approaches towards digital data governance (which include both personal and non-personal data) in the digital economy" (paragraph 4). The Master Plan on ASEAN Connectivity 2025 had named the Framework as an initiative (Initiative 7). The principle on cross border data flows "is intended to maximise the free flow of data within ASEAN" while keeping safeguards, and Member States are to "review and minimise restrictions" (paragraphs 19-20). The data classification framework "is not meant to be an exhaustive or binding" scheme (paragraph 16). Amendment is by mutual agreement of all Member States (37). The annual ASEAN Data Protection Forum is mentioned as the place for milestone updates (36).

# HIPCAR Privacy and Data Protection model policy guidelines and legislative texts

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #547, the Caribbean's regional instruments). The ITU's PDF (about 300 000 characters), read in part: the foreword, introduction, the structure of the model text and the provenance. `start_date` is empty: the workshops were in March and July 2010 and the ITU printed it in 2013, with no adoption or endorsement date by a regional body.

## What it says

Nine parts of model legislation: preliminary; obligations of the data controllers; rights of the data subject; particular obligations of public authorities; special exemptions; review and appeals; an **Office of the Data Commissioner** (Part VII); contravention and enforcement; miscellaneous. The introduction says it was developed in three phases (an assessment report, model policy guidelines, a model legislative text) by consultants Karen Stephen-Dalton and Kwesi Prescod under a HIPCAR Steering Committee chaired by the Caribbean Telecommunications Union, and that it "contains data and information as known in July 2010". Stage II of the project offered technical assistance to countries that wanted to transpose the models.

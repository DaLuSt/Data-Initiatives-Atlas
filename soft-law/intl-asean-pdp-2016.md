# ASEAN Framework on Personal Data Protection (2016)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #557, South-eastern Asia's regional bodies). The six-page text on `asean.org`, read directly in full.

## What it says

- **Effect (paragraph 2):** "This Framework serves only as a record of the Participants' intentions and does not constitute or create ... obligations under domestic or international law" — hence `soft-law` and no `applies-in` edges.
- **Principles (paragraph 6):** consent, notification and purpose; accuracy; security safeguards; access and correction; transfers to another country or territory (consent, or reasonable steps to ensure consistent protection); retention; accountability.
- **Implementation:** a Participant may delay application until ready (7); joint activities such as information sharing, workshops and joint research (8); each designates its data protection or privacy authority to coordinate (14); a Participant may withdraw on six months' notice (16).
- **Origin:** recalls the AEC Blueprint 2025 (27th ASEAN Summit, 22 November 2015) and the ASEAN ICT Masterplan 2020 (15th TELMIN, 27 November 2015), and has regard to the APEC Privacy Framework (2015).

Only Malaysia, the Philippines and Singapore of the ten participants have a country anchor; the other seven have none, so they have no edge.

# Benelux Summit – Joint Declaration 2016 (Digital Benelux)

> **Verified 2026-10-10.** The declaration's full English text, on the Benelux Union's own page, was read directly on a GitHub-hosted runner.

## Description

The declaration lists measures to "explore" under a Digital Benelux; among those touching data: "through pilot-projects, explore ways and means to expand the digitization of administrative operations and the optimization of data exchange" for paperless international trade formalities; "facilitate through pilot projects the use of digital freight documents and other paperless freight documents"; "facilitate the interoperability of eHealth services and strengthen patients' rights in cross-border healthcare"; and, on cybersecurity, "exploring the subject of responsible disclosure of technical vulnerabilities while taking care not to increase the administrative burden". It also mentions the Benelux Business Scams Fraud SharePoint and the Online Energy Platform as information platforms. Some of the measures were to enter the Benelux Annual Working Plan 2017.

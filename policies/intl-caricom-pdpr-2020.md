# CARICOM Secretariat Privacy and Data Protection Rules (2020)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #547). The Rules' text, read directly (a PDF of 59 pages of text). Filed as `policy` because the Atlas has no type for an international organisation's internal rules; they are binding on its staff, so "non-binding" in the type's definition does not fit and a better type is a vocabulary question (roadmap #457).

## What it says

Rule 1.1: "These Rules shall come into effect on the 28th day of October 2020." Rule 1.2: they apply to personal information supplied to the Secretariat "in relation to procurement of goods, works, services and consultancies ..., selection and employment of consultants, recruitment, selection, and employment of staff ..., disposal of assets, volunteer work and or internships". Parts cover the controller's obligations (limited collection and processing, collection directly from the individual, informing the individual of the purpose, retention, accuracy, protection), storage, use and disposal, disclosure (including for research, statistics and archives), the data subject's right of access (and its refusal and delegation) and processing by third parties under contract. The Foreword grounds privacy in the Universal Declaration, the ICCPR and the American and European Conventions on Human Rights. Who adopted the Rules and by what act is not stated on the pages read.

# APPI triennial review reform policy (Japan, 2026)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #482, Japan). The Commission's news page and the one-page English outline (a provisional translation) were read; the full policy (Japanese, 736 KB) was not. `start_date` is the date of the decision. **Whether the amendment bill was submitted to the Diet, passed or enacted was not found** (no later page was read), so this stays a policy; when an amending act exists it gets its own entity with an `amends` edge to [[JP-APPI]].

## What the outline says

- **Data utilisation.** No consent needed where data are used only to generate statistics (including AI development, as far as it is statistics) for third-party provision and the acquisition of published sensitive information; exceptions without consent where it is evident the handling does not go against the data subject's intent, and a relaxed test ("reasonable grounds for not obtaining consent") for protecting life or improving public health; medical institutions count as "academic institutions".
- **Risks.** For children under 16, consent and notices go to the legal representative and the best interests of minors take priority; facial feature data: duty to inform, easier cessation, and opt-out third-party provision prohibited; review of obligations on entrusted processors; relaxed duty to notify the data subject of a breach where the risk is low.
- **Misuse.** Information that is not personal information but can target a person: improper use and acquisition prohibited; opt-out recipients' identity and purpose verified.
- **Enforcement.** Faster, broader orders; a basis to ask third parties involved in a violation to stop it; higher criminal penalties and new ones for fraudulent acquisition; **administrative monetary penalties equal to the gain** from large-scale, economically motivated violations.
- **Timing.** A bill "as early as spring 2026" and, if approved, in effect "approximately two years later".

# PIFS Personal Information Protection Policy (P014)

> **Created 2026-10-23** (roadmap #558). The five-page PDF on `forumsec.org`, read in full. The policy's header reads "P014", "2021", "Version 1.1"; the file name says "Rev 2022 Approved"; the signature block says "Approved: 28 July 2023" and "Effective: 22 March 2023" (the effective date precedes the approval date as printed; both are recorded here as read). `start_date` is the effective date.

## What it says

Principles (2.1): fair and legitimate processing (consent, the information subject's best interest or another legal basis), purpose specification, proportionality and necessity, retention, accuracy, security, access, transfers ("the Secretariat satisfies itself that the third party affords appropriate protection") and accountability. Examples of personal information: name, address, photograph, employment history, passport details, health information, geo-location. It uses Google Analytics for website usage "without storing any personal information". Staff and non-staff personnel sign an Oath to Confidentiality; access and correction requests go to the Director Operations by email with the subject "Subject Access Request"; complaints likewise. The Secretary General may waive provisions at their discretion (9.1, 9.2).

# Open data policy (Panama)

> **Created 2026-10-16** (roadmap #479, Panama). Read on ANTAI's policy page; the decree and resolution (downloadable from that page) were not opened. `start_date` is the decree's date.

## What the page says

Open data are the "Información de acceso libre" defined by Law 33 of 2013: information held by state agents without restriction. Opening data does not cover personal data, national security data, copyrighted data or data under specific restrictions. Examples named: the public budget and its execution, statistics, census data, public procurement, service performance, transport, justice and police statistics, environment, energy and trade. The Information Officer evaluates each unit's data, coordinates publication on the national portal, and leads the institution's team.

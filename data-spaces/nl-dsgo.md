## Description

The DSGO is an agreement framework developed to organise data sharing within
the built environment — construction, installation and engineering. It
offers a set of uniform agreements ensuring safe, reliable and controlled
access to data in the design, construction and engineering sector, and
describes how organisations can exchange data with one another safely,
reliably and unambiguously, implementing data services in a standardised
way.

It is aimed at parties in the built environment that exchange data in
digital chain collaboration, and is an initiative of digiGO, a collaboration
between industry organisations, companies and governments.

Version 1.0 of the agreement framework was launched on 18 June and formally
transferred to the market; the programme ran from 2021 and ended in June
2024. `start_date: 2024-06-18` records the v1.0 launch, on the reading that
the two statements refer to the same June 2024 moment — **that inference is
not sourced**, and the year on the launch date was not stated explicitly
alongside the day. Flagged in `discovery/unresolved.md`.

Note the pattern: the programme that built the DSGO has **ended**, while the
agreement framework it produced is live and in market hands. `status:
active` describes the framework, not the programme. That distinction is
worth preserving if a separate programme entity is ever created.

# Common European language data space

> **Sourcing caveat.** Compiled from search-engine results only; the cited

> search-only`.

## Description

One of the fourteen common European data spaces, through which stakeholders from the publishing, language technology and press industries will be able to exchange and monetise their language data and other language resources.

## The one that names monetisation in its purpose

Most of the fourteen are described in terms of availability, interoperability
and sovereignty. The Language Data Space is described by its own sources as
letting stakeholders **exchange and monetise** their language data and
language resources.

That is worth recording because it is a different theory of what a data space
is for. [[EU-DGA]] and [[EU-DATA-ACT]] both contemplate paid data sharing,
and this is the sectoral data space whose framing puts it first.

## Its participants are industries, not public bodies

Publishing, language technology and the press. Contrast [[EU-EHDS]]
(health systems), [[EU-CEEDS]] (energy operators) and
[[EU-PUBLIC-ADMIN-DATA-SPACE]] (public administrations). The fourteen are not
uniform in who is expected to be in them, and the Atlas can now show that.

## Not modelled

- The relationship to **EU AI** policy, where language resources are training

  states.

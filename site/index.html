          One instrument, many countries

          Every supra-national instrument the Atlas records as applying in, or being implemented by, at least one country. Generated from applies-in and implements-requirement-from edges — nothing here is hand-maintained. Rows respect the sidebar filters; columns respect the country filter only.

          • Implemented — a national entity carries implements-requirement-from
          • Applies — applies-in is recorded, but no national instrument is modelled
          • Nothing recorded — neither edge exists
          Supra-national instruments by country. Each cell states whether the country implements the instrument, is recorded as covered by it, or has nothing recorded.
